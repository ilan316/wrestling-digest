🔥 The Kasper Brothers (FKA The Creeds) Make Surprise AEW Debut at Full Gear

At AEW Full Gear 2026 on Saturday night, November 14, in Phoenix, Arizona, a major surprise shook up the tag team division. Jacob and Drew Kasper, formerly known in WWE as Julius Creed and Brutus Creed of The Creed Brothers, made their shocking All Elite Wrestling debut. The arrival came just one week after news surfaced on November 6 that the former champions had parted ways with WWE when their contracts expired without renewal.

The debut took place immediately after The Young Bucks successfully defended the AEW World Tag Team Championships against Mark Davis and Jake Doyle of The Don Callis Family. Following the match, the number one contenders, the Motor City Machine Guns consisting of Alex Shelley and Chris Sabin, entered the ring. Rather than attacking the champions immediately, Shelley and Sabin helped Matt and Nick Jackson to their feet and offered handshakes, acknowledging their status as contenders. That is when a siren sounded and unfamiliar entrance music played, introducing the Kasper Brothers to the arena.

The newcomers immediately stormed the ring and laid waste to both The Young Bucks and the Motor City Machine Guns, leaving members of both teams laid out. Moments later, FTR’s Dax Harwood and Cash Wheeler rushed to the ring. While initially teasing a confrontation, FTR instead joined forces with the Kasper Brothers, helping to decimate both babyface teams. Stokely Hathaway, also known as Big Stoke, appeared to celebrate with the group, happily reuniting with the brothers from their NXT days together in the Diamond Mine faction. Despite a brief moment of tension where both FTR and the Kaspers grabbed the tag team titles, Hathaway stepped in to unite them, and the segment concluded with the two teams shaking hands and leaving together.

Following the surprise appearance, AEW President Tony Khan took to social media to officially confirm the signings. Khan wrote on social media that they have arrived at AEW Full Gear and attacked the AEW World Tag Team Champions and number one contenders, officially stating that Jacob and Drew Kasper are all elite.

The brothers finished a notable tenure with WWE where they captured the NXT Tag Team Championships, won the 2022 Men’s Dusty Rhodes Tag Team Classic, and later appeared on the main roster as part of American Made alongside Chad Gable. Their final appearance in WWE occurred when they competed in the Dusty Rhodes Tag Team Classic, losing to Hank Walker and Tank Ledger in the quarterfinals before disappearing from television and ultimately leaving the company. Now operating under their real names in AEW, Jacob and Drew Kasper have immediately put the entire tag team division on notice by targeting the very top of the company on their very first night.

🔥 Swerve Strickland & New Level Win AEW Trios Titles in Texas Death Match at Full Gear

New AEW World Trios Champions were crowned at Full Gear 2026 on Saturday night, November 14, in Phoenix, Arizona, at the Mortgage Matchup Center. Swerve Strickland and The New Level, consisting of Austin Creed and Kofi, defeated Hangman Adam Page and Brodido, featuring Brody King and Bandido, in a brutal Texas Death Match to capture the AEW World Trios Championships.

The match followed a series of championship exchanges throughout 2026. Strickland, Kofi, and Austin Creed initially defeated Page, Brody King, and Bandido at All In in August, only for the champions to regain the titles at WrestleDream in October. Strickland demanded Texas Death rules after losing the championships, hoping to repeat his victory over Page at Full Gear 2023. Page initially rejected the idea to protect his teammates from his death match history against Strickland, but Strickland attacked Bandido, bloodying him and taking his mask. Bandido pushed for the stipulation, and the champions ultimately accepted.

The chaotic contest featured an assortment of weapons, including barbed wire tables, thumbtacks, steel chairs, staple guns, chains, padlocks, light tubes, a cinder block, and even a barbed wire-wrapped trombone named Francesca. The New Level took their first death match spot in AEW shortly after the bout began, when the champions stuck wooden skewers into their opponents foreheads. The New Level immediately panicked as Prince Nana helped pull the skewers out, and Brody King also got drenched in his own blood, almost sporting an entire crimson bodysuit.

Among the highlights, Austin Creed pulled out a trombone wrapped in barbed wire and used it to attack Brody King. Hangman Page and Swerve Strickland soon brought out staple guns and dispensed several holes in each other and opponents bodies. Creed also utilized a Nintendo Power Glove to unleash an explosive Shoryuken or Dragon Punch uppercut right out of Street Fighter, causing an explosion right in King's face. King later sent himself through a barbed wire table in the corner with a cannonball after one of his opponents got out of the way, and Kofi delivered a double leg drop and Boom Drop through a pane of glass and chairs laid across Page.

The insanity also included thumbtacks, fire, and more barbed wire. Strickland tore at Bandido's mask, and Page jumped in to make the save. In a callback to the 2023 AEW Full Gear Texas Death match where Page drank Strickland's blood, Strickland drank a mouthful of Page's blood this time. Strickland then spit that blood right back in Hangman's face to prevent a chair shot, before hitting a Deadeye of his own. Bandido sent Strickland into thumbtacks with a frog splash, and Strickland brought out a cinder block, though Bandido stopped him from using it on Page. The New Level then took Bandido out with a move onto the edge of the block, while Strickland dropped King onto the steel ring steps with a Vertebreaker.

During the closing moments, with Bandido and King laid out on the outside, Page and Strickland did battle in the middle of the ring. Page connected with a series of Buckshot Lariats on Strickland and attempted a second, only for Swerve to counter with a steel chair shot. With all six competitors down, the referee began counting for the triple knockout or surrender stipulation. Strickland managed to return to his feet at eight, while Page barely answered the count at nine. Swerve immediately connected with a House Call that knocked Hangman back down, leaving him unable to answer the subsequent ten-count.

The referee called for the bell and completed the count, giving Swerve Strickland and The New Level the victory. The trio survived a match filled with blood, glass, barbed wire, and explosions to walk out with championship gold, becoming the new AEW World Trios Champions. Hangman Page and Brodido threw everything they had at their opponents, but it was not enough to stop the title change, leaving the commentators to note that nobody came out of this one as a winner considering everyone was covered in blood like they were victims at a murder scene.

🔥 Kris Statlander and Harley Cameron Win AEW Women's Tag Team Championship at Full Gear

Another championship changed hands at AEW Full Gear 2026 during the November 14 pay-per-view from Phoenix, Arizona. Kris Statlander and Harley Cameron, collectively known as Wrathlander, defeated Jamie Hayter and Alex Windsor of The Brawling Birds to capture the AEW Women's World Tag Team Championships.

Before the match, which was placed in a tough spot on the show following New Level's Texas Death match, Wrathlander prepared for their first pay-per-view match as a team by brainstorming wacky entrance ideas like being teleported by aliens or riding cows to the ring. Billy Gunn made a cameo to tell them to stick to basics by using cool pyro, to which Wrathlander responded by telling Daddy Ass to shut the fuck up. Tony Khan also had a cameo telling them to hurry up and get down to the ring.

The four women had previously been teammates in Blood and Guts, but when the time came to put respect and friendships aside, both teams fought hard. The Brawling Birds controlled stretches of the contest, using their chemistry and rough style to isolate Cameron and keep Statlander away from the action. Highlights of the back-and-forth action included Statlander connecting with a Michinoku Driver on Hayter, the champions delivering a Hate Breaker to Cameron, and dueling Electric Chair positions where Windsor and Cameron exchanged strikes while on their respective opponents' shoulders.

Hayter came close to putting the challengers away with heavy shots and lariats, but Kris Statlander repeatedly interrupted the pin attempts. Cameron also fought back, catching Hayter in a half-crab submission before Windsor broke up the hold. The champions looked ready to finish things when they attempted to set up their Two Birds One Stone finishing combination on Statlander. However, Harley Cameron prevented the move by pulling Hayter out of the ring.

That gave Statlander the opening she needed against Alex Windsor. With the champions teamwork momentarily disrupted and Cameron holding Hayter back from making the save, Statlander planted Windsor on the canvas with Saturday Night Fever and secured the three-count. Following the match, Hayter and Windsor shook hands with the new champions in a show of respect before Statlander and Cameron celebrated their victory.

The result brings The Brawling Birds championship reign, which began in late August, to an end. Their match had initially been scheduled for WrestleDream but was postponed following an injury to Jamie Hayter.

The victory also marked two significant milestones for the new champions. Harley Cameron made history as the first-ever two-time AEW Women's World Tag Team Champion, having won the inaugural title match with Willow Nightingale last December. Meanwhile, Kris Statlander became only the second woman in AEW history to win the female Triple Crown, having previously held the AEW Women's World and AEW TBS Championships. The other Triple Crown Champion is her close friend, Willow Nightingale. Wrathlander now holds the gold, while the former champions will have to regroup after seeing their title run come to an end.

🔥 The Cut Defeats Death Riders in Anarchy in the Arena Match at Full Gear

At AEW Full Gear 2026 in Phoenix, the annual Anarchy in the Arena match delivered an explosion of chaos, violence, and unexpected moments as The Cut faced off against the combined forces of The Death Riders and The Conglomeration. The match featured a massive lineup, with The Cut represented by Jay White, David Finlay, Clark Connors, Ace Austin, Austin Gunn, and Colten Gunn, while Jon Moxley, Daniel Garcia, Gabe Kidd, Orange Cassidy, Kyle O'Reilly, and Roderick Strong made up the opposing side.

True to the stipulation, the action started before everyone even reached the ring, with The Cut attacking members of The Conglomeration during their entrance. Moxley brought out his Wild Thing theme song and his signature fork to draw early blood, while Daniel Garcia offered some unusual entertainment by performing his signature dance to DMX playing Party Up. Shards of glass soon filled the ring, and the venue erupted into an out-of-control brawl.

The match was heavily impacted by a revolving door of outside interference and surprise appearances. The Phoenix Suns mascot, Go the Gorilla, entered the fight to spray the Gunns with a cannon before Austin took him out. David Finlay and Clark Connors also attempted to recruit Gabe Kidd into The Cut by offering him a shirt. Just as it looked like Kidd was turning on his allies, he shockingly struck Finlay with a steel chair instead.

Later, White and Connors attempted to use a heated branding iron on Roderick Strong, prompting Marina Shafir to intervene and put White in a sleeper hold. When The Cut tried to brand Shafir, Wheeler Yuta made the save. Shane Taylor Promotions then ran in to help The Cut, sending Yuta through a table. Mercedes Mone also made her presence known by delivering a low blow to Moxley from behind. Suddenly, the arena lights went out, revealing CMLL star Kemalito, who kicked Mone and hit a spinning headscissors to send her packing, where she was subsequently chased off by Willow Nightingale.

The chaos escalated further when Claudio Castagnoli arrived in a truck as a late ace up the sleeve for the opposition. Castagnoli cleaned house with a series of uppercuts, delivered a unique double swing to Finlay and Connors, and tossed Kemalito into Shane Taylor Promotions on the outside.

As the match reached its climax, the brawl moved back to the stage, where Connors and Finlay put their former friend Gabe Kidd through a flaming table. Back inside the ring, Jay White turned his full attention to Jon Moxley, planting him with a devastating Blade Runner directly onto the shattered glass still littering the canvas. White covered Moxley for the three count, securing a massive win for the newly formed faction as they stood tall over both rival groups to close out the unforgettable war.

🔥 Kazuchika Okada Defeats Adam Copeland to Retain AEW International Championship at Full Gear

Pro wrestling veteran Adam Copeland experienced a devastating loss at the hands of Kazuchika Okada at AEW Full Gear at the Mortgage Matchup Center in Phoenix, Arizona. The opening match of the main card quickly turned into a personal and brutal affair with the AEW International Championship on the line. Heading into the bout, AEW built hype by underscoring the legendary careers of both stars in a first-time-ever encounter representing two generations, two legendary careers, and one championship.

Copeland has amassed a legendary career due to his lengthy tenure at WWE where he went by the ring name Edge and won almost every title available, including the WWE Championship four times and the Heavyweight Championship seven times. At AEW, he has held the TNT Championship twice and the World Tag Team Championship once, alongside mainstream popularity from various acting roles. Meanwhile, wrestling fans know Okada for his legendary 18-year run at New Japan Pro-Wrestling where he won the IWGP Heavyweight Championship seven times. At AEW, he is an established member of the Don Callis Family stable, entering this bout as a two-time International Champion who won the title at AEW All In 2026 after defeating Kyle Fletcher and Konosuke Takeshita, holding it for a total of 77 days heading into his third title defense.

If fans were hoping for a balanced match, they were likely disappointed as the title bout proved quite one-sided. The men started off soaking in the moment before Copeland took Okada to the ropes and held his foot against the rope before they finally locked up. Okada faked out Copeland before laying in strikes and rolling out of the ring when Copeland picked up the offense. Okada sent Copeland into the steel steps and pulled up the mat to expose the floor. Copeland blocked a piledriver and landed a back body drop on the padded area, sending Okada across a steel chair before landing a big boot to the face. Copeland kicked Okada off the apron, but as he was jawing with Don Callis, Okada dropkicked him into the barricade and hugged Callis on the outside. Back in the ring, Okada focused on Copeland's neck and landed three consecutive DDTs. Copeland began fighting back, but Okada stopped him with a big boot, a scoop slam, and a flying elbow, though Copeland blocked a subsequent Rainmaker attempt.

Okada grabbed his belt, but the referee stopped him, leading to an exchange of blows and a piledriver from Okada. Copeland mocked Okada by flipping off the fans and charged at him, but Callis grabbed his leg. Okada then landed an Impaler on the exposed floor and laughed at him, briefly considering a count out before putting Copeland back in the ring. Okada pinned him, pulled him up, and hit the Rainmaker for the win after an almost 19-minute bout. The match also featured both competitors mocking each other's spear and iconic middle finger taunt. After the bell, Okada ran back into the ring to administer one last devastating Rainmaker to Copeland for good measure. Copeland has previously announced he will retire in 2027, making his final months in the ring intensely physical.

🔥 Tony Khan to Announce AEW Continental Classic Entrants During Monday Night Football

AEW President Tony Khan announced the details for the 2026 Continental Classic during a pre-show live stream on social media ahead of the Full Gear pay-per-view in Phoenix, Arizona. Things are not slowing down for AEW following the event, which featured brutal bouts including an Anarchy in the Arena match and a Texas Death match for the AEW World Trios Championships. This year marks the continuation of the prestigious round-robin tournament. Past winners of the Continental Classic include Eddie Kingston in 2023, Kazuchika Okada in 2024, and Jon Moxley in 2025, with Moxley defeating Kyle Fletcher and Kazuchika Okada at Worlds End last year to claim the crown. The semi-finals and finals for the 2026 edition will similarly take place at the company's final pay-per-view of the year, AEW Worlds End, scheduled for December 19.

Tony Khan Confirms Persephone Signed to Multi-Year AEW Deal

Tony Khan has put to rest any uncertainty regarding the future of Persephone by confirming that the CMLL standout is locked down to a long-term agreement with All Elite Wrestling. Speaking during the post-show press conference following AEW Full Gear, Khan addressed her contract status and made it clear that she will remain a fixture of the company for a long time.

I am happy to say that Persephone is somebody that is signed to AEW for years to come, Khan stated. I believe she is going to be a big part of AEW and CMLL for years to come.

This announcement clears up previous questions about the timeline of her tenure. When Persephone initially signed a dual contract with AEW and CMLL back in January 2026, reports indicated her deal might be up later in the year. Khan's latest comments confirm that AEW has secured her services well beyond 2026, ensuring she will not hit the open market anytime soon.

Her high-profile status also previously sparked behind-the-scenes confusion regarding WWE. Earlier reports suggested WWE had significant interest in Persephone following her standout performance at Forbidden Door. However, Bryan Alvarez later clarified that WWE never actually spoke directly with her because she was already under contract with CMLL and unable to negotiate elsewhere.

Persephone, I just want to clarify this, Alvarez explained. What I was told is that there have been stories saying she chose AEW over WWE, and of course the WWE side wasn’t happy about that. WWE made it very clear they never spoke to Persephone. She has been under contract with CMLL and was not able to talk to WWE at all.

Rather than leaving the Mexican promotion behind, Persephone added an AEW agreement to her resume, and her stock has continued to rise exponentially. She currently holds the CMLL World Women's Championship and has become increasingly involved in the AEW programming landscape.

As a former TBS Champion who is currently aligned with Britt Baker, Persephone most recently competed in a four-way match for the TBS Championship at Full Gear. While Khan did not reveal the exact number of years attached to her new deal, his commitment to having her feature prominently across both promotions ensures that international star power will remain a cornerstone of the television product for years to come.

AEW Full Gear 2026 Review and Analysis

AEW Full Gear 2026 was a massive event that featured everything from the former New Day competing in a Texas Death match to MJF making a shocking return to attack the world champion. The card also delivered high-stakes action like Anarchy in the Arena and the crowning of new women's tag team champions. For those who missed the live action, comprehensive coverage captured all the key moments from the desert show. Looking closer at what worked and what failed on the card highlights major strengths and weaknesses in the current booking.

One major negative from the night was the women's tag team match, which suffered from several glaring issues. The problems began with a severe lack of build, as Jamie Hayter's injury forced the match to be postponed from WrestleDream. Having the match happen after both teams were on the same side in Blood and Guts was awkwardly explained through a shared common goal, but light shoving does not equal a proper feud. Furthermore, the match placement did terrible favors for the competitors. Putting it right after the incredible Texas Death Match left the live crowd completely exhausted and unable to properly invest, despite the women doing the absolute best they could in a terrible spot.

The title change itself also missed the mark. There was immense anticipation for the Brawling Birds to win their titles in front of their hometown crowd at All In, but they only held the gold for 76 days, heavily hampered by Hayter's injuries. Dropping the belts so quickly after beating an undefeated team felt premature, especially given that their title defenses were repetitive. Tony Khan simply has not done enough to build up the women's tag division or sign enough distinct teams, leading to endless rematches and five-minute indie squashes. While the men's division continues to grow and sign fresh talent like a newly signed tag team debuting tonight, the women's tag division desperately needs more depth, more team signings, and stronger creative backing.

On the bright side, the Young Bucks delivered an absolute masterclass. Considering the show was obscenely long, it would have been easy to overlook the AEW World Tag Team Championship match between the Young Bucks and the duo of Mark Davis and Jake Doyle, but it easily stood out as one of the best bouts of the night. While a previous match against Kyle Fletcher and Kevin Knight was a fun, brainless exhibition of move-after-move, this encounter thrived by leaning into the most reliable and trusted formula in wrestling: the classic David versus Goliath story. The babyfaces needed to overcome the Goliath heels, which was a tough task when those heels are two of the most impressive big men in wrestling today. The Young Bucks proved their natural versatility by tapping into a strong babyface role to deliver one of their best matches in recent memory.

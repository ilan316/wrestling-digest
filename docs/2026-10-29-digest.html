🔥 Queen Aminata wins the TBS Championship on AEW Dynamite

AEW crowned a new TBS champion after a tough match on the October 28 episode of Dynamite, specifically the Halloween-themed Fright Night special from St. Louis, Missouri. Queen Aminata earned the title after defeating Persephone for the first time in her career in the main event of the evening. Aminata earned her title shot on the previous week's Dynamite when she defeated the returning Maya World in a number one contender's match. This marked Aminata's fifth crack at the title dating back nearly two years, as well as her first championship win in AEW overall, having previously unsuccessfully challenged for titles such as the ROH Women's World Championship against Athena and the AEW Women's World Championship against Mercedes Mone. Persephone entered the bout making the fifth defense of her title since winning it from Maya World at All In in August, having previously defended against Vertvixen and Hyan, and seeing off Aminata in a four-way match that also involved Hyan and Dani Luna at WrestleDream. Her reign ultimately ended at 60 days after four successful title defenses.

Persephone defended her title with help from Britt Baker, who was dressed as Miss Trunchbull from the movie Matilda and repeatedly tried to interfere from ringside. Aminata wasted no time once the match began, rolling up Persephone after the opening lock-up before Persephone kicked out and trapped her in a submission hold. Aminata reached the bottom rope to break free and fought back with clotheslines, a double stomp off the ropes, and a meteora that sent Persephone crashing over a chair at ringside. Persephone also worked over the injured area of Aminata's neck, even ripping the kinesiology tape off the back of her neck during the match. Later, while referee Aubrey Edwards was checking on Persephone at ringside, Baker took full advantage. She struck Aminata with the TBS Championship belt and threw her back into the ring, allowing Persephone to land a top-rope moonsault. Aminata amazingly kicked out of the pin, leaving Baker visibly frustrated at ringside.

Aminata eventually found the opening she needed to overcome the champion and the outside interference. She recovered, rocked Persephone with a headbutt, and connected with her signature running knee strike, also referred to as Off With Her Head, leaving Persephone unable to recover. Aminata covered the champion and secured the three-count to become the tenth TBS Champion in AEW history. Following the match, pyrotechnics erupted and confetti fell from above as an emotional Aminata celebrated her victory. The rest of the babyfaces and women from the locker room, including Willow Nightingale, Thunder Rosa, Hyan, Maya World, Mina Shirakawa, Harley Cameron, and Kris Statlander, poured into the ring to congratulate Aminata on the biggest win of her career and bowed before the new champion, bringing the Fright Night edition of Dynamite to a close.

🔥 Will Ospreay vs. Kyle Fletcher set for AEW Full Gear

Kyle Fletcher has his sights firmly set on the AEW World Championship, and he has officially secured his opportunity to challenge reigning champion Will Ospreay at AEW Full Gear 2026. Following a hard-fought and competitive singles match against Jack Perry on the Fright Night episode of Dynamite, Fletcher was joined in the ring by Don Callis Family members Don Callis, Mark Davis, and Jake Doyle. Fletcher first highlighted that Davis and Doyle would be capturing the AEW World Tag Team Championships at the pay-per-view before shifting focus to his own championship aspirations. Perry had nearly pulled off the victory during their bout after locking in the Snare Trap, but Fletcher managed to reach the ropes before eventually putting Perry away with a brainbuster.

Fletcher made his case for a world title shot by reminding the live crowd and viewers at home of his extensive history with Ospreay. The two former United Empire stablemates have shared a bitter rivalry since Fletcher and later Davis turned against Ospreay. Fletcher pointed to his previous singles victory over Ospreay at the Full Gear pay-per-view two years ago, as well as a recent tag team victory alongside Kevin Knight over Ospreay and Adam Copeland, as definitive evidence that he deserves the spot. Declaring himself the uncrowned champion, Fletcher delivered a clear warning, stating that he has beaten Will Ospreay at Full Gear before and is going to do it again. He then dared the champion to put the gold on the line.

AEW President Tony Khan wasted little time making the high-stakes match official. Ospreay will defend the AEW World Championship against Fletcher in the main event of Full Gear on Saturday, November 14, 2026, at the Mortgage Matchup Center in Phoenix, Arizona. Their history spans four previous singles encounters in AEW dating back to March 2024, with Ospreay holding a 3-1 advantage. Their most recent one-on-one clash took place inside a steel cage at Revolution in March 2025, where Ospreay emerged victorious. Meanwhile, Fletcher is looking for his second career shot at the top prize, having previously fallen in defeat to then-champion Hangman Page at All Out in September 2025, while Ospreay seeks his fourth successful title defense.

Before they collide for the championship, both men are scheduled to endure a brutal test on the November 4 episode of Dynamite. Fletcher and Ospreay will both compete in the men's Blood and Guts match, with Fletcher representing the Don Callis Family and Ospreay teaming up with The Young Bucks, Adam Copeland, Darby Allin, and the recently returned Steven Borden. This punishing encounter threatens to leave both competitors with extra wear and tear, potentially impacting them as they head into their championship showdown at Full Gear.

In addition to the marquee world title main event, several other championship matches are officially confirmed for the Full Gear 2026 card in Phoenix. The Young Bucks will defend the AEW World Tag Team Championships against Mark Davis and Jake Doyle. The Brawling Birds, consisting of Jamie Hayter and Alex Windsor, will put the AEW Women’s World Tag Team Championships on the line against Wrathlander, featuring Kris Statlander and Harley Cameron. Finally, AEW World Trios Champions Hangman Page, Brody King, and Bandido will defend their titles against Swerve Strickland, Kofi Kingston, and Austin Creed.

🔥 Anarchy in the Arena match set for AEW Full Gear

During the Fright Night edition of AEW Dynamite in St. Louis, Missouri, Jon Moxley issued a challenge for a major stipulation match at AEW Full Gear. The event takes place on Saturday, November 14, 2026, at the Mortgage Matchup Center in Phoenix, Arizona. The challenge came after the opening tag-team contest where AEW Continental Champion Jon Moxley and Daniel Garcia came up short against Jay White and David Finlay of the newly formed faction known as The Cut.

The opening bout featured interference from both factions. Ace Austin ran through the crowd to distract the referee while Wheeler Yuta tried to stop him with a Busaiku Knee. However, the distraction allowed Clark Connors to spear Garcia, and White followed up by hitting the Blade Runner on Garcia to secure the victory for his team.

Things quickly escalated after the bell when members of The Death Riders and The Cut rushed the ring, sparking a wild brawl. Gabe Kidd joined the rest of The Cut in launching a post-match attack on Moxley, Garcia, and Wheeler Yuta. Claudio Castagnoli eventually hit the ring to serve as a one-man wrecking crew, catching Clark Connors in the Giant Swing. The Death Riders then armed themselves with steel chairs and cleared the ring, sending The Cut retreating.

With his rivals regrouping outside, Moxley grabbed a microphone and declared that the war between the two factions was only getting started, drawing loud chants of anarchy from the St. Louis crowd. Moxley laid out the challenge for an Anarchy in the Arena match at AEW Full Gear. The segment concluded with The Death Riders dropping to the mat and doing push-ups together as their entrance music played.

While AEW has yet to officially confirm the match and announce the full participant lists, the challenge sets up a massive violent showdown. The Death Riders side appears to be set with Jon Moxley, Claudio Castagnoli, Gabe Kidd, Wheeler Yuta, and Daniel Garcia, while Jay White and David Finlay will represent The Cut alongside three other members. No one from The Cut has previous experience in this specific match type. The group was formed when White, Juice Robinson, Ace Austin, and the Gunns joined forces with the Dogs, David Finlay and Clark Connors.

This will mark the fifth-ever Anarchy in the Arena match in company history. The previous four iterations all took place at Double or Nothing events, with the most recent one happening in Glendale, Arizona last year.

The pay-per-view already features several confirmed championship matches. The AEW World Tag Team Champions The Young Bucks will defend against Mark Davis and Jake Doyle. The AEW Women's World Tag Team Champions The Brawling Birds will defend against Wrathlander. Additionally, AEW World Trios Champions Hangman Page, Brody King, and Bandido will defend their titles against Swerve Strickland, Austin Creed, and Kofi Kingston. Moxleys challenge adds even more heat to an already stacked pay-per-view lineup as fans await official confirmation from the promotion.

🔥 Toni Storm AEW return updates and teases

AEW has reignited the mystery surrounding Timeless Toni Storm with a series of unusual clues, but fans waiting for the former Women's World Champion to return to the ring will need to exercise patience. Storm has been absent from AEW television since defeating Marina Shafir at AEW Revolution in March 2026. She was subsequently written out of storylines following a mysterious backstage attack where she was found bloodied on the floor, leaving her status and the identity of her attacker unresolved.

According to backstage reports, AEW officials were aware following Revolution that Storm would be unavailable until late 2026 at the earliest. Despite her prolonged absence, the company previously had significant creative plans involving her throughout the year. Creative had developed a storyline surrounding her disappearance with plans to introduce several clues pointing toward different potential suspects responsible for taking her out. While the creative direction included a specific individual originally intended to be revealed as the mystery attacker, the identity was deliberately left flexible in case circumstances required AEW to change plans before the eventual reveal.

The first apparent signs of that storyline resurfacing came during AEW WrestleDream on October 17, when select media members and content creators attending the event discovered cassette tapes that had seemingly been planted as part of an effort to begin teasing Storm's return. Content creator Astrid Pizarro was among those who shared details of the tapes online, noting she was handed a package containing a cassette labeled Toni Storm Tapes, marked as the first of five. The first recording, titled Mundanities, featured Renee Paquette narrating audio supposedly recorded by a device Storm secretly carried in her purse. The fifth cassette reportedly carries the title Death, leaving more questions to answer as the mystery develops.

Despite these developments, sources have indicated that Storm's actual in-ring return is not considered imminent. There is, however, the possibility that additional storyline elements involving the former champion could begin appearing on television before she is ready to wrestle again. Storm has also reportedly been absent from AEW events throughout her time away, with little indication that she has been backstage at any recent shows.

AEW President Tony Khan addressed Storm's future during the WrestleDream media scrum, sharing an update regarding her absence and praising the locker room. Khan stated that while they have been missing Timeless Toni Storm, everybody has been doing absolutely fantastic and the women's wrestling in AEW has seen the locker room step up in her absence, alongside great world champion Mercedes Mone who had her first successful defense of the title at the event. Khan assured fans that Storm is on their minds and will eventually return to AEW looking to get payback for whoever took her out, though he could not specify when or where.

For now, AEW appears ready to keep the mystery alive while Storm's wrestling comeback remains uncertain. The cassette tapes give the company a way to continue the mystery surrounding her attacker, but fans should not mistake those teases for confirmation of an immediate comeback to the ring, as the full story continues to unwind.

🔥 Tony Khan comments on AEW ticket prices

Ahead of the November 4 edition of Dynamite: Blood and Guts at the VyStar Veterans Memorial Arena in Jacksonville, Florida, AEW President Tony Khan has promised fans an affordable night of live professional wrestling. This connection makes bringing the special Dynamite episode to the VyStar Veterans Memorial Arena particularly fitting, as Khan looks to draw a packed house for what he calls the biggest episode of Dynamite of the year.

🔥 Queen Aminata praises Mercedes Mone

During a recent appearance on CMLL Informa, AEW star Queen Aminata opened up about the lasting impact of her match against Mercedes Mone at the Grand Slam France edition of Dynamite. Reflecting on the encounter in Paris, where she grew up, Aminata explained how stepping into the ring with the AEW Women's Champion completely changed her perspective on her own wrestling career and motivated her to push herself to new heights.

Aminata shared that while she never had a particular wrestling role model during her childhood, preferring to make herself her own role model, her experience with Mone sparked a powerful new ambition. Something clicked in her head after the match, leaving her with the drive to match Mone's elite level of performance. She emphasized that Mone is truly amazing and bluntly stated her belief that people simply do not give the champion enough credit for her abilities.

The personal significance of the event added another layer to the experience for Aminata, noting that performing in France where she spent her formative years made the opportunity unforgettable. She credited Mone's elite skill set with transforming her into a fundamentally different and more confident wrestler.

Despite having already shared the ring with the champion during her title defense sequence following All In, Aminata made it clear that her rivalry or desire to test herself is far from over. She expressed a strong, enthusiastic wish for their paths to cross again so she can step into the ring with Mone for another match. While the future holds various challengers for the championship landscape, including recent confrontations from names like Marina Shafir, Aminata has firmly placed a rematch against the one and only Mercedes Mone on her career wish list.

AEW Dynamite 10/28/2026 reviews and reports

AEW Dynamite returned with its Halloween-themed episode dubbed Fright Night, delivering plenty of in-ring action and major developments, though it left reviewers with mixed feelings about the holiday execution. The broadcast featured major moments, including Queen Aminata winning the TBS Championship and Darby Allin retaining the TNT Championship, alongside setup for upcoming pay-per-view events. However, the review crew had specific thoughts on what worked and what fell short during the night.

One major critique of the evening was that the Fright Night theme fell flat so close to big upcoming events. While AEW had been on a hot stretch of themed episodes in 2026, the Halloween edition lacked actual spooky-themed stipulation matches. This was likely due to Blood & Guts taking place next week, as well as the strict no-blood rule enforced by the Missouri Athletic Commission. Outside of a few stars wearing costumes and some nice graphics, the show did not capture a strong Halloween spirit. Even without blood, viewers might have expected a slightly more festive or creative approach rather than a standard card, especially given the name Fright Night.

This issue highlights a broader challenge for AEW currently, as too many events are trying to do too much in a short period of time. The build to WrestleDream was rushed, and things still feel a bit chaotic and fast-paced. The booking team may have held back on over-the-top elements ahead of Blood & Guts. Furthermore, another massive announcement revealed that Anarchy in the Arena will take place at AEW Full Gear, further packing the promotion's busy schedule. Despite the lack of spooky holiday atmosphere, the show delivered strong in-ring competition, a fantastic title change in the main event, and effective setup for Full Gear.

🔥 Tommaso Ciampa defeats Andrade El Idolo for AEW National Championship on Collision

Tommaso Ciampa is the new AEW National Champion. On the November 7, 2026, episode of AEW Collision, Andrade El Idolo defended his AEW National Title against Tommaso Ciampa during a wild main event aboard the Chris Jericho Rock 'N' Wrestling Rager at Sea cruise ship in Miami. The victory marks Ciampa's first reign with the championship and ends Andrade's run at 104 days, after he originally defeated Mark Davis back in July.

The two first wrestled at the WrestleDream pay-per-view last month, where Andrade defeated Ciampa to retain the title. Ciampa continued to focus on Idolo and the National championship in the weeks after, admitting he took Idolo too lightly but vowing to win the next time they met.

The Psycho Killer did not fight clean to secure the gold. Prior to the match, he ordered referee Aubrey Edwards to check Andrade's pants for a foreign object, using the ploy to land a cheap shot and gain the early advantage. Later in the contest, Ciampa took matters into his own hands and ripped Andrade's trousers off. Outdoor elements also played a factor when rain started falling aboard the Norwegian Jewel, leaving the ring surface slippery and prompting Edwards to check on Andrade after an awkward landing near the apron.

Controversy arose later from a ref bump. Ciampa sent Andrade full steam toward Edwards, but El Idolo stopped short. Ciampa charged forward, Andrade sidestepped, and Ciampa collided with Edwards, leaving the referee down and unable to count Andrade's subsequent pinning attempt following the DM finisher. As Andrade checked on the referee, Ciampa struck with a low blow and an exposed running knee. Andrade kicked out, then answered by lowering his own knee pad to blast Ciampa with a running knee and followed up with a hammerlock DDT, but Ciampa also refused to stay stay down.

As the chaotic action continued, Andrade went for another DM, but Ciampa countered. After a back body drop, Ciampa hooked Andrade's legs and secured the three-count to become the new AEW National Champion. Following the emotional victory, Ciampa celebrated by posing with his daughter and placing the championship on her shoulder. The massive upset gives Ciampa momentum heading toward Full Gear, while Andrade now finds himself without the title he fought so hard to retain. Meanwhile, backstage developments indicate that Ciampa and AEW's newest signee, Ilja Dragunov, have been crossing paths on programming, teasing a possible future match between the two.

🔥 Mercedes Moné mourns death of younger sister Alexis

AEW Women’s World Champion Mercedes Moné has opened up about an extremely difficult period in her personal life, revealing that her younger sister, Alexis Varnado, passed away in September.

In the latest edition of her Moné Mag newsletter, Moné reflected on the devastating loss of her sister, who leaves behind two young children, while sharing memories of their childhood and the time they spent together. September was an incredibly hard month for me because I lost my younger sister, Alexis, Moné wrote. It’s still difficult to talk about. She was so young, and knowing she left behind two young children makes my heart hurt even more.

Moné recalled attending WrestleMania in Atlanta with Alexis when they were younger, describing the experience as a cherished memory from before she achieved her own dreams in professional wrestling. One memory I keep thinking about is when we went to WrestleMania together in Atlanta when we were younger, she continued. Wrestling has been such a huge part of my life, and I love that I have that memory with my sister from before so many of my own dreams came true.

The former WWE Superstar also remembered Alexis helping care for their mother in 2020, expressing gratitude for their time together while struggling to come to terms with her sister’s passing. I wish Alexis had more time. I wish her children had more time with their mom. I’m still trying to understand how somebody so young can be gone. I love you, Alexis.

Moné revealed that her grief was compounded later that month by the unexpected death of longtime friend and fellow wrestler PAC, who passed away on September 27 at the age of 40. And then, while I was still grieving my sister, we unexpectedly lost PAC, Moné wrote. That was incredibly hard, and it still is.

Reflecting on their friendship, Moné explained that she had known PAC since their time in FCW and NXT. She recalled numerous memories from their years traveling together, including playing video games with Austin Creed and sharing laughs with fellow WWE Superstars. We spent so much time laughing while playing UpUpDownDown with Austin Creed. We had Rocket League championships together, she recalled. I remember being on the buses during different tours and listening to his stories with Sami Zayn. Having the whole bus laughing There were so many funny moments that I wish I could go back and experience one more time.

Moné also discussed her decision to participate in the September 30 tribute show honoring PAC, explaining that stepping into the ring felt like the most meaningful way to pay her respects despite the emotional circumstances. The memorial tribute was absolutely beautiful, but the whole show was emotionally difficult for me, she wrote. I knew I needed to be part of it. I couldn’t imagine just staying backstage. Wrestling was something PAC and I had shared for so many years, and being in the ring felt like the way I needed to honor him.

During the tribute event, Moné teamed with Britt Baker and Persephone in a losing effort against Hyan, Marina Shafir, and Thunder Rosa.

Beyond their personal friendship, Moné praised PAC’s contributions to professional wrestling, highlighting his athleticism and the influence he had on other performers throughout his career. His influence on professional wrestling is going to live on forever, Moné wrote. You can see what he brought to this sport in so many wrestlers today. His athleticism and the things he could do inside a ring inspired an entire generation of performers.

However, Moné emphasized that her memories of PAC extend well beyond his accomplishments inside the ring, concluding her tribute with a heartfelt message to her longtime friend. But for me, I’ll also remember the person behind all of that. I’m so grateful I got to share those memories with him. I miss you, PAC.

Mone defeated Willow Nightingale for the title at All In on August 30 and has since made successful defenses against Queen Aminata at Grand Slam France on October 6 and Thekla at WrestleDream on October 17. She is currently scheduled to defend the AEW Women’s World Championship against Marina Shafir at AEW Full Gear on November 14 at the Mortgage Matchup Center in Phoenix, Arizona.

Kazuchika Okada vs. Adam Copeland International Title match set for AEW Full Gear

A high-stakes match for the AEW International Championship has been officially added to the lineup for AEW Full Gear, taking place next Saturday in Phoenix, Arizona. During Saturday night's episode of Collision, it was confirmed that reigning champion Kazuchika Okada will put his title on the line against Adam Copeland. The two competitors have found themselves increasingly at odds over the past few weeks, setting the stage for an explosive showdown.

Their paths have crossed multiple times recently, fueling a fiery personal feud. Last month at the WrestleDream event, both Okada and Copeland participated in the historic first-ever Clockwork Carnage match. Tension boiled over further on the October 21 edition of Dynamite. During a match where Copeland teamed with Will Ospreay against Kyle Fletcher and Kevin Knight, Okada interfered and confronted Copeland. Okada placed the International title on a turnbuckle, causing Copeland to crash directly into it and ultimately costing his team the match.

The animosity continued into a brutal Blood and Guts match. That grueling encounter ended when Copeland was forced to surrender on behalf of his team after Fletcher isolated Ospreay and threatened him by holding a screwdriver to his neck.

With the Okada-Copeland clash now official, the Full Gear card is heavily stacked with major bouts. The current lineup features AEW World Champion Will Ospreay defending against Kyle Fletcher, AEW Women’s World Champion Mercedes Mone defending against Marina Shafir, and AEW World Tag Team Champions The Young Bucks defending against Mark Davis and Jake Doyle. Also scheduled are AEW Women’s World Tag Team Champions The Brawling Birds defending against Wrathlander, and an AEW World Trios Championship Texas Death Match with Hangman Page, Brody King, and Bandido defending against Swerve Strickland, Kofi, and Austin Creed. The event will also feature an Anarchy in the Arena match pitting The Death Riders and The Conglomeration against one another.

Kyle Fletcher on Don Callis' advice

AEW star Kyle Fletcher recently revealed the most valuable advice he has received from Don Callis. Being a part of the Don Callis Family for almost two years now, Fletcher recently opened up about the guidance he has received from the veteran.

Speaking on What Happened When with Tony Schiavone, Fletcher shared that Callis regularly sends him clips and suggestions, but the most valuable lesson has been confidence in his abilities. He credited Callis encouragement with helping him develop both his in-ring and presentation styles.

It feels like almost every week there is a new story or a new try this or try that, Fletcher shared. He will literally just send me random clips that he sees all the time. One of, I think probably the biggest takeaway that I have got from him is just to believe in myself. I know, I know it sounds so corny, but it is like having someone like that who has been in the business so long, like really, really backing you and really in your corner and helping you realize your good traits and the things that you do that work well.

I think that is definitely the most valuable thing he has ever given me, he continued. It has given me that confidence. You know I think you can tell in the way that I wrestle now. You can see in the way I present myself now. I would not have that confidence if it was not for Don Callis.

Fletcher is now scheduled to face Will Ospreay at AEW Full Gear 2026 for the AEW World Championship.

WON Flashback: FTR vs. The Young Bucks at AEW Full Gear 2020

November 7 2020 was the date of that year’s AEW Full Gear pay-per-view, and arguably the best match on the card was FTR versus The Young Bucks, which received five and a quarter stars in that week’s newsletter review. The match saw the Young Bucks beat FTR in 28 minutes and 46 seconds to win the AEW tag titles. FTR came out in gear that read GTTOAT for greatest tag team of all-time. Playing off a decades long NBA rivalry, the Bucks wore new Lakers-inspired gear while FTR wore Celtics-inspired gear.

The whole match was all action and filled with incredible spots, twists, and turns. It also served as a tribute to the famous moves of both teams favorite tag teams. The Bucks performed a double dropkick popularized by the Rock and Roll Express. Dax Harwood punched the post and bled from the hand, prompting Matt Jackson to work on it and Doc Sampson to check Harwood’s hand, after which Harwood started working on Matt’s ankle. Cash Wheeler wrapped Matt’s left knee around the post. Harwood superplexed Matt off the top and Wheeler went for a frog splash in a nod to Power and Glory, but Matt got his knees up, further injuring himself. Cash came off the apron onto Matt, who moved, causing Cash to catch his throat on the guard rail. Matt backdropped Cash over the top rope to the floor and tagged Nick, who began throwing a variety of kicks.

FTR executed the Hart Attack of the Hart Foundation on Nick and a sky-high bulldog of the Steiners on Matt. There was a 3-D of the Dudleys, and Matt hit a Twist of Fate and Swanton of the Hardys on Harwood. Cash clotheslined Matt’s ankle, and Matt kicked out of a DIY attempt. FTR went for the spike piledriver, but Nick pushed Cash off the top rope and hit a flip dive on the stage. Matt applied the sharpshooter on Harwood but had to release it while selling the knee. Matt went back to work on Harwood’s hand, using the finger break spot of Sabre and Scurll, and followed with the BTE trigger, but Cash saved. Nick hit an escalera on Cash. Matt retrieved a chair, and Harwood begged him to use it for a disqualification, which would mean they would never get another title shot. Nick screamed at him not to do it, and he did not.

They set up the Meltzer Driver on Harwood, but Cash pulled Nick off the top rope and powerbombed him through a table. They hit the spike piledriver on Matt, but he managed to get his leg on the ropes. They pulled off Matt’s boot and sock, and Cash stomped his leg before Harwood applied a reverse figure four. Cash went to tackle Nick to stop a save, but Nick moved and Cash flew out of the ring. Nick then hit a 450 splash on Harwood to break up the hold. Cash superkicked Nick out of the ring and kicked Matt in the face. Cash missed a springboard 450, with the idea that the no flips guys had exhausted their playbook and couldn’t finish them, so they went against their motto and tried a flip. Matt superkicked Cash with his bad leg and fell on him for the pinfall victory.

Kenny Omega came out right away to celebrate with them, an offshoot of how in Japan Omega would have these classic matches and the Young Bucks would celebrate in the ring with him. Hangman Page wanted to come out, but seeing Omega and the Bucks looking so happy together, he simply stood there and watched.

US Senator Ruben Gallego declares allegiance to AEW

United States Senator Ruben Gallego has officially declared himself an All Elite Wrestling fan in a lighthearted response to a social media post involving WWE Hall of Famer Kane. Glenn Jacobs, globally recognized by his wrestling moniker Kane, recently concluded his eight-year run as the mayor of Knox County, Tennessee, when his second consecutive mayoral term officially ended on September 1. However, political reports indicate that he is widely viewed as a top candidate for the state's next United States Senate seat.

Current U.S. Senator for Tennessee Marsha Blackburn won the state gubernatorial election on November 3, 2026. Reports indicate that she will appoint her legislative successor after she is officially sworn in as governor in January 2027. Kane is currently viewed as the favorite to secure this appointment, and if selected, he would serve in Washington until a special election is held in 2028.

Following the circulation of these political reports, a fan on social media posted a classic video clip of Rey Mysterio defeating Kane at WWE Cyber Sunday 2008, accompanied by a caption imagining Ruben Gallego reacting to seeing Glenn Jacobs on the senate floor. Senator Gallego caught wind of the post and publicly pledged his allegiance to the competing wrestling company, writing that he is more of an AEW man.

Kane last competed inside a squared circle during the 2021 men Royal Rumble match. Amid the growing speculation regarding his potential Senate appointment, the legendary wrestler recently reunited with U.S. Education Secretary Linda McMahon at an event focused on higher education, which was held at Vanderbilt University in Nashville, Tennessee. With political pathways potentially bringing the wrestling veteran to the halls of Congress, Senator Gallego has firmly drawn his promotional battle lines.

AEW WrestleDream 2026 PPV buys trending near low numbers

AEW WrestleDream 2026 is reportedly on pace to finish among the company's lowest-performing pay-per-view events of the year. According to industry reporting, the October 17 event is currently trending toward an estimated 100,000 to 110,000 pay-per-view buys. That would put WrestleDream in the same range as AEW Redemption, which recorded the company's lowest PPV buy total of 2026. The projected figure would also represent a decline from WrestleDream 2025, which was estimated to have generated between 115,000 and 125,000 buys.

Domestic purchases were down for the event, although international business showed improvement, particularly through HBO Max. The HBO Max numbers, representing the biggest platform nowadays, were actually up from last year's WrestleDream show which was estimated at about 120,000 buys. For comparison, last year's event featured Adam Page versus Samoa Joe and Darby Allin versus Jon Moxley in an I Quit match. Despite the strong streaming performance, the overall total looks to be similar to Redemption, featuring lower numbers domestically and larger numbers outside the United States.

Analysts have also pointed to a developing trend involving AEW's international audience, suggesting that Will Ospreay's presence and the company's recent shift toward Saturday night pay-per-views could be contributing factors. Whether it is Will Ospreay, or more likely him and going on Saturday nights instead of Sunday nights, international numbers are generally doing a higher percentage than domestic for the last three shows.

WrestleDream 2026 was headlined by Will Ospreay successfully defending the AEW World Championship in the grueling eight-man Clockwork Carnage match. In addition to the pay-per-view metrics, the live gate drew 7,173 fans to the venue. While AEW referred to the event as a sellout on the air, it was actually 161 fans short of a complete sellout. This marked the fourth edition of the annual pay-per-view, following the very first event that took place on October 1, 2023, in Seattle, Washington. Officials note that these buy figures remain preliminary estimates rather than finalized totals.

🔄 המשך 🔥 Ilja Dragunov reflects on decision to join AEW and his new theme music

Dragunov is expanding on what led to his WWE departure. Speaking recently, the former champion explained that after returning from a complicated ACL tear and quickly winning the United States title, he felt a shift in his gut after dropping the belt. Wondering if there was more to experience and needing to breathe new air, he ultimately decided that WWE's renewed contract offer wasn't enough to commit his future, setting the stage for his arrival in AEW.

A major highlight of that transition was reclaiming a piece of his history. Explaining the choice to media, he noted that the heavy classical vibe fits his over-the-top, orchestra-composer-like persona while honoring his Russian-born and German-raised roots.

He praised AEW for a deeply collaborative creative process that successfully secured the rights to his classic theme, admitting that hearing the music play made him feel like a child in the business again. With his presentation firmly established, Dragunov wasted no time proving himself in the ring, securing a hard-fought victory over Speedball Mike Bailey in the main event of his Dynamite in-ring debut.

🔄 המשך Details emerge on health history of former AEW star PAC

New information sheds light on the health management and medical background of the late wrestler known to fans as PAC and Neville. According to recent reports, PAC had a history of lung issues and previous blood clots dating back to 2023. These health concerns reportedly influenced how he managed his international travel schedule, leading him to limit long flights by staying in America for extended periods before taking multi-week breaks in the United Kingdom, unlike peers who fly back and forth more frequently.

Despite maintaining an intense physical training routine that included heavy weightlifting and cardio, PAC suffered a fatal bronchial asthma attack. Medical professionals have addressed the tragedy, noting that deaths from asthma are exceptionally rare unless the individual has a history of prior hospitalizations, which was not indicated in this case.

Specialists discussed the connection between long-haul air travel and pulmonary embolisms, explaining that while preventative measures like compression stockings and blood thinners are often used, previous blood clots can theoretically strain the right side of the heart. Medical experts emphasized that while underlying heart strain from past clots can theoretically increase risks during a severe asthma attack, there is no definitive medical confirmation that these factors contributed to his death without reviewing specific medical records. Specialists also pointed out that the rapid onset of the emergency left little time for intervention, noting that the outcome might have differed had he been near the medical personnel typically present at wrestling events.

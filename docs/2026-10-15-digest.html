🔥 Clockwork Carnage match qualifiers and updates for AEW WrestleDream

After a set of qualifiers on Wednesday, the field of challengers is now confirmed for the first-ever Clockwork Carnage match at AEW WrestleDream on October 17. The seven challengers will look to upend AEW World Champion Will Ospreay in a match that is described as a combination of a Casino Gauntlet and an Iron Man match. The Collision portion of the three-hour Wednesday show closed with all eight men brawling.

Adam Copeland made his first appearance in the ring since AEW All Out by defeating Josh Alexander in a qualifier without any championships on the line. The Walking Weapon spent the majority of the match targeting the leg of the Rated-R Superstar, the same leg that Copeland broke back in 2024. Alexander brought an exposed turnbuckle into the match, which ultimately backfired when he went head-first into it, allowing Copeland to muster enough momentum to hit a Spear and book his spot. Following his victory, Copeland delivered a surprising message to the fans, stating that his face is not on teenagers' walls anymore and that he is aging out. Because of this, he announced that 2027 will be his last year in professional wrestling, and that is why he desperately has to go for the AEW Championship.

Konosuke Takeshita also made his return to action by outlasting Brian Cage in a physical battle. The Machine dug deep into his bag of tricks, hitting an Avalanche Hurricanrana and a Tope Con Hilo to throw The Alpha off his game. Takeshita was forced to change strategy, ultimately hitting the Power Drive Knee and locking in a Dragon Sleeper to get the submission victory.

Ricochet earned his spot in the match by defeating Ace Austin of The Bang Bang Gang, who was stepping in to replace the injured Jay White. Austin kept himself in the fight using his speed and leg strength, kicking Ricochet at every opportunity. Ricochet used his own speed and nearly missed a Shooting Star Press, but he caught Austin off guard with a headbutt, landed a Spirit Gun, and hit a Vertigo to score the victory.

Kazuchika Okada not only stayed AEW International Champion, but he also qualified for the Clockwork Carnage match with a victory over Myron Reed. Reed pushed him hard, connecting with a 450 Splash from the steel steps to the outside. Okada rolled out of the way of another 450 attempt in the ring and hit the Rainmaker for the victory.

The final spot was determined in the main event of the Collision portion, where TNT Champion Darby Allin defended his title against Hechicero. Hechicero started with the advantage thanks to his grappling and the injured, strapped-up leg of Allin. Allin fought his way into the bout using dirtier tactics, biting at the hand of Hechicero. After being driven face-first into the barricade, Allin took things to the outside, hitting a tope followed by a Coffin Drop to a standing Hechicero. Allin then hit two more Coffin Drops inside the ring to secure the winning pinfall, retaining his title and joining the WrestleDream main event field.

The current lineup for AEW WrestleDream features Mercedes Mone defending the AEW Women's World Championship against Thekla, and the Clockwork Carnage match featuring Will Ospreay, Samoa Joe, Mike Bailey, Kazuchika Okada, Adam Copeland, Konosuke Takeshita, Ricochet, and Darby Allin. The Young Bucks will defend the AEW World Tag Team Championships against Kevin Knight and Kyle Fletcher, Jon Moxley will defend the AEW Continental Championship against Mark Davis, and Swerve Strickland and The New Level will defend the AEW Trios Championships against Adam Page and Brodido in a two-out-of-three falls match. Additionally, Andrade El Idolo defends the AEW National Championship against Tommaso Ciampa, FTR faces the Motor City Machine Guns in match one of a best-of-five series, Chris Jericho faces Nick Wayne, and Persephone defends the TBS Championship against Dani Luna, Queen Aminata, and Hyan in a four-way match. A pre-show match will feature Divine Dominion taking on Lena Kross and Megan Bayne.

🔥 Adam Copeland retirement announcement and Christian Cage injury update

During the special three-hour edition of AEW Dynamite and Collision from Cleveland, Ohio, Adam Copeland delivered a massive bombshell regarding his in-ring future. The veteran star defeated Josh Alexander in a Clockwork Carnage qualifying match, securing his spot in the upcoming multi-man bout scheduled for AEW WrestleDream on Saturday, where the winner receives an AEW World Championship opportunity. However, the post-match segment quickly shifted from a simple victory celebration into an emotional retirement announcement. Copeland addressed the Cleveland crowd and revealed that 2027 will officially be his final year as an active professional wrestler. Addressing the physical toll of the business, Copeland noted that he has reached a point where he must seriously consider his future, acknowledging that he is aging out and his face is no longer on posters in teenagers bedrooms.

Copeland explained that recent events involving his longtime friend and tag team partner Christian Cage forced him to do some serious soul-searching. He revealed the exact extent of the brutal injuries Cage suffered during their grueling three-way ladder match for the AEW World Tag Team Championships against The Young Bucks at AEW All Out. According to Copeland, Cage suffered a punctured lung and broke three ribs just five minutes into the bout. Despite the severity of the injuries, Cage finished the match out of sheer work ethic and dedication to the fans. Seeing someone so close to him suffer such a severe health scare played a major role in Copeland's decision to openly set a timeline for his retirement.

With 2027 now officially set as the end of his illustrious career, Copeland made it clear that he still has one major goal left to achieve before hanging up his boots for good, which is capturing the AEW World Championship. This upcoming championship pursuit adds massive stakes to his appearance in the Clockwork Carnage match. Copeland has previously crossed off items on his bucket list like competing at Arena Mexico, Wembley Stadium, and the former Maple Leaf Gardens, having viewed the last six years as a gift after returning from a severe neck injury that sidelined him from 2011 to 2020. Before retiring, he still hopes to accomplish goals like wrestling at the Tokyo Dome, possibly at Wrestle Kingdom 21 in January 2027, and facing dream opponents like Kenny Omega, Swerve Strickland, and Kyle Fletcher in one-on-one matches. While he has not decided what he wants to do in post-retirement life, he has mentioned being open to a conversation with Tony Khan about a possible backstage role in AEW.

Before Copeland could finish addressing his future on Dynamite, his segment was abruptly interrupted by Don Callis and Brian Cage. Callis mocked Copeland's retirement announcement, claiming he would not even make it to see 2027 or even his match at WrestleDream this weekend. The verbal confrontation quickly turned physical when other members of the Don Callis Family, including Kevin Knight and Lance Archer, ambushed Copeland from behind. The violent attack was eventually thwarted when Konosuke Takeshita and members of The Conglomeration rushed out to make the save, forcing the attackers to retreat and leaving the wrestling world to digest the monumental news of Copeland's approaching retirement.

🔥 FTR and Motor City Machine Guns best-of-five series at AEW WrestleDream

FTR and the Motor City Machine Guns are ready to settle things in the ring after agreeing to a best-of-five series that will begin at AEW WrestleDream. The two top tag teams came face-to-face during an episode of AEW Dynamite, a segment that began with Tony Schiavone introducing FTR and their manager, Stokely Hathaway. Dax Harwood took the microphone and explained how much winning tag team gold meant to him, stating that his main goal was to become a champion again. He then called out the AEW World Tag Team Champions, the Young Bucks, hoping to gain another chance to establish himself and Cash Wheeler as the number one contenders. Harwood justified the callout by noting that FTR has beaten the Young Bucks three out of the five times they have wrestled.

However, Harwood did not get the response he expected, as Chris Sabin and Alex Shelley of the Motor City Machine Guns came out with a different challenge in mind. Chris Sabin acknowledged FTR's achievements over the years and the strength of AEW's tag team division, but argued that past success did not automatically make a team the best in the division today. He and Shelley wanted to prove they were the best team by facing FTR directly. Alex Shelley took things further by bringing up FTR's rivalry with the Young Bucks. Shelley pointed out FTR's three-out-of-five record against the champions and questioned what it would mean if the Motor City Machine Guns could achieve the exact same result against Harwood and Wheeler. His argument was that a best-of-five series would give both teams several chances to prove themselves instead of leaving the debate to a single match.

Shelley officially proposed the series and even suggested that the first match take place right away on the show. However, Stokely Hathaway refused to let the series begin right then, arguing that a matchup this big deserved a bigger stage. He agreed to the best-of-five series and announced that the first match would take place at WrestleDream. The segment ended as both teams shook hands to cement the arrangement. This rivalry marks a major moment for Shelley and Sabin, who have had a limited presence since signing with AEW earlier this year in August. Until they signed, their sole AEW appearance and the only time the two teams shared the ring was at AEW All Out 2022, when FTR and Wardlow defeated MCMG and Jay Lethal in a trios match. This upcoming encounter at WrestleDream will serve as the first time MCMG faces off against FTR in a traditional two-on-two setting, kicking off what promises to be a definitive series for the tag team division.

🔥 Samoa Joe comments on losses of PAC and Andy Williams and AEW talent potential

In a recent interview, AEW star Samoa Joe spoke candidly about the atmosphere backstage following the tragic and untimely losses of Andy The Butcher Williams and Pac, who passed away just weeks apart from each other. Joe shared how the locker room has dealt with grieving both men so close together, noting that there is no real established protocol beyond how friends and loved ones mourn. He emphasized that in times like this, the roster tries to be there for each other, available for late night support and conversations. Joe feels the profound impact of Williams and Pac resonates far beyond AEW, reflecting the meaningful legacies both men left during their careers. He also noted that continuing to work has helped the locker room process the loss, expressing gratitude that they have the opportunity to keep the show going. AEW honored both wrestlers with tribute shows featuring ten bell salutes, with Williams memory capped off by a 12 man battle royal on AEW Collision, and Pac tribute anchored by a main event 10 man tag team match on AEW Dynamite featuring his former Death Riders stablemates against The Don Callis Family. Joe praised AEW President Tony Khan for his sensitivity toward the talent and highlighted the strong solidarity within the locker room, stressing that these moments remind everyone that life is precious and that people should express their love and care for others whenever they get the opportunity.

Shifting focus to the current roster, Joe was asked which performers fans should pay closer attention to and who might not be receiving enough recognition. He immediately pointed to Queen Aminata and Trish Adora on the women side, stating that both possess a ton of unrealized potential and that their best performances are still ahead of them. Queen Aminata is a regular across AEW and Ring of Honor television, with a notable recent Women World title match against Mercedes Mone on AEW Dynamite. Trish Adora, who has been with AEW since 2021, primarily features on ROH programming while remaining an active presence. On the men side, Joe singled out Action Andretti as someone who could become a major force if given more opportunities and prominent spots. Andretti is also a regular across both AEW and ROH brands, seeing frequent action on the latter.

Joe also reflected on the broader evolution of AEW since he joined the company, describing the current landscape as an era of new faces. He noted that a new generation of younger wrestlers who previously were not in the spotlight or the main event picture are now forcing themselves into those positions, and rightfully so. Joe expressed his excitement and joy in witnessing this progression firsthand, viewing the emergence of new talent as one of the most rewarding aspects of his time with the promotion. Additionally, the veteran addressed the strong public response to a viral video featuring a conversation about his decision to lean into his faith. Acknowledging the varying reactions, Joe remarked that people will always feel strongly about worldly statements and noted that in America, people have free speech. Looking ahead, Samoa Joe is scheduled to compete in the first ever Clockwork Carnage match for the AEW World Championship at the upcoming AEW WrestleDream 2026 pay per view in Orlando, Florida, alongside champion Will Ospreay, Speedball Mike Bailey, and other qualifiers.

🔥 Christian Cage injury updates from AEW All Out

Former AEW multi-division champion Christian Cage is currently out of action recovering from significant injuries suffered during the AEW All Out pay-per-view. The extent of the damage remained private until Adam Copeland addressed the situation on a special episode of AEW Dynamite and Collision.

According to Copeland, Cage sustained three broken ribs, with one rib fractured in three separate places, alongside a collapsed lung. These severe injuries occurred only five minutes into the three-way ladder match for the AEW World Tag Team Championships when Cage was violently thrown into a ladder. Despite the excruciating pain and the severity of the damage, Cage somehow managed to continue competing, remaining heavily involved throughout the contest and even climbing to the top of a ladder in the closing stages to reach for the titles.

Copeland heavily praised his longtime friend and partner's incredible determination, work ethic, and unwavering commitment to the fans, noting that Cage powered through the ordeal simply to finish the match.

Witnessing the physical toll and the reality of what Cage endured forced Copeland to reflect on his own career in professional wrestling. During the same emotional address, which followed his victory over Josh Alexander to qualify for the Clockwork Carnage match at WrestleDream, Copeland announced that 2027 will be his final year as an active in-ring performer. He also emphasized that winning the AEW World Championship remains a major goal before he hangs up his boots.

Copeland's heartfelt promo was eventually interrupted by Don Callis, who questioned whether Copeland would even make it to WrestleDream. Members of the Don Callis Family then attacked Copeland from behind, turning the poignant segment into a physical brawl.

While AEW and official sources have not yet announced a recovery timetable for the 52-year-old Cage, fans and colleagues continue to send their well wishes for a speedy recovery as both veterans face major challenges ahead in their respective careers.

Jon Moxley comments on pain and recent AEW TV pinfall loss

Jon Moxley suffered an unexpected setback on the October 14 episode of AEW Dynamite, leaving the Death Riders with plenty to reconsider ahead of WrestleDream. The AEW Continental Champion entered the opening trios match alongside Gabe Kidd and Daniel Garcia to face Mark Davis, Jake Doyle, and Wardlow of the Don Callis Family. The contest featured heavy strikes, power moves, and several shifts in control, with Wardlow proving a difficult opponent for the Death Riders to handle. Moxley eventually took control and exchanged hard shots with Davis, following up with headbutts and a cutter. The Death Riders appeared to have the upper hand, but Wardlow and Doyle interrupted a pin attempt, keeping their team in contention. The finish came when Davis connected with a devastating piledriver on Moxley, securing the victory for the Don Callis Family. The result marked Moxley is first pinfall loss on AEW television since April, when Will Ospreay pinned him during an eight-man tag team match.

The defeat seemed to be too much for Moxley to process. During a backstage interview with his wife Renee Paquette at the end of the show, he started throwing objects and smashing wooden pallets to let out his frustration before dropping to one knee. Paquette rushed over to check on her husband, asking what was wrong. Moxley confessed that he was in a great deal of pain and struggled to explain what kind, stating that he could no longer tell the difference between physical and emotional pain. Marina Shafir stepped in to help her stablemate, getting down beside him, lifting his face, and telling him to lock in and that the only way out is through. Moxley took her words to heart, repeated the word through, and eventually regained enough composure to leave with the group.

This emotional moment comes after a tough run for Moxley in the ring, including a previous loss to Will Ospreay at All Out. Outside the storyline, Moxley has also been mourning the sudden death of his Death Rider stablemate, PAC, making the whole situation another difficult development for the faction during an already challenging period. For Mark Davis, the victory offers an opportunity to build confidence before challenging one of AEW is toughest competitors. The timing makes the defeat particularly significant as Moxley prepares to defend the AEW Continental Championship against Davis at WrestleDream. The challenger has landed a meaningful blow before the title match, while Moxley must regroup before putting his championship on the line, as the advantage has officially shifted.

The match was set up during a recent episode of television after Wayne came to the ring to celebrate his recent victory over the veteran. Wayne leaned all the way into the moment, repeatedly bragging that he beat Jericho while pyro went off around him. He even rolled a celebratory video package set to Superman It Is Not Easy by Five for Fighting.

That is when Jericho interrupted, revealing that he knew Wayne had a video package coming and had prepared one of his own.

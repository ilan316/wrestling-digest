🔶 AEW News

Mick Foley compares AEW and WWE storytelling

While speaking to Kofi Kingston on The Mick and Kenny Show, WWE Hall of Famer Mick Foley offered his perspective on the differences in storytelling between AEW and WWE after taking a break from following the industry. Foley admitted that the AEW product ended up speaking more to him than WWE, largely due to how the company treats its entire roster rather than just focusing on a handful of top stars.

One of the things that immediately caught Foley's attention was how AEW manages the middle of the card. He expressed amazement at how they make those matches and performers meaningful. When he spoke with AEW President Tony Khan about it, Foley expressed his confusion over how they pull it off, noting that everyone who goes out there plays a specific role and maintains a clear sense of importance.

While comparing this to other promotions, Foley pointed out that elsewhere, a rigid group of about ten main guys occupies the top spots, while everyone else can sometimes feel like an afterthought. Though he made it clear he did not want to disparage anyone or knock WWE, he reiterated his belief that AEW simply does a better job of making almost everyone on the roster feel relevant. Because of this depth, Foley believes that on any given night, almost anyone on the roster could realistically step up and challenge for the AEW world title, calling the overall execution a marvel.

Foley also specifically highlighted AEW's commitment to its tag team division. He noted that in the broader wrestling landscape, dedicated tag teams are frequently overlooked because promotions often just throw two established singles stars together and treat them as a superior team. In contrast, Foley praised AEW for taking the opposite approach by placing genuine value and respect on established tag teams, pushing them as every bit as important as the singles division. Ultimately, Foley's praise was not about a single wrestler or isolated storyline, but rather AEW's consistent ability to make a much larger portion of its locker room feel essential from week to week.

Samoa Joe praises Tony Khan's response to AEW tragedies

AEW has been going through a rough patch recently, but Samoa Joe believes Tony Khan has responded to the difficult times with great sensitivity. In the past month, AEW has hosted several tribute episodes dedicated to Rebel, Butcher, and PAC. Despite all the sad occurrences and tragedies, the former World Champion is full of praise for the company president's reaction to these events.

Speaking to TV Insider, Joe discussed how the roster deals with loss. The truth of it is there is no real established protocol beyond how anybody grieves amongst friends and loved ones. He noted that in times like this, they try to be there for each other and remain available for that late-night call if someone needs to talk or work through something, adding that everyone is simply more sensitive to these matters during such difficult periods.

Joe emphasized that the impact of these losses extended far beyond a single promotion. Obviously, the loss of Ben, referring to PAC, and Andy Butcher was felt far and beyond even AEW. Almost every locker room in the world felt it because they made an impact in the many places that they worked.

Reflecting on the wrestling industry as a whole, Joe shared that despite the traditional show must go on mentality, the roster remains grateful to even have the opportunity of having the show go on. He believes that starting such endeavors with gratitude usually leads to something pretty spectacular in the end. He specifically highlighted leadership, stating that Tony Khan is very admirable in that he is very sensitive to these types of situations and understands how much performers mean to each other, especially over the breadth of their careers and the journey that is what they do.

Joe also stressed that this feeling of solidarity extends beyond his home promotion, noting it would be selfish to say it is just AEW. He believes the entire wrestling industry understands the great loss endured over the past months, leading everyone to band together. Concluding his thoughts, Joe stated that these moments make performers cherish life more and understand that our time on this earth is precious, reminding everyone that we should tell the people that we care about them and we love them when we get the opportunity.

Kevin Knight discusses Steven Borden's injury at AEW All Out

Kevin Knight has opened up about the frightening moment Steven Borden suffered an injury during AEW All Out 2026, revealing that he stayed with the young wrestler at the hospital overnight. The incident occurred during a tag team match that ended prematurely after a dangerous mid-air collision went wrong. Borden teamed with Darby Allin against Knight and Kyle Fletcher at the pay-per-view. During the match, Knight and Borden attempted a springboard clothesline that they had discussed and rehearsed beforehand. However, Borden struck the back of his head on the mat during the landing, prompting officials to stop the contest and arrange medical assistance.

Speaking to ClutchPoints, Knight explained why he went to the hospital with Borden after the incident. As a human, it was just checking on him, making sure he is okay, Knight said. You know, I spent the night at the hospital with him. He was perfectly fine. It was just a scary bump, a scary risk. Knight acknowledged the situation was difficult, particularly because he was directly involved in the spot. He also stressed that the move had been planned, but accidents can happen even when wrestlers prepare carefully.

We talked about it and everything. We walked it through, he explained. But, just like I said, just with anything, stepping through the ropes, walking on the stairs, hitting the ropes, taking any kind of bump, you are risking your life anytime you are doing any of those things. Knight described the incident as a freak accident and expressed confidence in Borden’s ability to return. Steve is a trooper, so I am sure he will come back better than ever, he added.

The two also spent time talking at the hospital about how they could learn from the experience and move forward. I was in the hospital room with him all night, Knight said. He explained that they discussed finding opportunities for growth despite the setback, adding that wrestlers must pivot and learn from them and grow.

Borden’s return date remains unknown, but Darby Allin has indicated that he will wrestle again. For now, Knight’s account highlights the risks behind the spectacle and the importance of looking after one another when a planned spot takes an unexpected turn.

🔄 המשך AEW talent booked in CMLL tag match with Tessa Blanchard

Following last week's teases, the full details for Dr. Britt Baker's upcoming CMLL debut have been officially locked in. Baker will team with Persephone this Friday at Arena Mexico to face the duo of Thunder Rosa and Tessa Blanchard. The booking reunites Baker and Blanchard, who previously squared off at the inaugural All In event in 2018. Blanchard has been heavily featured in CMLL recently, including an ongoing rivalry with Persephone over the promotion's women's championship.

The high-profile tag match serves as a major attraction on an entirely female-wrestler card for Viernes Espectacular, celebrating the Month of the Amazonas. The event also boasts an international flavor with appearances by Stardom talents AZM, Miyu Amasaki, and Momo Watanabe, alongside Scottish wrestler Emersyn Jayne. Additionally, the night will feature the CMLL Campeonato Universal de Amazonas tournament final between Olympia and Skadi, rounding out a loaded lineup for the historic venue.

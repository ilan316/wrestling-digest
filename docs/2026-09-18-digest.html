🔥 Jeff Jarrett Set for Semi-Regular Runs in CMLL

AEW veteran Jeff Jarrett is set to make his CMLL in-ring debut against Blue Panther at the promotion's upcoming anniversary show at Arena Mexico. The expansion of the card to a larger lineup included the surprising addition of Jarrett, which led some fans to express frustration that a non-CMLL wrestler was given a spot while some regular performers were left off. While Jarrett is currently being presented for the major event as a one-night attraction, reports suggest Friday's match could be the first chapter rather than the whole story.

According to reports, Jarrett is very interested in actually not doing a one and done appearance. Instead, the WWE Hall of Famer reportedly wants to become a semi-regular performer while remaining under contract with AEW. The idea would not involve Jarrett working in Mexico every single week, but rather occasionally making appearances, developing programs, and eventually working toward larger matches. Sources noted that Jarrett hopes for an arrangement similar to what Johnny TV did earlier this year, except bigger, though it may not ultimately happen. Fans may recall Johnny TV's short but memorable run under the name Johnny Consejo, which culminated in a high-profile hair versus hair match against Angel de Oro where Johnny lost and was shaved bald.

Although this marks Jarrett's first time working with CMLL, he is no stranger to lucha libre. He previously worked with rival promotion AAA starting in 2004, where he had multiple stints and won the AAA Mega Championship twice. During his last full time run in 2018, Jarrett developed a feud with lucha legend Dr. Wagner Jr. that built to a hair versus hair match at Heroes Immortales XII, which Wagner won. More recently, Jarrett has worked alongside CMLL talent at AEW events, including a dark match at AEW Grand Slam Mexico where he teamed with Brillante Jr., Mascara Dorada, Orange Cassidy, and Roderick Strong to defeat Nick Wayne, The Dogs, and The Swirl. For now, Jarrett's immediate focus remains on his upcoming match against Blue Panther, and whether that single bell rings in a longer CMLL chapter remains to be seen.

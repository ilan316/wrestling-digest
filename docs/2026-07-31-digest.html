🔥 Motor City Machine Guns Sign with AEW

The Motor City Machine Guns are officially All Elite. After months of speculation following their WWE release, Alex Shelley and Chris Sabin made their highly anticipated debut on the special live Thursday episode of AEW Collision from the Masonic Temple Theatre in Detroit, Michigan. The hometown heroes received a massive ovation as they opened the show and confirmed their signing with the company, providing AEW with one of its biggest acquisitions of 2026.

The duo's arrival was perfectly timed for AEW's tag team division, with All In just around the corner and many top teams expected to return or debut in the coming weeks. When the familiar entrance music and video of the Motor City Machine Guns played, the crowd erupted in cheers as Sabin and Shelley walked out to thunderous hometown support.

During their opening segment, Sabin took the microphone first and asked the Detroit crowd if they really thought the Motor City Machine Guns wouldn't show up in their hometown. He expressed that there is no place they would rather be than in the greatest city in civilization, referencing their two decades representing Detroit. Sabin emphasized that this is where the best wrestle, declaring that the duo has been proud to represent Detroit for over twenty years and are excited to finally be in AEW.

Shelley then grabbed the microphone and said the night felt like a homecoming in more ways than one. Not only were they back in Detroit, but they were also starting a new chapter in their careers. He officially announced that the Motor City Machine Guns were now "All Elite," and the famous AEW "All Elite" graphic appeared on the big screens, officially confirming their signing.

AEW President Tony Khan confirmed the signing on social media shortly after the segment, posting "It's official TONIGHT: Chris Sabin and Alex Shelley, Motor City Machine Guns are ALL ELITE! Thank you all watching THURSDAY AEW Collision live on TNT and HBO Max tonight, RIGHT NOW!"

The Motor City Machine Guns' journey to AEW was notable for its winding path. After leaving TNA in 2024, both WWE and AEW were interested in signing them. Many expected them to join AEW at that time, but they ultimately signed with WWE in the fall of 2024. However, their time in WWE did not last long.

The duo was released by WWE in late April 2026 during the company's post-WrestleMania releases. They were former WWE Tag Team Champions, holding the titles for 42 days and defending them twice before losing to Johnny Gargano and Tommaso Ciampa. They had not wrestled since the Andre the Giant battle royal on the SmackDown before WrestleMania. After completing the standard 90-day non-compete period, fans immediately began expecting the Motor City Machine Guns to show up in AEW, and their appearance on this Thursday episode confirmed those expectations.

This is not technically the Motor City Machine Guns' first appearance in AEW. They previously appeared at All Out in 2022, when they teamed with Jay Lethal in a losing effort against FTR and Wardlow. However, this marks their first appearance as officially contracted talent for the company.

The Motor City Machine Guns have held tag team championships in virtually every major promotion they have worked for. In addition to their WWE Tag Team Championship, they are former ROH Tag Team Champions, three-time TNA Tag Team Champions, NJPW Junior Heavyweight Tag Team Champions, NJPW Strong Openweight Tag Team Champions, and GCW Tag Team Champions. They will be looking to add AEW World Tag Team Championship gold to that impressive collection.

Later in the episode, Shelley and Sabin's impact was immediately felt. Following an eight-man tag match involving the Bang Bang Gang, the newly signed Motor City Machine Guns ran down to the ring with steel chairs to save Juice Robinson, Ace Austin, and The Gunns from a post-match attack by the Death Riders and The Dogs. After the dust settled, the Motor City Machine Guns shook hands with their new allies, an angle that could very well lead to their first match as a contracted AEW team.

The signing of the Motor City Machine Guns represents a significant boost to AEW's tag team division at a critical time. With All In at Wembley Stadium in London approaching, the AEW World Tag Team Championship will be on the line when Adam Copeland and Christian Cage defend against the Young Bucks. The arrival of the Motor City Machine Guns, combined with the expected debut of fellow released WWE stars the New Day and the anticipated return of FTR who have been out of action, is poised to transform the competitive landscape of the tag team scene in AEW.

🔥 AEW Continental Cup Tournament

AEW Continental Champion Jon Moxley sparked curiosity during the July 29 episode of Dynamite when he referenced the "Continental Cup" in a promo without elaboration, leaving fans and observers wondering what he meant or if he had misspoken about the Continental Classic. The confusion was cleared up on the following night when Tony Schiavone made an official announcement on Collision, detailing a brand-new tournament format that will take place this summer.

The Continental Cup will be a 16-man, single-elimination tournament with a unique twist. Rather than utilizing a traditional bracket, all matchups will be determined through random draws, a format inspired by England's historic FA Cup. The tournament will kick off on the August 8 episode of Collision and culminate at AEW All In London on August 30 at Wembley Stadium. Each match will be contested under Continental Rules, which means a 20-minute time limit and no seconds permitted at ringside.

The stakes could not be higher. The winner of the Continental Cup will not only claim the new Continental Cup trophy but will also leave Wembley Stadium as the AEW Continental Champion. This is particularly significant for reigning champion Jon Moxley, who must win the entire tournament if he hopes to retain his title. Moxley, the third Continental Champion in AEW history following inaugural champion Eddie Kingston and Kazuchika Okada, won the title after winning last year's tournament by defeating Okada in the finals at Worlds End. He has since defended the championship seven times. As the current champion, Moxley has automatically been entered into the tournament field. Should Moxley be eliminated before the finals, the two remaining finalists will compete for the vacant Continental Championship at All In.

The announcement immediately drew attention from an unexpected quarter. Nigel McGuinness, who has spent the last decade-plus serving as an AEW commentator, took to the microphone to declare that he was throwing his name into the hat as a competitor. McGuinness, a former ROH World Champion, expressed his lifelong dream of competing in a cup final at Wembley Stadium, and referenced his years of watching the FA Cup. His announcement marks yet another chapter in his remarkable return to in-ring competition after more than a decade away from active wrestling. Since ending his 13-year retirement at All In 2024, McGuinness has stepped away from his commentary duties for select high-profile matches, competing against Bryan Danielson, Lee Moriarty, and FTR alongside Daniel Garcia.

Several other prominent AEW wrestlers have also confirmed their participation. Orange Cassidy, Kyle O'Reilly, and Roderick Strong, the now-former AEW World Trios Champions, all announced during a backstage segment that they had thrown their names into the hat. At the time of the Collision announcement, 11 spots remained to be filled in the 16-man tournament. The full field of competitors will be announced by Tony Khan and Tony Schiavone via AEW's YouTube channel on Monday.

With random pairings determining each matchup, the tournament has the potential to create compelling storylines and unexpected encounters. Every draw could result in a main event-caliber match, and luck may prove to be as dangerous as any finishing move. The tournament begins next week on Collision with the first round of matches and will continue until the finals at Wembley Stadium, where the champion and the Continental Cup will be crowned on August 30.

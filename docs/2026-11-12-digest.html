🔥 Tay Melo Parts Ways With AEW and Removed From Roster Page

Tay Melo has officially parted ways with All Elite Wrestling, bringing an end to her nearly six-year run with the promotion. As of November 11, Melo has been granted her release from the company and has been removed from the official AEW roster page. The departure had been in the works for several months following discussions about her future, though neither side initially issued a formal announcement confirming the exit until the website update.

The situation traces back to the summer, when AEW President Tony Khan revealed during a media call that Melo had personally requested her release. According to Khan, the request came after her June 6 appearance on AEW Collision, where she and Anna Jay wrestled Divine Dominion. Melo approached him backstage and expressed a desire to step away from both the company and professional wrestling entirely. Khan admitted the request caught him off guard, but noted they had a positive conversation and that he intended to grant her release. He stated that he had not booked her since that talk, emphasizing that there was no animosity and recounting that Melo told him she loved AEW and the people there.

Melo began her wrestling career in WWE, competing in NXT under the ring name Tay Conti for four years before being released in early 2020 amid pandemic-related cuts. She joined AEW later that year in August and was officially signed in September. During her tenure, she became a recognizable fixture of the women's division, notably partnering with Anna Jay to form the popular duo TayJay and challenging Hikaru Shida for the AEW Women's World Championship. Her personal life also intertwined with the company as she married fellow wrestler Sammy Guevara in August 2022 and gave birth to their daughter, Luna, in November 2023.

Although Khan initially stated that Melo wanted to step away from the industry, speculation about her status grew in September when fans noticed she had removed the word wrestler from her Instagram biography, leading some to believe she had ended her in-ring career. When a follower thanked her for her career under the assumption she had retired, Melo quickly dispelled the rumor by clarifying that she had not retired. Melo further signaled her ongoing passion for the business by participating in World Series Wrestling events in Australia alongside her husband Sammy Guevara, noting that the experience and watching the show as a fan reminded her why she loves wrestling. With her release now finalized and her removal from the AEW website complete, Melo is a free agent able to explore opportunities elsewhere in the wrestling industry, though she has not yet publicly announced her next destination or a timetable for an in-ring return.

AEW Dynamite/Collision November 11 Review

AEW Dynamite collided with its sister program Collision on Wednesday night for the final broadcast before Saturday's Full Gear pay per view. The show was packed with title matches, title challenges, and plenty of table setting for the big night. The writing staff sat with their feelings and came up with a few things they really liked, as well as a few things they very much did not. The main event of Collision divided the staff, as the match itself earned praise from one writer, while Queen Aminata's overall handling gave another writer pause. There were also dizzying highs like the National Title match, and worrying lows like the awkward non finish to New Level versus Brodido.

One of the major elements that drew criticism was another unnecessary squash match. If there is one thing you can bank on when it comes to any AEW show, it is a squash match. AEW will throw out these random squash matches as a way to justify getting someone on television right before they go and wrestle on a pay per view or a marquee TV match. It is a classic formula used back in the territories, and it has worked in AEW before, with some of the most famous matches in company history being squashes, but this was not one of them. Wardlow had a squash match against Nick Comoroto that felt unneeded over three hours into a weekly wrestling show, but the bigger issue was Persephone. Persephone has been fuming about losing her AEW TBS Championship to Queen Aminata and still believes the title is hers, resulting in her needing to get some frustrations out on a jobber. It is an understandable thing to do, and it keeps Persephone strong with a dominant performance, but it raises questions when compared to the end of the show.

Aminata beats Hyan and Skye Blue to retain her title later in the evening. Persephone then comes out to clean house, only for Maya World to show up, which makes you ask why they had to feed a jobber to Persephone two hours prior. You can do one without the other and still have it work, to the point where the show would not need to be nearly four hours long. Persephone came off more as a heel in the closing segment by attacking Aminata from behind, instead of eating poor Gypsy Mac alive in just a few minutes, which ultimately makes her look very cool because Persephone is just good at this whole wrestling thing. Bookers should have dropped the squash and perhaps had Dr. Britt Baker come out at the end as a surprise before Persephone made her appearance by attacking the champion, flowing naturally into Maya World showing up for the three way at Full Gear.

On the positive side, fans and staff loved Speedball Mike Bailey crushing his National title attempt, setting up Tommaso Ciampa versus Ilja Dragunov next. It is time AEW puts some singles gold on Speedball Mike Bailey, as any time he gets a title shot, he absolutely knocks it out of the park. His match against Tommaso Ciampa for the AEW National Championship was no different. He did not get the victory, sadly, as Ciampa was seemingly always meant to face Ilja Dragunov at Full Gear, and the powers that be correctly felt the bout needed a championship attached to it.

Queen Aminata Still AEW TBS Champion After Chaotic Three-Way Match

Just two weeks after winning the AEW TBS Championship from Persephone, Queen Aminata already had the deck stacked against her in her first defense as she took on both Skye Blue and Hyan on the November 11 episode of AEW Collision. However, the champion overcame the odds and retained her title, while also finding out who she will be facing at AEW Full Gear 2026.

After all three women tried to take an early advantage, it was Hyan who came out on top with a Crossbody on her opponents, before delivering a pair of running knees on Skye and Aminata, and finishing off by dumping Skye on to Aminata as the match entered the commercial break. During the break, Skye got back into things by isolating Hyan in the ring, though it did become a Handicap match for a brief moment as both Hyan and Aminata teamed up on Skye. Aminata used her strength to take Skye over with a Suplex, but Skye fired back and took control of things once more.

Coming out of the break, Aminata delivered a pair of Atomic Drops to the challengers, continuing to use her strength to her advantage. Skye tried to use her speed to get back into things but she was given a Falcon Arrow by Hyan, who herself was given a PK by the champion while trying to pin Skye. On the outside, Aminata took Hyan out with Shotgun Dropkick off the apron, but while the referee's back was turned, Skye threw a chair at Aminata's head and tried to win things in the ring. Skye looked for the Code Blue on Aminata but both women were taken out by a Spear from Hyan, who got Skye up in the Fireman's Carry only to be cut off. Skye hit the Code Blue on Hyan for a near fall as the pin was broken up by a Diving Foot Stomp by Aminata.

With Hyan out of the equation, Aminata hit Skye with the Off With her Head running kick to get the win and make her first successful defense of the TBS Title. After the match, Dr. Britt Baker D.M.D. came to ringside, but she was a distraction for Persephone, who ran down to attack Aminata while claiming that the TBS Title is hers. Maya World would run out afterwards to make the save for both Aminata and Hyan, with AEW President Tony Khan making a TBS Title match official for the Tailgate Brawl portion of Full Gear that will see Aminata defend her title against Persephone and Maya World.

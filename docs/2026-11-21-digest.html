🔥 ROH Final Battle and Women's TV Title Updates

The card for Ring of Honor Final Battle is rapidly coming together following a series of major announcements regarding championships and tournament brackets. Tony Khan revealed that ROH World Champion Bandido will defend his title in a Survival of the Fittest multi-man elimination match at the December 4 event at the Boeing Center at Tech Port in San Antonio. Bandido successfully defended the World Title in a similar Survival of the Fittest match at Final Battle last year. Mark Davis of the Don Callis Family became the first wrestler to qualify for the upcoming match by defeating Darius Martin on a special television episode titled Fortnight to Final Battle, planting Martin with a gutwrench piledriver for the victory. Additional qualifying matches will take place on upcoming episodes of Ring of Honor television.

The title picture has also seen significant turbulence regarding the ROH Women's World Television Championship. Tony Khan delivered the news that Red Velvet is not medically cleared to compete at Final Battle, resulting in the championship being vacated. Velvet was running hot as a fighting champion in her second reign, having previously carried the TV title for 481 days before losing to Mercedes Mone. Velvet got the gold back from Ultimo Mone at last year's Final Battle event, and her current reign will officially end at 349 days. The promotion has wished her the best on a swift and healthy recovery.

To crown a new champion, an eight-woman tournament was established with the finals scheduled to take place at Final Battle. The opening-round quarterfinal matchups feature Zayda Steel versus Robyn Renegade, Isla Dawn versus Viva Van, Trish Adora versus Dani Luna, and Vert Vixen versus Mina Shirakawa. During the Fortnight to Final Battle special, the first two quarterfinal bouts took place as Isla Dawn defeated Viva Van and Zayda Steel defeated Robyn Renegade in the main event. Dawn will now face Steel in the tournament semifinals.

Several other matches have been officially confirmed for the Final Battle lineup. In the women's division, ROH Women's Champion Athena will defend her title against Hyan. ROH Women's Pure Champion Deonna Purrazzo will defend her title against Emi Sakura in a rematch from last month. The ROH Television Championship will be contested in a three-way match with champion Action Andretti defending against Adam Priest and El Phantasmo.

Furthermore, the ROH Tag Team titles will be defended at the event, though the official participants are still being determined. Before Final Battle, current champions Mistico and Mascara Dorada will defend the titles against Don Callis Family members Rocky Romero and Volador Jr. at Arena Mexico during an upcoming Consejo Mundial de Lucha Libre event, with the winners advancing directly to Final Battle.

🔥 GTA VI Wrestling Content and AEW Producer Involvement

Grand Theft Auto VI has officially arrived, bringing an unexpected crossover with the world of professional wrestling both on screen and behind the scenes. Alongside the game's massive launch, fans have discovered an in-game wrestling challenge set up in the open world. Located in Hamlet, an area southwest of Vice City, players can find a makeshift wrestling venue called Backyard American Mania. By knocking on a trailer door, players can trigger a quick-time wrestling match against an opponent known as The Gentrifier. The encounter requires players to reverse several attacks, including a Full Nelson, a facelock, and a suplex. The main challenge comes when The Gentrifier climbs the turnbuckle to attempt Jeff Hardy’s iconic Swanton Bomb, which players must counter before climbing the ropes themselves to finish the match with a flying elbow drop. Defeating The Gentrifier grants players access to his trailer, where they can collect items including a Luchador mask used to conceal their identity, alongside a trading card that can be sold at a pawn shop for extra cash.

The wrestling connection to the game goes even deeper into the real-world industry. AEW producer Kevin Matthew McDonald recently revealed that he played a behind-the-scenes role in the development of Grand Theft Auto VI. McDonald took to Instagram to announce that he spent portions of the last five years working a handful of days doing motion capture for Rockstar Games. Over the course of the project, he brought several different characters to life, ranging from security guards and bouncers to street thugs and MMA fighters. McDonald expressed his excitement about being part of such a historic project, writing that it is pretty wild to think about just how massive the project is and thanking stunt coordinators Manny Siverio and Chris Colombo for the opportunity. Before transitioning into his backstage producer role with AEW in 2023, McDonald enjoyed a two-decade in-ring career competing for independent promotions, TNA Wrestling as KM, and occasionally for AEW and Ring of Honor.

Back in the real world, the release of Grand Theft Auto VI is already causing minor domestic humor for other AEW stars. Swerve Strickland found himself getting hilariously roasted by his partner over the amount of time he is spending playing the game and interacting with its main character, Lucia. In a video uploaded to Instagram, Swerve was handed a massive plate of food before his partner abruptly told him to have Lucia cook his damn dinner instead, pointing out that the character had just gotten out of prison in the game. Swerve immediately defended Lucia by bringing up her current situation in the storyline, proving that Grand Theft Auto VI has already created some stiff competition for his attention at home.

🔥 AEW Announces Toni Storm Project

All Elite Wrestling has announced a new project involving Toni Storm as the promotion continues to build intrigue around the former Women's World Champion's mysterious absence. Storm has been absent from programming for several months, with the promotion keeping the circumstances surrounding her disappearance part of an ongoing storyline. Her absence has also generated speculation about when the four-time Women's World Champion will resume her wrestling career.

Storm last competed at AEW Revolution on March 15, 2026, where she defeated Marina Shafir. She was subsequently scheduled to face Shafir again in a No Holds Barred match on the March 18 episode of Dynamite. However, AEW pulled Storm from the scheduled match after a backstage segment showed her laid out in her locker room. Mina Shirakawa replaced her and went on to defeat Shafir, while the mystery surrounding the attack remained unresolved. Through the storyline, it was indicated that Storm had been attacked, launching an investigation that eventually went cold.

AEW announced on social media that The Toni Storm Tapes, a five-part series, will premiere on Friday, November 27, at 10 a.m. ET on the company YouTube channel and MyAEW. The social media announcement noted that since March 18, 2026, no one has seen or heard from Toni Storm until now, introducing the project featuring five mysterious tapes from an indeterminate past. The series will explore five mysterious cassette recordings attributed to Storm, which are labeled Seminar, Cinema, Seance, Death, and Mundanities, offering different glimpses into her life and the character she portrayed during her time away from the ring.

Renee Paquette narrates the announcement, explaining that it was long rumored that Toni had a recording device inside her purse capturing every conversation, every meeting, and every moment. Paquette noted that those rumors are now true. In this five-series, viewers will delve into each tape to get a glimpse into her former Timeless life, ranging from routines to the salacious, and from wisdom to masterpieces and the overall mystery.

The physical tapes were initially distributed to members of the media at AEW WrestleDream in October before another mysterious delivery followed AEW Full Gear. Ahead of this announcement, AEW had been dropping various mysterious teasers related to Storm. While the announcement does not confirm Storm's in-ring return date, AEW President Tony Khan previously addressed her absence during the post-Full Gear media scrum, assuring fans that the former champion would eventually return to the promotion. Storm remains one of the most popular characters in all of professional wrestling thanks to her acclaimed Timeless gimmick.

Ruby Soho Teases AEW Return

Ruby Soho appears to be teasing a comeback to professional wrestling, and she will be sporting a dramatic new look if and when that time arrives. Soho last appeared on television on April 20, 2024, during an episode of AEW Rampage where she and partner Angelo Parker announced they were expecting their first child together. The happy couple has since given birth, and Soho has remained away from programming since that announcement.

However, her latest Instagram activity has fans wondering whether she could be preparing to step back into the spotlight. Soho recently shared a photo revealing her noticeably different appearance, accompanied by a mysterious caption consisting entirely of emojis. The post included several symbols associated with time, including a clock, hourglass, and alarm clock, alongside a bomb emoji and other cryptic imagery.

AEW Dynamite Viewership Ratings

AEW Dynamite on November 18, 2026, drew 727,000 viewers on TBS. This number is down slightly from the 742,000 viewers that the Dynamite portion of the three-hour episode drew on November 11. For that previous week, the entire three-hour block averaged 621,000 viewers.

Despite the decrease in total audience by 15,000 viewers, Wednesday's episode performed better in younger demographics. The show averaged a 0.19 rating in the key 18 to 49 demographic, which is up from the 0.16 rating the Dynamite portion drew the week prior.

When compared historically, the growth is even more apparent. During the same week in 2025, AEW Dynamite drew 491,000 viewers and a 0.1 demo rating on November 19. Compared to the November 18, 2026 numbers, that represents a year-over-year increase of 48.1 percent in viewership and a 90.0 percent increase in the demo rating.

These figures reflect only linear television viewership on TBS and do not include streaming or digital viewership data. The episode was also simulcast on HBO Max, though viewership information for that platform is not publicly available.

Digital interest remained high on YouTube, highlighted by the five most viewed videos from the episode. The segment featuring The New Level and Swerve Strickland getting asked to join The Don Callis Family led the way with 315,000 views. Kyle Fletcher interrupting Andrade El Idolo brought in 212,000 views, while footage checking if Don Callis was able to recruit Ilja Dragunov into the family drew 147,000 views. Rounding out the top clips, Jungle Jack Perry earning Samoa Joe's respect secured 128,000 views, and what Swerve Strickland said to Hangman Page pulled in 115,000 views as the company builds toward its next major live event.

Chris Jericho Comments on Sammy Guevara's Absence

When AEW first hit the pro wrestling scene, Sammy Guevara went from an independent standout to one of the Four Pillars that the Jacksonville-based promotion centered its attention toward. Now, it seems Guevara is off his well grounded post, which Chris Jericho can't comprehend. During a rapid-fire Q&A session with The Stunner, the wrestling veteran acknowledged Guevara's lack of on-air presence on both AEW and its sister promotion, ROH, and why his former Le Sex Gods tag team partner and Inner Circle affiliate should be given more television time.

I think he should be on TV more, Jericho said. He's got a great personality, too. He's got a very punchable face. I think we should at least try to do something with him.

It's been five months since The Spanish God was featured on any AEW programming and three months since appearing on ROH. His last AEW match was an unsuccessful World Trios Title challenge on the June 6 edition of AEW Collision. Back in July, Guevara addressed his absence, saying it wasn't his call, yet was hopeful that his return would happen soon. That same month, AEW President Tony Khan revealed that Guevara is still with the company, and that Guevara still admires the promotion he's called home since 2019.

Hoping to make that comeback soon, Guevara recently posted a photo of himself on X with the caption: Ready. #WorldChampSeason. He tagged both AEW and ROH in his post.

In addition to Guevara, the former Le Champion listed a few other stars he thinks deserves more television time. I think Mike Bailey is excellent, Jericho said. I think Kevin Knight is great. And also, Daddy Magic, Matt Menard, as well. That guy could be a cult hero.

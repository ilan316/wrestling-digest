🔥 Adam Copeland's bear encounter before AEW Full Gear

AEW star Adam Copeland had an unexpected and dangerous opponent to deal with shortly before his AEW Full Gear pay-per-view appearance on November 14 in Phoenix, Arizona. The veteran wrestler reportedly found himself in a frightening situation while walking his dogs through a residential neighborhood last week. Copeland heard a noise nearby and turned to discover a mother bear accompanied by her cubs approaching him.

The former WWE Superstar knew that when facing an approaching bear, the usual approach is to make yourself appear larger. However, he had both hands occupied holding his dogs leashes, making it difficult to follow that advice without potentially losing control of them. As the animal continued approaching, Copeland had to make a quick decision. He used his shin to deliver a kick directly underneath the bear and its jaw. The strike was enough to make the animal retreat, and Copeland managed to get away with his dogs without further incident.

The encounter was not without consequences for the AEW star. Copeland later told people that his shin was hurting badly and remained extremely sore from the impact of kicking the bear. That injury and discomfort may have affected his performance at Full Gear, where Copeland opened the pay-per-view against Kazuchika Okada for the AEW International Championship. Copeland tried to show resilience and tenacity, but he could not get past The Rainmaker, who retained his title by delivering a vicious beatdown after almost nineteen minutes of action.

Copeland faced multiple unexpected challenges leading into the event. In addition to the bear encounter, he experienced significant travel difficulties following a nationwide FAA flight shutdown on Friday. Copeland revealed that AEW President Tony Khan arranged private transportation for him after his commercial flights were canceled, allowing him to reach Phoenix in time for the show.

During the Full Gear weekend, Copeland also participated in an excellent backstage sit-down one-on-one discussion with fellow WWE Hall of Fame legend Mick Foley, adding another notable appearance to his schedule. While the details of the bear encounter are based on Copeland and his reported account rather than independently verified footage, the unusual incident adds a wild chapter to an already eventful week for the veteran wrestler. Copeland has previously announced that 2027 will be his last year competing in professional wrestling, and he has gone on to lose every match so far since that announcement.

🔥 AEW fires Cole Karter over ROH absence

Cole Karter has reportedly been released from All Elite Wrestling, with new details emerging about the circumstances surrounding his departure after approximately four years with the company. According to reporting, the 26-year-old wrestler was fired several weeks ago, although news of his exit has only recently surfaced and became a topic of conversation among wrestlers during the recent Chris Jericho Cruise television tapings.

Karter had been absent from in-ring competition for several months and was primarily featured on Ring of Honor programming during his tenure. Before that, he was in WWE for a year working under the NXT brand, where he competed under the ring name Troy Donovan or Troy Two Dimes as part of The D'Angelo Family led by Tony D'Angelo. His 2022 exit from WWE was reportedly due to a policy issue or a wellness policy violation.

Following his WWE exit, Karter joined AEW in 2022. He initially wrestled on YouTube shows like Dark, challenged Ricky Starks for the FTW Championship, and later joined QT Marshall's group The Factory. After that group disbanded, Karter regularly appeared on Ring of Honor and teamed with Griff Garrison, eventually launching a new wrestling stable called the Frat House with Preston Vance and Jacked Jameson. The faction has since effectively disbanded.

According to the reports, AEW officials allegedly discovered that Karter had been dishonest about a family-related matter that he had cited as his reason for missing a set of recent ROH television tapings. The company reportedly decided to terminate his contract after learning of the alleged discrepancy. Interestingly, Karter had reportedly been offered a new AEW contract earlier in 2026, suggesting that the company had previously considered retaining him beyond his existing agreement. The specific circumstances surrounding the family-related issue have not been publicly detailed, and Karter has not provided a response to the allegations.

Karter's reported dismissal comes amid other recent roster changes in the company. Tay Melo has also confirmed that she is no longer with the promotion after requesting her release earlier this year, with her name previously having been removed from the official roster page. As of now, AEW has not publicly issued a detailed statement regarding Karter's reported termination, and the allegations surrounding his absence from the tapings remain based on backstage reporting.

Jimmy Korderas criticizes AEW Full Gear

Former WWE referee Jimmy Korderas has delivered a scathing assessment of AEW Full Gear, criticizing the promotion's reliance on hardcore wrestling, excessive blood, and chaotic match formats. In a video shared on his social media accounts, Korderas expressed frustration with the direction of AEW, arguing that the company has moved away from the alternative to WWE that initially generated excitement among wrestling fans.

Korderas, who spent more than two decades working as a WWE referee, began by reflecting on AEW's early years and the potential he believed the promotion had demonstrated. He stated that a company that gave hope from 2019 to 2022, when it looked like a real alternative could press WWE during a slump, has now become a completely different entity. He called Full Gear horrible, noting that aside from a couple of good wrestling matches, the rest was complete slop. He preemptively dismissed critics who might call him soft or claim he cannot handle hardcore wrestling.

Korderas took particular issue with the lengthy Texas Death Match involving Swerve Strickland, The New Level, Hangman Adam Page, and Brodido. He argued that the match relied too heavily on weapons, blood, and repeated high-impact spots rather than delivering a compelling wrestling contest. He noted that there was blood in four out of the matches on the pay-per-view, which represented half the card and left him feeling desensitized.

The former referee also criticized the Anarchy in the Arena match, describing it as another example of AEW prioritizing spectacle over effective professional wrestling and joking that it should have been called the Clusterfurnace in the Arena. He highlighted the controversial moment during the Texas Death Match in which Strickland was shown drinking Page's blood, noting that such shocking visuals are ultimately what people remember. This spot also drew criticism from WWE Hall of Famer Ricky Morton, who questioned its contribution to Strickland's character.

Korderas dismissed the argument that the incident was justified by the long-running storyline between Strickland and Page dating back to 2023, stating that it was bad then and remains bad now, making the company a complete disappointment. Despite his criticism, Korderas acknowledged that AEW had previously delivered shows he considered excellent, specifically pointing to Double or Nothing as an example of the promotion's potential and a time when it seemed to be getting back on track before regressing.

Korderas concluded by emphasizing that his criticism comes from disappointment with AEW's creative direction rather than opposition to the company itself. He expressed that this is not the company he was cheering and hoping to see flourish, and that it shows a disregard for constructive criticism. While Korderas strongly criticized the event, reaction to Full Gear has not been universally negative, as the Texas Death Match in particular has received substantial praise from some wrestling fans, earning a user rating above 9 out of 10 on CAGEMATCH.

AEW announces three-week Collision residency in January 2027

All Elite Wrestling is heading back to a familiar venue for another extended run of shows, officially announcing a three-week residency at the Arlington Expo Center in Arlington, Texas, scheduled for January 2027. The venue, formerly known as Esports Stadium Arlington, has hosted multiple AEW residencies in the past and will once again serve as the home of AEW Collision for three consecutive weeks. The Saturday night program will emanate from the Arlington Expo Center on January 9, January 16, and January 23. Tickets are going on sale to the general public next Monday, November 23.

Darby Allin, Ricochet and Harley Cameron, Persephone, Andrade and Kyle Fletcher, and Willow Nightingale, Kofi, and Austin Creed are featured on the respective advertising graphics for the three episodes. AEW, in partnership with the city of Arlington, had its first residency at the venue in summer 2024 and then returned in January 2026.

AEW President Tony Khan has previously spoken highly of the venue and the atmosphere created during the company past residencies there. The wrestlers love it, the fans love it, the staff love it, I love it, Khan told the Dallas Morning News. There is a biological connection between the wrestlers, the staff, the fans, the building, everybody, the way it all clicks, the cameras, we all have a great chemistry and magic together there that could only happen organically.

Along with these Collision episodes, AEW made five more new date announcements covering Dynamite events taking place while this Collision residency is going on. These include Wednesday, January 6 for Dynamite at the H-E-B Center at Cedar Park in Cedar Park, Texas; Wednesday, January 13 for Dynamite at Payne Arena in Hidalgo, Texas; Wednesday, January 20 for the Maximum Carnage 2027 edition of Dynamite at UNO Lakefront Arena in New Orleans, Louisiana; Wednesday, January 27 for Dynamite at Tucson Arena in Tucson, Arizona; and Wednesday, February 3 for a Dynamite and Collision taping at Smart Financial Centre at Sugar Land in Sugar Land, Texas.

These events are happening on the road to AEW Dynasty 2027, which will be held in Liverpool, England on Sunday, February 7.

PAC's wife pens emotional note

Almost two months ago, AEW wrestler PAC passed away at the age of 40, leaving behind his wife, Natalie Satterley. The couple wed over a decade ago in 2015 and maintained a very private relationship throughout the years. PAC passed away just a few hours after AEW All Out on September 27, 2026. Now, roughly ten weeks after his passing, Satterley has penned an emotional note dedicated to him, sharing the post on Instagram alongside several wholesome pictures.

With Christmas just around the corner, Satterley opened up about how much PAC adored the holiday season. She noted that watching decorations go up in shops, seeing television advertisements, and hearing people talk about festive plans makes the reality of celebrating without him feel heavy, sad, and even scary. She reflected on how everyday events serve as brutal reminders, mentioning moments where she instinctively wants to message him—such as seeing Peter Beardsley in the food hall, unexpectedly hearing a Dennis Brown song while watching Chungking Express, or finding the perfect toad decoration for their animal themed tree—only for reality to hit and cause her to crumble.

Satterley shared that while some days she feels semi competent and finds purpose working away in the garden they created and cherished together, other days she achieves nothing and is paralysed with anguish at the prospect of a life without him. She lamented that they had so much more to see, do, and enjoy together, and that he had so much more life to live and goals that he hoped to achieve. She also expressed disbelief over the immense effort they put into creating their forever home over the last couple of years, only for him to never get to enjoy and experience it, adding that she can never make sense of his absence and how life can be unbearably cruel.

Despite her grief, Natalie expressed deep gratitude to PAC because he always made her feel protected, loved, respected, and heard. She emphasized that a true testament to his legacy is reflected in the kindness, care, and support they have received from family, friends, work colleagues, and his many fans who have all helped to keep them going. Concluding her heartfelt message, she offered her thanks to anyone reading who has been part of that support system, and left her final thoughts for her late husband by writing that he is so loved and missed, and that she is thinking of him always.

Tony Khan discusses potential future AEW tournaments

AEW is speeding full-force toward the end of the year. Following AEW Full Gear, the company is moving into Continental Classic season, with the selection show set for Monday night, and the tournament to begin on AEW Dynamite on Wednesday.

During the post-show press conference following Full Gear, Khan spoke about possibly expanding the C2 into other tournaments. Many fans have been asking for a women's version of the tournament, and in recent months, as AEW and Khan have expanded the tag team division, many are also asking for a tag tournament.

I do think there are potential for other tournaments, too, Khan said. They don't necessarily have to have the same branding as the C2, but there's a potential for a lot of great tournaments we can do. I think the C2 has been hugely, hugely successful and it's a round robin format. There are some things about it that are unique to its identity that I think are very strong.

Khan cited the complete lack of outside interference, the C2's most protected rule. He said it surprises most people unfamiliar with AEW, and he has to reiterate how serious he is when he means everyone is banned from ringside. Out of nearly 100 C2 matches across three tournaments, no one has tested the rule.

It would be the end of that person, Khan said. Nobody will ever do it. There will never be outside interference in it, and I think that's part of what makes it so unique because I can't think of another circumstance that's been tested this many times.

He said he loves the C2 for many reasons, including the unique scoring format of three points for a win rather than two. He reiterated that the rules and way of scoring could be implemented in other tournament formats not under C2 branding, but did not announce anything official.

Anthony Bowens on planned Billy & Chuck reunion in AEW

Billy Gunn and Chuck Palumbo are not a gay couple, but they played one on television, while Anthony Bowens is a gay man on television whose sexuality is rarely mentioned. Four years ago, the three of them were nearly involved in the same segment on AEW Dynamite, but the plans ultimately fell through. The Daddy Ass Birthday Bash aired on the November 2, 2022 episode of AEW Dynamite, and Bowens discussed the original plans for the segment during a conversation with DS Shin of Ring the Belle.

Bowens explained that what aired on television was nothing like what was originally supposed to happen. He wrote a whole segment that got approved and announced, making everyone extremely excited. His original vision was a birthday celebration for Billy where fans could bring gifts, and they could pick one from the crowd. Max Caster would give him a gift, and then Bowens would provide his gift, which was supposed to be a surprise person from Billy's past to reunite Billy and Chuck.

The reunion would not have had a happy ending, however. At the time, the group was feuding with Swerve Strickland and Keith Lee. Swerve was supposed to come out and complain about not being invited to the party before offering his own gift. As the group headed up the ramp to fight them, the camera would cut back to the ring to show Chuck hitting Billy with a low blow. They would then turn around to find themselves attacked from behind, ruining Billy's birthday bash.

For reasons unknown to Bowens, Palumbo was never part of the show. Bowens stated he did not know if Chuck did not want to be a part of it or if he was simply not available, but the idea did not come to fruition. Bowens then wanted to bring in Sean X-Pac Waltman or someone else from Gunn's long career, but no deal could be made. It ended up being just them giving Billy random gifts, which still ended up being fun, but Bowens believes it would have been epic if it followed his original script.

Bowens and Caster are former AEW World Tag Team Champions, and along with Gunn, they are former AEW World Trios Champions. Bowens recently experienced the expiration of his contract with AEW and told Shin he is enjoying his newfound free time while possibly exploring modeling or acting. Gunn and Palumbo were two-time WWF and WWE Tag Team Champions in the early 2000s, best known for an advertised wedding segment on SmackDown that fell apart when the two men revealed their relationship was a work. According to Gunn's official biography page on the WWE website, Palumbo was merely his good buddy.

🔄 המשך 🔥 AEW Continental Classic 2026 participants announced

The official lineups for both the Blue and Gold leagues are now locked in for the upcoming round-robin tournament.

The Gold League features reigning Continental Champion Jon Moxley alongside Kevin Knight, Orange Cassidy, Nick Wayne, Speedball Mike Bailey, and Tommaso Ciampa. This bracket sets up compelling clashes, such as former tag partners Knight and Bailey facing off, and Ciampa looking to bounce back following his recent title loss.

Over in antithesis, the Blue League features Jay White, Hangman Adam Page, Ricochet, Daniel Garcia, Brody King, and Bandido. Several of these competitors share immediate history and animosity, including Page, King, and Bandido who recently dropped their Trios titles together.

Matches will strictly adhere to the tournament's established regulations. Every bout carries a twenty-minute time limit, with competitors earning three points for a victory and one point for a draw. Furthermore, all outside interference is prohibited, as everyone is officially banned from ringside during tournament matches.

The tournament will run through the weeks ahead and conclude at AEW Worlds End on December 19. Both of the league final matches and the overarching championship final will take place at the pay-per-view event to crown the new Continental Champion.

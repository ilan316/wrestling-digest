🔥 Sheamus Free Agency and AEW Interest Rumors

Sheamus, whose real name is Stephen Farrelly and who has also used the name S. Farrelly, became a free agent following the expiration of his WWE contract after nearly twenty years with the promotion. His departure came after he declined a restructured contract that reportedly involved a significant pay cut. Following his exit, he signaled his readiness for the next chapter by posting a message online stating all challengers welcome alongside his new name.

During his lengthy WWE career, the former champion built an impressive resume of accolades. He is a multi-time world champion, having won the WWE Championship three times and the World Heavyweight Championship once, alongside reigns as a three-time United States Champion, a five-time WWE Tag Team Champion, and a holder of the Intercontinental Championship. Furthermore, he achieved major tournament and match victories, including winning the 2010 King of the Ring, the 2015 Money in the Bank ladder match, and the 2012 Royal Rumble.

Now that he is officially on the open market, his future has sparked considerable backstage discussion. Several influential veteran names and former coworkers within All Elite Wrestling have pushed the company to sign him. AEW and Tony Khan have historically maintained a cautious approach regarding potential free agents, generally avoiding substantive discussions until talent is contractually free to negotiate elsewhere. Although that restriction is no longer an issue, there are currently no substantial contract talks or finalized agreements between Sheamus and AEW, and no debut is locked in.

The reported internal support for his signing is not entirely universal. Some personnel within AEW have previously expressed concerns about adding another established veteran to an already crowded roster, though these concerns center around available television opportunities rather than any personal issues with Sheamus himself. Additionally, Claudio Castagnoli previously stated a desire to see his former tag team partner join the promotion, and other former WWE stars like Kofi and Austin Creed have previously transitioned to the company.

AEW is not the only destination drawing attention for the available star. Sheamus is drawing interest from multiple promotions now that he is available, with TNA among the companies believed to have interest in bringing him in. With multiple options on the table, the Celtic Warrior remains uncommitted as he decides where his next in-ring chapter will begin.

🔥 Will Ospreay Feuds and Title Reign Analysis

Will Ospreay has embraced the heavy responsibilities of being the AEW World Champion, aiming to represent the company on a global stage while bringing a competitive nature and sports presentation back to professional wrestling. In an interview with the Extreme Sports Channel, Ospreay explained that he wants to recapture a sports atmosphere that he feels has been lost in recent years, drawing inspiration from major boxing main events like the Conor Benn fight and tournaments like the World Cup to highlight drama and comebacks. He envisions introducing brand new concept matches never done before in AEW that emphasize his identity as one of the best sports-based wrestlers in the world. This philosophy could manifest at the upcoming AEW WrestleDream pay-per-view with the inaugural Clockwork Carnage match for the AEW World Championship, an ultimate endurance test he helped design, provided he successfully defends his crown against Kazuchika Okada at AEW Grand Slam France.

History has heavily influenced Ospreay's championship matches and ongoing feuds to date. Ahead of his defense against Kazuchika Okada in Paris, Ospreay has faced familiar faces like David Finlay in an eliminator match, Jon Moxley at All Out, and Ricochet on Dynamite, all of whom share years of history with the champion spanning multiple continents and promotions. Looking toward future challengers within AEW, former United Empire stablemate Kyle Fletcher has a storied past with Ospreay after joining The Don Callis Family and turning his back on him, though Fletcher is currently pursuing the TNT and Tag Team titles. Another major prospect is Gabe Kidd, a blunt instrument within the Death Riders whose shared history with Ospreay across NJPW could eventually lead to a massive title-versus-title match combining the AEW World Championship and the IWGP Global Heavyweight Championship at an event like Forbidden Door.

Ospreay's ongoing crossover work with New Japan Pro Wrestling has also positioned several NJPW stars as potential challengers for the AEW World Championship. At NJPW King of Pro Wrestling 2026, Ospreay returns to team with HENARE and Great O Khan to defend the NEVER Openweight Six-Man Tag Team Titles against the Knockout Brothers and Jeff Cobb. The Knockout Brothers include Yuto-Ice, who has been acting as a flagbearer for NJPW and engaged in a heated social media exchange with Ospreay, and OSKAR, who also traded barbs with the champion over past comments. Meanwhile, Jeff Cobb recently turned on the United Empire to join Unbound Co, setting up a potential path to challenge Ospreay down the line. Zack Sabre Jr., whom Ospreay has called the first British mountain he had to climb in his career, looms as a massive inter-promotional threat after appearing as a ghost from Ospreay's past during his All In London entrance. Finally, Callum Newman and Ospreay have been telling a story surrounding the leadership of United Empire since the beginning of the year, suggesting Newman could soon earn his own shot at the AEW World Title as Ospreay continues to elevate stars on the cusp of the main event picture.

Jim Ross Comments on Kevin Owens and AEW

Jim Ross believes it is still not too late for WWE Superstar Kevin Owens to eventually join AEW. Having been a part of the WWE roster for over a decade, Owens has firmly established himself as one of the biggest names in the promotion and is a former multi-time champion.

At the same time, Ross pointed out that WWE has taken good care of Owens while he was hurt, ensuring his surgery was paid for. He noted that those kinds of gestures are big, especially for a family and a wife, so everyone will have to see how his situation transpires moving forward.

Even with WWE's loyalty during his recovery, Ross claimed that it still might not be too late for Owens to jump promotions and join AEW at some point in time, adding that at least that is his personal take on it. Ross stated that if he were Tony Khan, he would welcome Owens with open arms into his roster and locker room, praising him as a hell of a hand and a rugged heel. He highlighted that Owens matches are consistently physical and logical, which seals it for him because he loves that kind of logical physicality and would love to see it, though he admitted he does not know how likely a move actually is.

While Ross would certainly love to see Owens make the jump to AEW, he ultimately believes that the former Universal Champion has already re-signed with WWE. Owens, who is forty-two years old, is currently set to be a part of the upcoming 2026 WWE Money in the Bank ladder match.

AEW Dynamite Global Search Interest

Google Search Interest for AEW Dynamite as a search term is up 20% globally, however it has only grown 5% in the United States year on year, 2% in the UK year on year and 100% in Mexico year on year. It is that Mexico number, plus gains across some other countries that has really pushed up that 20% number to where it sits now. Google Trends scores measure relative search interest rather than the total number of searches and the highest point in the selected location and period is assigned a score of 100. It is not an exact science of knowing how much hype is around a certain event or weekly show, but it is a good indicator.

There appears to be four jumps in interest for AEW in Mexico, with April 12th to 19th ranking fourth, June 14th to 21st ranking first, August 23rd to 30th ranking third and most recently September 27th to October 4th ranking second. While I can not tell you exactly what drove these jumps in interest, I can probably give you some context as to what drove them.

I initially thought it would be Mistico that really drove these spikes, but I am not quite so sure of that after looking at the data. The first spike from April 12 to 19 was around the time of AEW SpringBreak Thru. While Mistico did not appear on Dynamite, instead appearing on Collision that week, there were also jumps across the year for donde ver aew dynamite, meaning how to watch AEW Dynamite, which was up 50% in Mexico. However, MJF versus Darby Allin took place on that show, and interest in MJF as a term in Mexico is up 30%, remaining elevated since he faced Mistico back in September 2025, so I am thinking we can attribute that rise to him and Darby getting the shock win.

The second spike from June 14 to 21, which is the biggest of the year thus far, was the week that Dynamite took place from the Rio Rancho Events Center in New Mexico, marking the first time the company had ever run the building. On top of that, Dynamite that week featured Death Riders consisting of Claudio Castagnoli, Jon Moxley and Wheeler Yuta versus Brodido consisting of Bandido and Brody King alongside Mistico.

The third spike came August 23 to 30, which is of course the week that AEW went to the UK for All In on August 30. Because Dynamite that week did not have any Mexican representation, I attribute that likely to the broader hype for All In.

Finally, the most recent spike from September 27 to October 4 comes around the All Out pay per view event and, of course, the tragic death of PAC. Interest in PAC as a search term flew up in the last few weeks, so it would make sense that the AEW Dynamite tribute show would gain traction. Again, PAC had matches in CMLL this year as well as back in 2024, so the current Mexican audience would have had knowledge of him if they watched both CMLL and AEW, which is very likely the case.

While I can not give a definitive number on the success of AEW partnering with CMLL, it is clear that crossover talent like Mistico, Persephone and others have given All Elite Wrestling and Dynamite as a show a jump in brand awareness in Mexico. Sending talent over to CMLL appears to have made a difference too, as that title change involving MJF seemed to push interest, and for a far less joyful reason, PAC passing away in the last few weeks did as well. Both men have appeared in CMLL and moments involving them have driven interest. Again I can not 100% say for certain they caused these jumps, but it is a heck of a coincidence otherwise. For a bit of context, search data comparing AEW Dynamite in Mexico shown as the blue line against WWE RAW shown as the red line demonstrates that WWE is still of course the household name, and a recent RAW event in Mexico drove a huge amount of online interest.

🔥 Official cause of death revealed for Benjamin 'PAC' Satterley

The official cause of death for professional wrestling star Benjamin Satterley, known professionally as PAC in AEW and Neville in WWE, has been revealed nearly three weeks after his passing. A spokesperson for the Cook County Medical Examiner’s Office confirmed that investigators determined Satterley died from bronchial asthma, and his manner of death was officially ruled natural.

Satterley was 40 years old when he died on September 27. He was found unconscious inside a rental car outside a CAVA restaurant in Des Plaines, Illinois, at around 4 p.m. Emergency responders arrived and tried to save him, but he was pronounced dead at approximately 4:08 p.m. The 911 call described a worrying scene, with a witness noting the car was still running and Satterley was unresponsive inside with his face straight down and eyes open, showing no chest movement.

Investigators found that Google Maps was open on his phone with directions to Chicago O'Hare International Airport. Satterley was preparing to return to the United Kingdom after competing at the AEW All Out pay-per-view event in Chicago the night before, where he wrestled Andrade El Idolo in the opening match and later appeared to aid Jon Moxley during his match against Will Ospreay. A woman who spoke to police said Satterley had planned to stop for lunch before heading to the airport after his flight was delayed.

The incident report noted that Satterley had saliva on the front of his shirt, and medication was found inside the vehicle, though the specific medication was redacted and its presence alone did not confirm the cause of death. Early reports confirmed there were no signs of physical injury or foul play at the scene. While an initial autopsy was performed promptly, officials waited for additional test results before finalizing the cause of death.

Bronchial asthma is a long-term chronic inflammatory condition that affects the airways leading to the lungs. During a severe asthma attack, these airways become swollen, narrow, and produce excess mucus, making it extremely difficult to breathe. This can cause oxygen levels to drop rapidly and become life-threatening if airflow is restricted enough.

In the wake of his sudden passing, the professional wrestling community rallied to honor his memory. AEW held an emotional tribute episode of Dynamite featuring matches with the Death Riders and the in-ring return of Bryan Danielson. AEW management later called it the greatest episode of Dynamite ever produced by the company, and the promotion even unveiled an updated pay-per-view logo with subtle tributes to PAC. WWE also posted a tribute video featuring talent who remembered him fondly from his time as Neville. Additionally, his widow Natalie shared a heartfelt message on social media, writing that they would try their hardest to get through the nightmare and miss him every minute of every day.

🔥 AEW updates Full Gear 2026 logo with tributes to PAC

AEW has updated the official artwork and logo for Full Gear 2026, adding several touching tributes to the late PAC ahead of the November 14 pay-per-view event at the Mortgage Matchup Center in Phoenix, Arizona. The revised design highlights the late wrestler's signature moves and familiar objects associated with his presentation, giving this year's event a deeper connection to its origins and honoring his legacy following his unexpected passing at the age of 40 on September 27. His cause of death is still pending additional testing after an initial autopsy was performed.

The redesigned graphic features a Black Arrow Warning sign with an illustration of PAC performing his signature finishing move set against the Arizona desert backdrop. Two black arrows have also been incorporated into the artwork, including within one of the Full Gear cogs. Furthermore, the design incorporates his trademark Death Riders chair and the towel associated with his entrances.

These additions recall a memorable moment from AEW's official launch rally in Jacksonville, Florida, in February 2019. PAC arrived donning his wrestling trunks and boots, ready to wrestle, and confronted Hangman Adam Page. While most of those in attendance and other participants were dressed in formal business attire, PAC stood apart. That incident later became a running joke on Being the Elite during their rivalry and ultimately inspired the name of AEW's annual Full Gear pay-per-view.

The first edition of the event took place in November 2019, where Page defeated PAC in a singles match. Years later, the 2025 edition of Full Gear saw PAC defeat Darby Allin. PAC's connection to the event has thus been a part of the promotion's history from its earliest year.

The updated artwork brings together references to PAC's in-ring identity and the moment that helped give the event its name, providing the Full Gear branding with a personal connection beyond the event itself. The Black Arrow imagery and familiar ringside items ensure that PAC remains part of the show's identity as AEW prepares for its next chapter. Full Gear 2026 is scheduled to take place on Saturday, November 14, with the updated poster offering a lasting reminder of PAC's place in wrestling history.

Trios and singles competition will also highlight the night. Finally, the main event scene features a chaotic structure for the ultimate prize. Tommaso Ciampa discusses interest in wrestling for NJPW

Tommaso Ciampa has discussed the possibility of eventually wrestling for New Japan Pro-Wrestling. Having wrestled in Japan just once previously, and that too under the WWE banner, the AEW star addressed his future prospects during an interview with James Dator on SB Nation ahead of AEW WrestleDream 2026.

During the conversation, Ciampa revealed a specific opponent he has had his eye on. Shingo Takagi always jumps out at me as someone I have always wanted to share a ring with, he said.

However, the veteran competitor noted physical and personal limitations that would prevent him from committing to a heavier schedule with the Japanese promotion. I do not know that my body or personal life at this point bodes well for a full-on tour or G1 Tournament, but doing a show here or there, or three shows, I would be 100 percent open to that, Ciampa added.

Although his prior experience with the cross-over scene is limited, Ciampa did wrestle under the NJPW umbrella during the ROH and NJPW War Of The Worlds event in 2014. During that span, he wrestled twice in the span of one week, appearing once in Canada and later in the USA.

Looking at his immediate future in All Elite Wrestling, Ciampa is currently scheduled to face Andrade for his National title at the upcoming AEW WrestleDream 2026 pay-per-view event.

🔄 המשך Austin Creed on leaving WWE, joining AEW, and feeling revitalized

Speaking at a recent convention appearance, Creed admitted that the thought of joining AEW crossed his mind during his final years in WWE. He was eager to experience a different style of wrestling, noting that longtime fans might remember his early days in the X Division. Creed expressed excitement about being able to move around the ring that way again, emphasizing the distinct differences between the major promotion styles.

Creed shared that both he and Kofi feel completely revitalized since making the move. He praised the infectious energy of the audience, describing them as passionate enthusiasts who make performing feel deeply fulfilling. Comparing the backstage atmosphere to his early days on the independent circuit, Creed said the mutual drive among the roster to constantly improve has heavily fueled his creativity.

Beyond their initial tag team success, The New Level is also beginning to transition into singles competition. Kofi recently showcased this shift by challenging Bandido for the ROH World Championship at Dynamite: Grand Slam France, signaling that the duo plans to make waves across multiple divisions moving forward.

🔄 המשך Jeff Jarrett reacts to Max Caster's rap controversy at TNA Bound For Glory

Jeff Jarrett is the latest industry veteran to address the fallout from Max Caster's controversial TNA debut.

Speaking on his podcast, the Hall of Famer acknowledged that while he views Caster as a good and young talent, the controversial promo was indefensible. Jarrett emphasized that actions have consequences and questioned the internal accountability of the company, specifically asking who approved the rap before it went live on the broadcast.

Reports have since clarified that TNA originally granted Caster creative freedom for his entrance at the pay-per-view event. However, TNA President Carlos Silva was unimpressed by the outcome and immediately pulled the trigger on terminating Caster's pay-per-appearance deal. Following his swift release and a subsequent public apology issued earlier in the week, Caster currently remains a free agent.

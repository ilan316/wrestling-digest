🔥 AEW Detroit Dynamite Card Updates

ECW legend and former World Heavyweight Champion Rhino will be making an appearance during All Elite Wrestling's two-night television tapings in Detroit this week. This return to his hometown of Detroit carries added significance for Rhino, who has deep roots in the region and has been instrumental in promoting both events through local media appearances. He recently appeared at Fox 2 Detroit studios to help publicize the upcoming shows.

During an appearance on the ROAR Around The Ring podcast, Rhino confirmed his return and gave fans a sense of what to expect. When asked about the events, he said: "Well, this year, um, Christian was a little bit of a, you know, we didn't like him last year, but this year we love him. Christian is Christian. During the company's previous visit to Detroit in May 2025, he challenged Nick Wayne for the ROH World Television Championship on Dynamite, losing in less than four minutes. He remained with the company for additional tapings, competing in matches during the accompanying Ring of Honor programming. In one dark match, he defeated Max Caster on Collision, and he also teamed with Hologram to defeat Cole Karter and Griff Garrison of The Frat House in a match taped for Ring of Honor.

Beyond his work with AEW, Rhino has remained active in professional wrestling. He recently competed in the television launch of Scott D'Amore's Maple Leaf Pro Wrestling Mayhem series, defeating Gama Singh Jr. on the debut episode. 🔥 Nick Jackson Health Update After AEW Redemption

Nick Jackson provided a positive health update after a concerning moment during The Young Bucks' tag team match at AEW Redemption on July 26. Early in the match against Jon Moxley and Will Ospreay, Nick took a hard shot to the head following a collision that immediately seemed to affect him. The incident prompted him to stay outside the ring for much of the match while his brother Matt Jackson handled most of the action.

After the match, Nick was taken to the trainer's room where he underwent multiple concussion tests. Speaking on the latest episode of Being The Elite, Nick confirmed the positive results of his medical evaluation. "I went to the trainer's room, of course, obviously, and so, I had to take multiple concussion tests, and I passed, so that's good. They said I definitely had my bell rung. But I had a lot of dizziness," Nick said.

The situation was somewhat complicated by a pre-existing injury. Nick revealed that he has been dealing with a neck injury since AEW Forbidden Door in San Jose, where he landed directly on his head during an event. Doctors ruled out a concussion at that time and instead diagnosed him with whiplash. Since the Forbidden Door incident, he has occasionally felt foggy due to the neck injury, though he did not experience vision issues.

When the collision occurred at AEW Redemption, the symptoms felt familiar to Nick. "The last pay-per-view, you could even show this...the clip you have. I landed right on my head in. Where was that? San Jose, right? Landed right on my head and that was not a concussion or anything. It was considered a whiplash injury so, ever since then though, I've had these weird effects where I feel kind of like foggy, because of the neck. I didn't have any vision issues or any of that but, today, it felt very similar to what I was experiencing last month. But now I feel a lot better," Nick said.

Nick also provided insight into factors that may be affecting his recovery timeline. "The doctor said that I'm probably not healing fast enough because I'm not having enough carbs," Nick said. He also noted feeling dehydrated during the event. Both of these factors can contribute to slower healing and increased susceptibility to concussion-like symptoms.

Despite the health concern, The Young Bucks successfully defeated Moxley and Ospreay in the match. After their victory, Matt and Nick confronted AEW World Tag Team Champions Cope and Christian Cage. The confrontation led to an announcement that the two teams would meet at AEW All In with the tag team titles on the line. The commentary team had worked the injury situation into the storyline during the match, with Moxley suggesting that Ospreay continue targeting Nick whenever he returned to the ring, adding dramatic elements to what was a legitimate medical concern.

Kenny Omega AEW Retirement Opponent

Over the past few years, AEW World Champion Kenny Omega has made it very clear that he does not intend to keep competing inside the squared circle for much longer. After breaking his body down to create one of the most celebrated and decorated careers of his generation, Omega is winding down his in-ring career and doing it in grand fashion as the current AEW World Champion.

It will be an important decision for AEW and The Cleaner himself to decide who will be his final opponent when he finally retires. Here are ten potential Kenny Omega AEW retirement opponents.

Will Ospreay ranks tenth on this list. Back in 2023, Kenny Omega and Will Ospreay stepped inside the ring after years of insults and disrespect towards one another in what became one of the best Tokyo Dome matches ever at NJPW Wrestle Kingdom 17. After Omega won that first clash, Ospreay got revenge when he beat The Best Bout Machine at AEW x NJPW Forbidden Door later that same year. Following these battles, Ospreay and Omega gained respect for one another and even became friends, but that will all come to a head when they clash for the AEW World Title at All In London 2026 on August 30. Ospreay being Omega's AEW retirement opponent would be the perfect culmination to what has been one of the defining pro wrestling rivalries of the 2020s.

Speaking of rivalries, there has been no one who has been a more consistent thorn in the side of Kenny Omega than MJF, ranking ninth. At AEW Dynasty 2026, MJF defeated Omega to retain the AEW World Championship. However, Omega got his revenge when he beat MJF to win the title on the July 8 Beach Break special edition of Dynamite. Looking for the rubber match this year, MJF could return and look to put this feud to bed once and for all. MJF retiring Omega would be a great way to give The Devil yet another accolade that can further strengthen his legacy as the biggest star in AEW.

Kyle Fletcher comes in at number eight. To prove himself as a top contender for the AEW World Title, Kyle Fletcher took out Kenny Omega with a deadly Sheer Drop Brainbuster off the apron through a table back in September 2025. Despite coming back several weeks later, Omega never confronted and sought vengeance on The Protostar. Now that he is the AEW World Champion, The Cleaner might find himself across the ring from Fletcher again. Considering his status as one of the fastest rising stars on the AEW roster, it would make sense if Fletcher gets the honor of retiring Omega to further cement him as a main eventer moving forward.

Swerve Strickland ranks seventh on the list. At the start of 2026, Kenny Omega and Swerve Strickland got in each other's way in their quest to become AEW World Champion. This led to Strickland defeating Omega in their first meeting and then turning heel with a vicious post match attack with a steel chain. However, Omega got his revenge when he returned to defeat Swerve to become number one contender for the AEW World Title. Now at a 1-1 record against one another, Swerve could get the ultimate victory by not only beating Omega but also retiring him once and for all.

The concept of what eventually became independent wrestling was being developed, leaving the talent that just started to ride a wave of uncertainty in a business that was known for its lack of guarantees.

Rantula was one of Atlas' early opponents, but he also shared the ring with names like Bobby Fulton and Chief Jay Eagle during his formative years in the business, zigzagging across several states just to get the valuable ring time in front of a crowd that allowed him to truly hone his craft.

By the pro wrestling boom of the late-90s, an era when more than a combined 10 million viewers tuned into the weekly Monday night shows on cable, Atlas used his experience of nearly a decade to cement himself as one of the top names in the area when independent wrestling capitalized on the sports entertainment trend of the time period.

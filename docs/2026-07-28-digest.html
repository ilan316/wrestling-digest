🔥 AEW Redemption and Road to All In London

AEW concluded its final pay-per-view before the London stadium show with Redemption, the company's inaugural event in Montreal at the Bell Centre. The card delivered significant announcements and storyline progression while also generating some memorable moments both in and out of the ring.

One of the evening's biggest achievements was Willow Nightingale capturing the AEW Women's World Championship by defeating Thekla. The victory marked Nightingale's return to competition after suffering an AC sprain in her right shoulder during a TBS Championship defense against Red Velvet in May. The injury forced her to relinquish the TBS title and withdraw from the Owen Hart Cup Tournament. Nightingale explained that the severity of an AC sprain can vary dramatically, with some cases requiring months of rehabilitation or even surgery. She decided to vacate the title because she wanted to be able to defend it whenever an opportunity arose, and she felt unable to do that while healing.

Beyond the physical challenges, Nightingale opened up about significant mental health struggles she has been battling over the past year. During her appearance on the Redemption Countdown show and at the subsequent media scrum, she revealed she has been dealing with depression while maintaining her public persona as the cheerful, smiling competitor fans know. She emphasized that AEW has provided mental health support, including a traveling therapist available to talent, which has been crucial to her wellbeing. Nightingale spoke candidly about the expectations placed on her character and how she has found ways to keep going despite missing family events, birthdays, and weddings due to her wrestling commitments. She noted that while struggling internally, she has continued to show up and deliver when opportunities have presented themselves. With her championship victory, Nightingale is now scheduled to face Mercedes Mone at AEW All In 2026 on August 30th.

An awkward moment during a backstage segment drew significant attention from the wrestling community. Mick Foley, the WWE Hall of Famer who has joined AEW as a broadcast team member, appeared in an interview with AEW Women's Tag Team Champions Divine Dominion, consisting of Megan Bayne and Lena Kross. The segment's conclusion featured the champions performing their signature "Bow Down" routine, but Foley appeared to misunderstand the instruction. Instead of bowing, Foley barked like a dog, seemingly responding to what he heard as "Bow Wow." The moment stretched awkwardly, with unclear audio making it impossible for viewers to determine whether the miscommunication was intentional or accidental. Foley later clarified on Instagram that he had simply misheard the champions, mistaking "Bow Down" for "Bow Wow." He humorously apologized and referred to the tag team as his "new favorite tag team." The segment did serve a purpose in the broader narrative, as Foley mentioned that Divine Dominion have yet to defeat The Brawling Birds (Alex Windsor and Jamie Hayter), establishing the latter as legitimate challengers for the titles.

An incident involving Daniel Garcia and fans drew scrutiny after the main card concluded. Videos circulating on social media showed Garcia making his way through the crowd with The Death Riders following the event's conclusion. The footage revealed two separate interactions where Garcia appeared to slap phones out of fans' hands. In the first instance, Garcia knocked a phone from a fan's hand near the barricade and stopped to exchange words with the spectator, appearing visibly upset before walking away. The lack of clear audio made it impossible to determine what prompted the interaction or what was said. Moments later, a second incident occurred when a fan wearing a red jersey leaning over a nearby balcony recording the scene had his phone slapped away by Garcia, causing the device to fall to the area below. The situation took a more positive turn when fellow Death Riders member Jon Moxley retrieved the fallen phone and returned it to the fan before exiting the arena.

Tony Khan met with the media following Redemption and addressed numerous topics regarding AEW's present and future. Khan emphasized that Redemption successfully set the stage for All In, pointing to several moments throughout the show that advanced storylines for the London event. He announced three major matches for All In at Wembley Stadium and expressed that he is more excited about AEW's future than he has ever been. Khan acknowledged recent visa issues that had affected talent but noted significant improvement, stating that Komander, The Beast Mortos, El Clon, and Mark Davis have all recently returned. While acknowledging there are still "a few obstacles," he indicated the situation is much better than it had been previously.

Khan also revealed plans for interactive fan experiences at All In, with similar attractions planned for future major events. When asked about Kenny Omega's value to AEW, Khan became emotional and reflective. He stated that Omega was central to his original plans for AEW, with Khan writing episodes of Dynamite before the show even debuted. Khan declared there would be no AEW without Omega, emphasizing the irreplaceable nature of the wrestling icon to the company. Regarding a potential sequel to the AEW: Fight Forever video game, Khan indicated it was "a good thought" and something he has been interested in pursuing.

Khan also reflected on his journey with AEW, becoming emotional while discussing lessons learned, particularly regarding the deaths of Brodie Lee and Jay Briscoe. He stated he learned a great deal from both men and called the Brodie Lee tribute show the best event AEW has ever produced. Khan expressed that he wishes certain things had gone differently but acknowledged that both men remain "still with us" and that Briscoe helped make him a better person. Additional notes from the media scrum included Khan's intention to conclude Redemption before midnight Eastern time while still delivering substantial wrestling content for fans' money. He also revealed that an AEW podcast network is coming soon and joked about searching for a new golf course for another edition of Fairway to Hell.

Regarding talent health, Khan provided an update on Mistico, the AEW and CMLL luchador who was originally scheduled for Redemption but became unavailable due to injury. Khan confirmed that Mistico had been planned for involvement in the PPV before concerns arose that he might need to be held back, resulting in his absence. Khan expressed optimism that Mistico could return for Grand Slam Mexico. Mistico has been out of action since being attacked at Arena Mexico by long-time rival Averno and his stablemates Euforia and Mephisto during CMLL's July 17 event. It is believed, though not confirmed, that the attack will lead to a mask versus hair match between Mistico and Averno at the CMLL Aniversario in September, which would be the first such encounter between the two despite their decades-long rivalry.

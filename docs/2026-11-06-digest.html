🔥 Mick Foley praises Megan Bayne's performance at AEW Blood & Guts

WWE Hall of Fame legend Mick Foley had plenty of praise for the women who competed in AEW's Blood and Guts match, with Megan Bayne receiving some particularly high marks from the hardcore wrestling icon. While he might not know the match firsthand, there is arguably no one in professional wrestling who knows more about Blood and Guts than Foley. The WWE Hall of Famer was in attendance for the 2026 edition of AEW Dynamite on November 4, even interviewing one of the teams before the men's match took place in the main event. However, it is Foley's experience in such extreme matches that made him appreciate the women's match more than most.

Foley took to his Instagram Stories throughout the episode, sharing clips from the violent encounter and offering his thoughts on the performances. At one point, he expressed concern for Bayne after she was left covered in blood early in the match, enduring a lengthy fight alongside her peers. The former WWE Champion was deeply impressed by the punishment the competitors endured. This match was pure insanity, Foley wrote, adding respect to everyone involved and noting that these women are truly hardcore.

The wrestling veteran particularly singled out Bayne, suggesting that her performance in the brutal contest would be remembered as a significant moment in her career. Years from now, when we look back at the career of Megan Bayne, this will be one of the matches that defines it, Foley stated.

The women's Blood and Guts match featured a lot of brutal spots involving broken glass, barbed wire, and blood. Bayne teamed up with Lena Kross, Thekla, Skye Blue, Julia Hart, and Persephone to face Kris Statlander, Harley Cameron, Willow Nightingale, Queen Aminata, Jamie Hayter, and Alex Windsor. Specifically, the match featured The Brawling Birds consisting of Jamie Hayter and Alex Windsor, Wrathlander featuring Kris Statlander and Harley Cameron, Willow Nightingale and Queen Aminata, against the Triangle of Madness featuring Thekla, Julia Hart, and Skye Blue, alongside Divine Dominion featuring Megan Bayne and Lena Kross, and Persephone.

Bayne's team ultimately lost the match after Julia Hart submitted. Statlander secured the win for her team by forcing Hart to surrender with help from Hayter, as both wrapped Hart in barbed wire and locked her in dueling submissions. Despite everyone in the match being bloodied, according to a report from Bryan Alvarez, no one suffered any lasting injuries coming out of the contest.

Following the match, Foley shared an Instagram post alongside a bloodied Bayne in which he could be seen bowing before the AEW star. This humble hardcore servant gladly bows down before thee, Foley wrote in the caption.

This latest gesture has once again left wrestling fans divided. While some fans understood the gesture as a sign of respect for the brutal performance and punishment Bayne endured, others were far less impressed. This ongoing interaction has become a running joke between the WWE legend and Bayne and her Divine Dominion stablemate, Lena Kross. It originally started during an appearance in July when Bayne and Kross demanded that Foley bow down to them, though Foley initially misunderstood and started barking, joking that he confused bow down with bow wow. Foley eventually got the message, and following Bayne and Kross violent Chicago Street Fight against The Brawling Birds at AEW All Out in September, he shared a video of himself repeatedly bowing to Divine Dominion.

While some fans defended Foley and felt his actions were completely understandable given what Bayne went through, others questioned whether the picture was appropriate or entertaining. Several critics described Foley's repeated interactions with Bayne as strange and embarrassing, while others mocked the legend for continuing the same routine. With the Hardcore Legend continuing to embrace his role in Divine Dominion's ongoing joke, it seems that the running gag is not going away anytime soon.

🔥 Brawling Birds postpone indie match following AEW Blood & Guts

The Brawling Birds will have to wait a little longer before stepping into the ring for their next independent match. Alex Windsor and Jamie Hayter were originally scheduled to compete at Pro Wrestling EVE on November 8 in Wolverhampton against the team of Hyperactive, featuring Safire Reed and Anita Vaughan. However, that match has now been postponed following their participation in the violent women's Blood & Guts match on the November 4 episode of AEW Dynamite.

The decision to pull the AEW Women's World Tag Team Champions came after discussions between the promotion and the talent. Pro Wrestling EVE promoter Dann Read confirmed the postponement during an appearance on a wrestling podcast on November 6, explaining that it was a mutual decision rather than something forced upon the wrestlers. Read noted that such brutal cage matches can have a significant physical impact, and with the Brawling Birds preparing for their major title defense at AEW Full Gear on November 14 in Phoenix, Arizona, where they will face Kris Statlander and Harley Cameron, the promotion felt it was best to play it safe.

Read explained that those matches do have a massive effect on you, and after speaking to the talent, considering Blood & Guts was last night and they have a big tag title defense at Full Gear a week from Saturday, the decision was to play safe and reschedule the Hyperactive versus Brawling Birds match for as soon as they can in the future. He emphasized that it is not a thing being forced on them, but literally just a case of talking with the talent about what they are comfortable with after what has happened and what they have coming up. The promotion is still working on new opponents for Hyperactive for Sunday's card since they just found out about the change.

While the Brawling Birds match is delayed, EVE is still moving forward with its weekend events. The all-women promotion features several other AEW-affiliated talents and matches, including Kris Statlander defending the EVE title against Kay Lee Ray, Miyu Yamashita putting the EVE International belt on the line against Marina Shafir, and Skye Blue challenging Lucy Deathmatch for the Spirit of Eve championship. Additionally, Hyperactive is still scheduled to challenge French Art for the EVE Tag Team titles on Friday. EVE events are available on the MyAEW service via pay-per-view, with its library streaming on AEW's FAST channel.

Despite the severe physicality and gore associated with the sixth annual men's and second-ever women's Blood & Guts matches, reports from wrestling observer Bryan Alvarez confirmed that there were no major injuries to the winners or defeated sides, including the Brawling Birds, Wrathlander, Willow Nightingale, Queen Aminata, the Don Callis Family, the Triangle of Madness, Divine Dominion, Persephone, Will Ospreay, Adam Copeland, the Young Bucks, Darby Allin, and Steven Borden. Even with everyone reported to be okay, the grueling toll of the match was enough to prompt the Brawling Birds to prioritize their upcoming championship defense and reschedule their independent appearance.

AEW restricts commentary terminology during Blood & Guts TV broadcast

During the recent annual Dynamite Blood and Guts television episode, AEW reportedly introduced an unusual restriction for its commentary team. The rule became particularly noticeable during the women's Blood and Guts match, which kicked off the show for the second consecutive year. During the contest, familiar household items entered the action when Willow Nightingale came to the caged ring wearing a backpack containing broken glass and building blocks. However, these items could not be identified on air by their commonly used name.

According to reports, the promotion instructed its talent and commentators to avoid using the trademarked brand name LEGO during television broadcasts. Speaking on Wrestling Observer Radio, wrestling journalist Bryan Alvarez explained that AEW's broadcast team must now use the generic description toy bricks instead of referring to the building blocks by their brand name. Alvarez noted that the instruction was made explicit, with commentators expected to correct any accidental mention immediately. The restriction became especially clear when commentators had to correct themselves after accidentally using the prohibited term during the broadcast.

The issue centers around LEGO, a trademarked brand owned by The LEGO Group. Although the products are commonly described by the brand name, AEW no longer uses it on television. During the match, the toy bricks appeared among the weapons used as Willow Nightingale, Queen Aminata, Wrathlander, and the Brawling Birds eventually defeated Persephone, Divine Dominion, and the Triangle of Madness.

Alvarez did not provide details of a specific legal notice or explain whether the toy manufacturer made a direct request to AEW. However, the company has a Fair Play Policy that discusses counterfeit items using their logo, protects their trademark, and outlines how internet users should refer to their products. While that policy does not specifically mention television broadcasts, the reported restriction appears to reflect caution around using a protected brand name on air, with AEW likely erring on the side of caution and using the more generic term. It stands as an unusual addition to television guidelines, especially given how commonly the brand name is used to describe building bricks, but for now commentators must stick to toy bricks because even in Blood and Guts, some words remain off limits.

All Elite Wrestling is officially making its move to Hulu Japan, with the promotion’s streaming rollout now underway after the deal was first announced back in September. The company previously announced its agreement with the streaming service on September 16, 2026, marking the next step in bringing AEW programming to Japanese viewers. The agreement covers AEW Dynamite, AEW Collision, and live pay-per-view events, all streaming exclusively through Hulu Japan.

According to Hulu Japan’s schedule, Dynamite and Collision began streaming on November 10. The service also made approximately 20 episodes from September and October available, giving viewers a chance to catch up on recent storylines. AEW All Out and WrestleDream are included with regular Hulu Japan subscriptions at no additional charge. Future live pay-per-view events will require a separate purchase, starting with Full Gear on November 15. Hulu Japan later issued a schedule correction regarding Full Gear, noting that the broadcast begins at 9:00 AM, with the pre-show Buy-In and main PPV scheduled accordingly.

The move gives AEW a dedicated streaming home in Japan, where several of its biggest stars already have established followings. Kazuchika Okada, Kenny Omega, Will Ospreay, and Konosuke Takeshita are among the names familiar to Japanese wrestling audiences. Hulu Japan is operated by HJ Holdings and is separate from the American Hulu service. The AEW agreement applies exclusively to Japan and doesn’t change where the company’s programming airs in the United States.

Hulu Japan is an online video streaming service offering a wide range of exclusive content, including Hulu Originals such as movies, dramas, and variety shows produced or exclusively distributed by the platform, as well as catch-up episodes of popular TV broadcasts. The service is officially accessible only within Japan.

Founded by CEO, GM, and Head of Creative Tony Khan in 2019, All Elite Wrestling is a red-hot professional wrestling promotion that presents more than 100 live events a year around the world and features a world-class roster that injects new spirit, freshness, and energy into the industry. AEW Dynamite airs every Wednesday on TBS and simulcasts on HBO Max, while AEW Collision airs every Saturday on TNT and simulcasts on HBO Max.

Kyle Fletcher responds to Randy Orton comparisons

AEW star Kyle Fletcher has addressed the comparisons to WWE veteran Randy Orton, explaining why he considers them a compliment while remaining focused on establishing his own identity in professional wrestling. Fletcher has drawn comparisons to the 14-time world champion in recent years, particularly when it comes to their physical appearance. After shaving his head and adding considerable muscle to his frame, Fletcher has increasingly been compared to a younger version of The Viper. During an appearance on Tony Schiavone's podcast, Fletcher discussed his initial reaction to hearing these comparisons, noting that when he first started hearing it, his reaction was that Randy Orton is great and he loves Randy Orton, so to be mentioned in the same sentence as him was awesome.

At the same time, Fletcher believes any wrestler should want to carve their own path, and he feels he has started to do so. He acknowledged the significant difference between their respective careers, pointing to Orton's longevity and championship accomplishments as reasons why the two aren't necessarily comparable at this stage. It is hard to compare them because Randy has decades in the business and has won 14 world championships, and Fletcher noted that he is obviously not at that level yet, which is why people make the comparison.

Despite those differences, Fletcher hopes to eventually reach a point where younger wrestlers are being compared to him in the same way. He emphasized that he does not take the comparison as a negative thing whatsoever. Instead, being likened to a multi-time world champion only makes him more and more determined to continue working hard and carve his own distinct path in the industry.

Chris Jericho discusses preparing to play Gorgeous George in Muhammad Ali series

Chris Jericho recently discussed the process of portraying legendary professional wrestler Gorgeous George in the new limited series The Greatest, which chronicles the rise of boxing icon Muhammad Ali.

During an interview with FOX 5, the AEW star revealed that he auditioned for the role after producers had spent considerable time searching for someone to portray the wrestling legend. Although Jericho only appears in one episode, he explained that he put significant effort into studying George’s mannerisms, voice, and larger-than-life personality.

Well, I auditioned for it, Jericho said. It turns out they were looking for a Gorgeous George for a while and couldn’t find anybody. I found this old movie that George starred in, so I kind of imitated his drawl, his accent, some of his motions, his persona.

Jericho went on to explain the importance of Gorgeous George’s influence on a young Cassius Clay, who would later become known worldwide as Muhammad Ali.

It turns out, the reason it was such an important part is that Cassius Clay was a great boxer, but didn’t have the personality, Jericho noted. He was really inspired by Gorgeous George and they actually had a meeting at a radio station in Las Vegas where George told Cassius, it’s not about the fighting, it’s about connecting with the fans.

According to Jericho, that meeting helped shape the showmanship and charisma that would eventually become synonymous with Ali’s legendary career.

Making them love you, making them hate you, that’s what sells tickets, Jericho stated. That’s something that Ali always remembered and based all of his persona on from that time forward.

Jericho also reflected on the opportunity to bring such an influential figure in professional wrestling history to life on screen.

It was a great moment in Ali’s career and a great moment in mine, getting to play such an outrageous character in a such a massive show, he said.

The Greatest is currently available to stream on Amazon Prime Video.

Kevin Knight tops 2026 Black Wrecellence 500 rankings

Rising star Kevin Knight has earned the top spot on the 2026 Black Wrecellence 500 rankings. Righteous Reg unveiled this year’s BW500 list on social media on Thursday, November 5. The annual rankings recognize 500 Black professional wrestlers from around the world, highlighting their accomplishments while promoting representation and diversity throughout the industry.

Knight takes over the number one position previously held by Mercedes Moné, who topped the rankings in both 2025 and 2020. The creator described the project by stating that the list features 500 of the best Black wrestlers in the world, noting that it is about representation and culture, and encouraging fans to discover some new talent and share the rankings.

Moné remains near the top of the rankings, coming in at number two behind Knight. Swerve Strickland secured the third position, followed by Oba Femi at number four and Willow Nightingale rounding out the top five. The remainder of the top ten features Athena at number six, Lee Moriarty at number seven, Leon Slater at number eight, Michael Oku at number nine, and Royce Keys at number ten.

Looking back at previous years, Swerve Strickland topped the BW500 rankings in 2024, following Bryan Keith in 2023, Bianca Belair in 2022, and Trish Adora in 2021. Mercedes Moné, then known as Sasha Banks, claimed the number one position in 2020, which marked the first year the rankings expanded from 250 wrestlers to 500. Kofi Kingston was the inaugural number one selection when the list debuted in 2019.

Jake Roberts says Tony Khan needs to loosen up creative reins in AEW

Tony Khan's creative process has generally received praise, with many workers highlighting his collaborative nature. However, WWE Hall of Famer Jake Roberts believes the AEW CEO needs to be more receptive to advice from others. Having spent a few years in AEW and witnessing Khan grow as a booker, the legendary star shared his thoughts during an interview with Nick Teodoro regarding the differences between AEW and WWE. Roberts stated that while Khan is a sweet person, he needs to be more open to input from his roster.

There are a lot of differences, creative being number one, according to Roberts. He noted that Tony Khan needs to loosen up the reins a little bit and take some advice. Roberts acknowledged that Khan has a good product and a lot of talent, but believes the roster needs to be used better. Emphasizing his personal feelings, Roberts stated he loves working for Tony Khan, wishes him nothing but the best, and described him as a sweet guy. While Khan previously booked shows entirely on his own, reports from last year indicated that a few wrestlers now assist him backstage.

Roberts recently announced that he is no longer a part of AEW, bringing an end to a professional relationship that began back in 2019. During his tenure with the company, the iconic star shared the screen with Lance Archer. Roberts holds a very high opinion of Archer and believes the powerhouse should have received more opportunities. Calling it a perfect pairing, Roberts noted they had everything that people needed. He lamented that Archer is such an unbelievable talent who has never gotten the break he deserves, calling the situation a damn shame.

To support his former client, Roberts even asked fans to start a petition to help crown Archer champion. Archer was managed by Roberts beginning in 2020, with the partnership continuing for a few years before Roberts eventually went on to manage La Faccion Ingobernable, which marked his final notable stint in the company.

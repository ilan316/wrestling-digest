Dave Meltzer issued a significant correction regarding the Hogan pin footage that caused the initial conflict. 🔥 WWE SummerSlam 2026 Night 2 - Chad Gable Wins Intercontinental Championship

CHAD GABLE WINS FIRST SINGLES TITLE IN HIS HOMETOWN AT WWE SUMMERSLAM

Chad Gable finally captured the Intercontinental Championship at WWE SummerSlam 2026 Night Two in the second-to-last match of the evening, defeating Penta in his hometown of Minneapolis, Minnesota. The victory marked the first singles title of Gable's WWE career after years of coming close and being pushed in several different directions.

The match itself was a closely contested affair rooted in respect between the two competitors. The two contenders shook hands before the bell rang, setting the tone for what would be a physical and technical battle. Gable looked to end things early with a leg lock takedown on Penta and attempted an Ankle Lock, but the luchador fought out of it. After some back-and-forth, Penta took Gable down with a superkick, bulldog, and a backbreaker before going for the pin, but Gable kicked out.

Chad Gable then caught Penta with a German Suplex, hitting another one with a pin combination for a near fall. Penta regained control and sent Gable crashing into the turnbuckle, with Gable hurting his shoulder in the process. However, Gable bounced back with a clothesline and attempted a moonsault, only for Penta to kick him down. The match continued with several near falls, with Penta hitting a wild dive over the top rope while Gable answered with German suplexes in the corner and several attempts to lock in the ankle lock.

The fight became particularly brutal on the apron. Penta hit a Mexican Destroyer on Chad Gable on the ring apron. He rolled him back inside and went for the cover, but Gable still kicked out. The luchador went for another Mexican Destroyer, but Chad Gable moved out of the way and countered it into a German suplex, bouncing Penta to the floor. Gable then took him out with a moonsault back on the outside.

Back in the ring, Gable hit the flying headbutt, but Penta kicked out at two. When Penta looked for another Mexican Destroyer on the apron, Gable countered into an Ankle Lock. The final moments saw Penta almost reach the ropes, his hand just barely grazing the bottom, but Gable dropped to the mat to lock in the hold tighter, forcing him to tap out and ending the match.

With this victory, Gable secured the first singles title of his WWE career. Penta placed the title around Gable's waist after the match, raising his arm as the crowd cheered. It was soon followed by Gable's family, including his children, spouse, and parents, joining him inside the ring and soaking in the moment. The new champion then delivered a passionate promo in front of his hometown crowd and celebrated with his family in the ring, later chugging down two root beers during a sponsored segment.

The significance of this victory cannot be overstated. Gable had tried to win a singles championship several times over the years but never managed to do so. The victory came after Gable won a gauntlet match on the July 13 episode of RAW to earn this opportunity, then made the most of it by beating the same man who defeated him in Penta's WWE debut in January 2025.

That previous loss to Penta in January 2025 helped kick-start the entire El Grande Americano storyline. In 2025, after losing to Penta in his debut match and then beginning a feud with luchadores, Gable embraced the El Grande Americano character. However, following a shoulder injury and his return from it in early 2026, Gable began appearing in AAA in Mexico, where he started confronting Ludwig Kaiser's El Grande Americano. The two feuded, and their match at AAA Noche de Los Grandes was critically acclaimed.

Speaking at the WWE SummerSlam post-show following his title win, Gable reflected on his time in Mexico. "I call that just an absolute magic period in my career. It was like lightning in a bottle. The crowd down there, the fans, the people, the culture, they're all really responsible for what you saw go on down there, because they put just as much into it as we did as performers."

However, the road to this moment was not easy. Just days after competing at AAA Worlds Collide and WWE Money in the Bank on the same night in 2025, Gable sustained a shoulder injury that kept him away for months. "I had the match at Worlds Collide as Chad Gable, later in the day Money In The Bank as El Grande Americano. Time of my life, favorite day of my career. Shoulder injury right after that. I thought that was it. We were there, on the cusp; we had it, got cut off."

Despite that setback, Gable refused to give up. He reflected on the emotional toll the journey had taken on him. "I was a grown man, husband, father, and I found myself fighting back tears and I couldn't do it; I lost it. The tears were just flowing. It's reminiscent. It just reminds you of everything. I don't need to list all of it, but I mean, Shorty G, there was some low points, and you either stick with it and demand it of yourself that you're gonna overcome it all, or you don't, and I demanded it of myself and that was so fulfilling."

Gable's perspective on how circumstances ultimately led to his victory demonstrates his resilience. "But you never ever know. Then on the flip side, all those circumstances lead to the moment. It's a cliche, and I do hate cliches, but really you have to stick with it. If you never give up and deliver on what you say and work your ass off, you'll get what you deserve."

This victory marks a significant milestone in Gable's 13-year career with WWE. His previous accomplishments include being a two-time WWE Raw Tag Team Champion, a WWE SmackDown Tag Team Champion, an NXT Tag Team Champion, and a WWE Speed Champion as El Grande Americano. However, none of those achievements compare to capturing his first singles title, especially in his hometown in front of his family and loved ones.

WWE leadership also recognized the importance of the moment. Triple H posted a backstage photo with Gable holding the championship and pointed directly to the long road it took to get there. "The road to redemption and the Intercontinental Title ran through his hometown of Minneapolis, Minnesota. Congratulations, Chad Gable!"

Gable had spent years being pushed in different directions, dealt with comedy angles like Shorty G, and faced numerous setbacks along the way. But SummerSlam finally gave him the moment that had always slipped away. He walked into the show chasing his first singles championship and left with the Intercontinental Title, cementing one of the most emotional victories of the evening.

🔥 WWE SummerSlam 2026 Night 2 - Baron Corbin Wins United States Championship

Baron Corbin Wins United States Championship at WWE SummerSlam 2026 Night 2

Baron Corbin completed an impressive WWE comeback by capturing the United States Championship on Night Two of SummerSlam, defeating Trick Williams in the second match of the evening from U.S. Bank Stadium in Minneapolis, Minnesota. The victory marked Corbin's first main roster singles title in nearly nine years, with his last championship victory coming in October 2017 when he also held the United States Championship.

The match kicked off immediately with Williams going on the offensive, clotheslining Corbin to the outside and grabbing a table from under the ring. Corbin responded with a Deep Six on the outside, sending Williams crashing into the barricade, ring post, and steel steps. The challenger continued his assault, maintaining control of the match's early stages as the two battled in and out of the ring.

Lil Yachty, who accompanied Williams to ringside, became increasingly involved as the match progressed. Yachty initially got in Corbin's face and was shoved into the barricade. He then armed himself with a kendo stick and chased Corbin back into the ring. While the referee was distracted, Yachty managed to hit Corbin with the kendo stick, allowing Williams to mount an offensive surge.

Williams hit a neck breaker and the Book End for near falls, keeping Corbin grounded momentarily. However, Corbin countered Williams' eye rake and continued his aggressive assault. The pivotal moment came when Corbin grabbed the United States Championship from outside the ring. As Yachty attempted to reclaim the belt from Corbin's hands, the challenger raised the rapper up to the apron. Williams then knocked into Corbin, which sent Corbin into Yachty, causing the rapper to crash through the table that had been set up at ringside earlier in the match.

With the referee's attention diverted by the chaos, Corbin struck Williams with the title belt and immediately followed up with the End of Days finisher to secure the pinfall and claim his second United States Championship. The finish showcased the chaotic nature of the encounter, with multiple interferences and near-finishes building to Corbin's decisive victory.

Corbin's return to WWE began in July after the company chose not to renew his contract in November 2024, ending his initial 12-year run. He wasted no time making an impact, immediately targeting Trick Williams upon his return. Prior to the SummerSlam match, Corbin appeared on three house show cards in Fatal Four Way matches for the championship, but the SummerSlam bout marked his first official televised match following his comeback.

During his post-SummerSlam interview, Corbin addressed his rapid ascent back to championship gold. He emphasized his singular focus upon returning, stating he was on a mission and that people stepping in his way would get run over. He noted that just three weeks after his return, he was competing for championship gold on one of WWE's biggest stages. He made it clear he was not interested in making friends or relying on past accomplishments, instead focusing on making an immediate statement.

Corbin also discussed plans for the championship itself. Trick Williams had previously customized the United States Championship by backing and lining it with a sensational faux-fur design just two weeks before losing the title. When asked about the belt's future, Corbin stated he planned to restore it to its original design. He said, "The best part about this title is Trick just had this done for himself. I think it's pretty new, like two weeks old, and I'm gonna rip every ounce of fur off of this, and get it back to its pristine original state where it gets the respect it deserves."

Triple H, WWE's Chief Content Officer, wasted no time acknowledging the significant moment on social media. He posted a backstage photo of Corbin holding the United States Championship with the caption "The End of Days ushers in a NEW US Champion," making clear that WWE viewed the victory as a major moment for Corbin's second run with the company.

Not everyone is celebrating Corbin's title win. Carmelo Hayes, who was attacked by Corbin upon the latter's return, responded within hours of the championship victory. Hayes shared artwork heavily inspired by Eminem's iconic Killshot cover artwork, the diss track aimed at Machine Gun Kelly. The image featured Corbin's face locked inside a sniper scope with X's over his eyes, making it crystal clear that Hayes has the new champion in his sights. Hayes added nothing more than a devil emoji, letting the artwork send the message that he's calling out the new United States Champion.

The imagery suggests that Hayes is positioning himself as a potential challenger for the title, setting up what could be the next major program involving the United States Championship. With Corbin immediately establishing himself as a credible threat upon his return and Hayes making his intentions known, the United States Championship picture appears poised for an interesting chapter in WWE programming.

Cody Rhodes' departure from WWE in 2016 proved to be a pivotal career move that ultimately led to his ascension as the company's poster child upon his return in 2022. Rhodes initially left the promotion as Stardust amid creative disputes, embarking on a journey that took him through various independent promotions, New Japan Pro Wrestling, and co-founding AEW with Kenny Omega, The Young Bucks, and Tony Khan. After several years in AEW, Rhodes made his triumphant WWE return at WrestleMania 38 in 2022.

The Miz recently reflected on Rhodes' career trajectory during an interview with Denise Salcedo, praising the former Stardust for his boldness in leaving WWE. "I remember when Cody left and said, 'I'm better than this. I know I'm better than this, and you're not treating me like the main event that I feel like I am.' And he went out and became a main event, and now he is our poster child of WWE," The Miz stated. "And if he did not do that, didn't have the balls to do that, then who knows where he would have been, but he did it. And it's awesome to see."

Since his WWE return, Rhodes has accumulated an impressive championship resume that validates his decision to leave. He has won the Royal Rumble twice, captured the 2025 King of the Ring and 2024 Crown Jewel Championship, won WWE tag-team gold alongside Jey Uso, and held the Undisputed WWE Championship on three separate occasions. Despite his success, Rhodes recently fell short in his bid to capture the Undisputed WWE Championship from CM Punk at SummerSlam 2026.

Michael Cole, one of WWE's most senior members, recently recounted his journey with the company, which began in 1997 as a backstage interviewer. Speaking on the "What's Your Story? With Steph McMahon" podcast, Cole discussed how his career path led him from CBS to WWE. While working at CBS in New York after relocating from Houston in 1995, Cole was promised minimal travel. However, when 1996 arrived, he found himself traveling for six months on the presidential campaign circuit, a situation his wife did not welcome.

Cole's entry into WWE came through former colleague Todd Pettengill, who facilitated an audition with Kevin Dunn and Michael Hayes. After an initial silence lasting three to four months, Dunn called Cole with a job offer just as his CBS contract was about to expire. Cole initially intended to work at WWE for only about a year to gain television experience while continuing his news career aspirations. Instead, nearly thirty years later, he remains with the promotion and credits it with giving him a fulfilling career he never expected.

Cole's wife had a different perspective on his WWE schedule compared to his CBS days. The structured nature of WWE's touring calendar appealed to her, unlike the unpredictable emergency assignments that CBS demanded. Cole recalled receiving middle-of-the-night calls about plane crashes or other breaking news requiring him to travel until stories concluded, sometimes for weeks at a time. During these early years, Cole was in his mid-twenties, newly married, and becoming an instant stepfather to his wife's two children, making the predictability of WWE's schedule a significant advantage.

As Kevin Dunn's first WWE hire, Cole benefited from Dunn's protection and guidance. Rather than being thrown into the business without preparation, Cole was paired with Ron Simmons and JBL for his early travels, an arrangement that proved instrumental in his development. "I ended up travelling with Ron Simmons and JBL, and that shaped my life in WWE and I grew up quick," Cole reflected.

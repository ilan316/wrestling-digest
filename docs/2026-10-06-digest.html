🔥 Enzo Amore comments on AEW locker room and Maven interview

Former WWE star Enzo Amore made waves online following the release of an interview with Maven Huffman, during which he took a sharp shot at AEW while discussing backstage locker room issues. The discussion was sparked by a resurfaced story from Heath Slater regarding an altercation between Amore and Curtis Axel on a bus during their time in WWE. Amore stated that he was completely unaware details of the incident had ever become public.

Reflecting on his time in WWE, Amore explained that he had to fight for everything he achieved and was never a pushover, but emphasized that physical altercations and private conversations with management like Vince McMahon or Paul Triple H Levesque always remained confidential. Amore said that if he fist-fought another guy in the locker room, nobody ever heard about it because he likely won, and maintained that WWE locker room fights never left the building. He then directly criticized AEW, stating, Fuck AEW for all the pussy ass fighting that they do over there and letting it leak and shit. We are some badasses that you got to keep some credibility in that locker room.

While the conversation was released on a Sunday, Amore quickly took to social media to clarify that the interview was actually recorded months prior, specifically in March or May of 2025. He strongly took issue with the timing of its release, arguing it came right on the heels of AEW holding a touching tribute show for his close friend and former travel partner, PAC, whose real name was Benjamin Satterley. Amore praised the tribute as absolute class, thanking everyone who honored Ben, while adding that he was still grieving and suggesting Maven clearly was not. Evidence supporting Amore timeline included his facial hair, as he sported a smaller beard in the Maven interview compared to a longer black beard he wore during a podcast appearance shortly after PAC passing.

Maven later addressed the scheduling controversy in the comments section of the video. According to Maven, the footage was filmed in May 2025 and was initially shelved because Amore had requested a portion reacting to Simon Gotch's infamous critical interview be cut. However, after Maven shared that story in another video, fans flooded the comments asking to release whatever footage was available. Since a recent video was shorter than usual, Maven and his team decided to publish the archived interview.

PAC and Amore shared a close bond during their time in WWE developmental, connecting over a shared love of hip-hop and spending years traveling together as road partners despite their contrasting personalities. Speaking on Busted Open Radio following PAC passing, Amore remembered his late friend as extraordinarily humble and soft-spoken, noting that PAC struggled to accept compliments and genuinely did not realize how remarkably talented he was in the ring.

In addition to the locker room controversy and tributes, background reports noted that following Big Cass returning to WWE earlier in August, there was significant backstage interest in potentially reuniting Cass and Amore, though no official moves have been made regarding Amore signing with the company. Maven, the first ever WWE Tough Enough winner, continues to generate wrestling news through his popular YouTube channel, which features career retrospectives and stories from the industry.

🔥 Jon Moxley and Gabe Kidd tag team match at AEW Grand Slam France

AEW President Tony Khan has announced a new tag team match for the upcoming AEW Grand Slam France event, with Jon Moxley and Gabe Kidd joining forces to represent the Death Riders against the Don Callis Family.

The match was officially added to the card for the special Tuesday night edition of AEW Dynamite, broadcasting live from the Adidas Arena in Paris, France. The booking stems from recent events, specifically following a hard-fought Continental Championship Eliminator match where Moxley defeated Jake Doyle. After the bout, Doyle and his Don Callis Family stablemate Mark Davis issued a challenge to members of the Death Riders, prompting Khan to officially make the match for Paris.

This unexpected partnership comes with a notable amount of history and underlying tension between Moxley and Kidd. Just weeks prior, Moxley stopped Kidd from attacking Will Ospreay with a steel chair, highlighting that their alliance has not always been the smoothest. Nevertheless, with the Don Callis Family calling them out, Moxley and Kidd are putting their differences aside to represent their stable on international soil.

The addition rounds out a loaded lineup for Grand Slam France. The card features several major championship matches, including Will Ospreay defending the AEW World Championship against Kazuchika Okada, Mercedes Mone putting the AEW Women's World Championship on the line against Queen Aminata, and Darby Allin defending the TNT Championship against Kyle Fletcher. Additionally, Bandido will defend the ROH World Championship against Kofi, and Samoa Joe will face Jungle Jack Perry in a Clockwork Carnage qualifying match.

Fans can catch all the action as Grand Slam France airs on TBS and HBO Max.

🔥 New police report details regarding PAC's passing

New details have emerged from the police incident report regarding the death of AEW star PAC, real name Benjamin Satterley. The report provides additional information about the circumstances surrounding the 40 year old wrestler being found unresponsive in a vehicle outside a CAVA restaurant near Chicago O Hare International Airport on September 27. It does not, however, establish what caused his death. PAC had competed at AEW All Out the previous night and was preparing to return home to the United Kingdom.

When authorities found him, his phone remained open with Google Maps providing directions to the airport. Police also noticed that the phone was receiving calls. Officers answered one from a woman who said she had spoken with PAC earlier that afternoon. According to the report, she last spoke to Benjamin on the phone around 1320 hours and he related he was going to CAVA for lunch. He had been traveling for his occupation as a professional wrestler for the past three weeks around the United States and had been in the Chicago area since Thursday 09 24 2026 or Friday 09 25 2026.

The woman also informed authorities that PACs flight had been delayed and that he was scheduled to fly back to the United Kingdom later that afternoon. She related his flight had been delayed and Benjamin was scheduled on his flight back to the UK at approximately 1530 hours.

According to the report, police did not discover anything notable in PACs pockets or backpack. Authorities also did not observe any obviously suspicious marks on his body or suspicious circumstances involving the vehicle. The report noted saliva on PACs shirt as well as medication inside the vehicle. The name of the medication was redacted from the publicly available report. An initial autopsy has already been performed, but PACs official cause and manner of death remain pending additional testing. PAC turned 40 years old in August, just over a month before his passing.

The wrestling world has been reeling and mourning the loss of PAC, paying tribute to him all last week. AEW held a tribute show for him in the United States, and Dragon Gate honored him in Japan. A statement from PACs family was read on an episode of AEW Dynamite. 🔥 Chris Jericho says he doesn't have to win ever again in AEW

Chris Jericho says wins and losses are no longer his primary concern as he focuses on helping AEW's younger talent reach another level. Speaking on TMZ's Inside The Ring podcast, Jericho discussed his approach to working with emerging stars and said that building both AEW and new names has been one of his goals since the company's earliest days.

His goal was always to build the company and build and build names from day one, like he noted with the Inner Circle and all the work that was done. Sometimes it works and sometimes it does not, but that is just the way it goes. Looking at MJF, they literally had an angle for 366 days, a year and a day. Jericho considers that program to be the beginning of MJF's true ascension to being a top star, following the early work MJF did with Cody Rhodes.

Jericho also highlighted how working with him has helped shape other top roster members. When he wrestled Will Ospreay at Wembley, Ospreay was not even signed to AEW yet, with the company hoping to secure his signature before he eventually became a champion. The same pattern applied to Bandido, whose first match with Jericho was a great encounter that preceded his signing and eventual championship success in the promotion.

This philosophy drives his current career outlook, as he feels he does not have to win ever again. While he noted that he still has to win on occasion so that he does not lose all the time, which hurts storytelling, the outcome of the match ultimately does not matter to him. Instead, his focus is on putting on the best performance and match possible, and helping male and female wrestlers find something they might not have known was there yet, much like veterans did for him earlier in his career.

This mindset coincides with his ongoing television storylines. Jericho recently defeated Nick Wayne on an episode of AEW Dynamite, with the two scheduled to wrestle again in Paris at AEW Grand Slam France. He maintains a 4-3 record in AEW singles matches, holding victories over Ricochet, J.D. Drake, Tommaso Ciampa, and Nick Wayne, while suffering losses to Ricochet, Ciampa, and Kevin Knight in an AEW TNT title match.

Bailey also discussed the high-profile match between Will Ospreay and Ricochet at the tribute event, reflecting on their specific tribute moves and high spots from his vantage point at ringside.

Ospreay noted that he had not performed certain maneuvers in eight years, back when he was significantly lighter. He acknowledged that while tensions and hard feelings always exist between himself and Ricochet due to competing egos and the desire to sit on top of the throne, they successfully put their differences aside to deliver a great tribute to a mutual good friend.

Ospreay also mentioned the intense pressure leading up to the bout, specifically regarding fans and peers stating that if anyone could successfully hit PAC signature move, the Black Arrow, it should be him. Having not executed the move in eight years, Ospreay ultimately found a way to do it right.

🔥 Buddy Matthews pulled from Australian indie tour due to scheduling conflicts

AEW star Buddy Matthews will no longer be appearing on World Series Wrestling's upcoming MEGAMANIA tour across Australia and New Zealand. World Series Wrestling posted on Instagram that Matthews would be pulled from the tour just days before it was scheduled to start due to scheduling conflicts. The promotion released a statement saying they have just learnt that due to scheduling conflicts, Buddy Matthews will no longer be appearing on the WSW MEGAMANIA Tour. They added that they are really disappointed Buddy won't be joining them this time around, but completely understand that schedules can change, and they hope to welcome Buddy Matthews back to World Series Wrestling on a future tour while thanking fans for their understanding and continued support.

The MEGAMANIA tour is scheduled to run from October 29 through November 2, hitting five cities across two countries. Matthews was originally announced for the tour back in May, when he made a surprise appearance for the promotion to attack Donovan Dijak and set up his anticipated return to his home country.

Matthews has been sidelined from competition since February 2025 following a severe ankle injury suffered during his entrance at AEW Grand Slam Australia, though he managed to finish his match against Kazuchika Okada. In a video, he shared that he had to relearn to walk after his surgery. In July of this year, Matthews started to hint that he was cleared to return to the ring and suggested he had finally received medical clearance, leading to heavy speculation about an imminent return.

It remains unclear whether the scheduling conflict is personal or professional in nature, and no additional details were provided by the promotion. Several other AEW stars are still scheduled to appear on the tour, including National Champion Andrade El Idolo, Sammy Guevara, Brian Cage, and Cassie Lee. Meanwhile, AEW has a packed schedule ahead with Fright Night Dynamite and Collision, Blood and Guts, Full Gear, a debut in Monterrey, Mexico in December for Winter Is Coming, and the annual C2 tournament scheduled to begin on November 18 and culminate at Worlds End. Additionally, Matthews' wife, WWE star Rhea Ripley, is also currently sidelined with an injury.

Dustin Rhodes writing second book and comments on WWE restrictions

Given he has almost forty years in the wrestling business, it would make sense that Dustin Rhodes would have enough stories to tell that he could write a book. A fan learned directly from Rhodes that not only was that the case, but Rhodes had already written one and was in the process of writing another. Rhodes made the revelation when he took to social media to confirm there would be a second book regarding his career, while also taking a shot at his first book and WWE. He stated that he is in the middle of writing his second book, noting that this one will be way more in depth over his whole career, including the ups and downs, returning to glory and doing it his way, while adding that his hands were tied with the other company.

Rhodes' first book, Cross Rhodes: Goldust, Out of the Darkness, was released in 2010 while Rhodes was under contract with WWE, who also co-published the autobiography. At the time, Rhodes career seemed to be coming towards an end as he was primarily working as a producer. Instead, the next sixteen years would see Rhodes have a bit of a renaissance, winning the WWE Tag Team Championships twice with brother Cody Rhodes, the Ring of Honor World Tag Team Titles with Sammy Guevara, the ROH Six Man Tag Team Titles with Marshall and Ross Von Erich, and the AEW TNT Championship, which he won in his home state of Texas at All In 2025.

Rhodes joined AEW during the promotion's first year of 2019 and has stayed there even after his brother Cody left to go back to WWE. He is under a long-term AEW contract that will likely extend past his in-ring retirement. Rhodes, 57, is keeping things close to the vest but has started thinking about plans for his retirement match when it does come time to step away. Double knee surgery has kept Rhodes out of action following a Chicago Street Fight last summer where he lost the TNT Championship to Kyle Fletcher, though he returned to his backstage role with AEW and has indicated his hope to return to the ring. Rhodes also operates the Rhodes Wrestling Academy training school and Rhodes Wrestling Association indie promotion in addition to his AEW work. Earlier this year, Rhodes drew controversy for responding to a tweet that many considered Islamophobic; he later deleted the post and apologized to fans and wrestlers, including TNA's Mustafa Ali.

He and his wife Karen Jarrett also posted a video showing the couple sharing a quiet moment together on a bench in celebration of the milestone. The wrestling veteran first got sober in 2017 and will celebrate a decade of sobriety in October 2027.

Jarretts struggles with alcoholism were recently explored during the seventh season of Vice TVs Dark Side of the Ring, which featured a three-part documentary covering his life, career and the founding of TNA Wrestling. During the documentary, Jarrett and Karen discussed his battle with alcohol addiction and the personal hardships he faced throughout his life. Those included his complicated relationship with his father, Jerry Jarrett, the tragic death of his longtime friend and tag team partner Owen Hart, and the passing of his first wife, Jill. Jarrett eventually reconciled with his father before Jerrys death in 2023.

Following his recovery, Jarrett returned to WWE and was inducted into the WWE Hall of Fame in 2018. He has been with AEW since 2022, working both behind the scenes and as an on-screen performer, which includes appearing as a host for pay-per-view pre-shows. Additionally, last month saw Jarrett compete at CMLLs 93rd Aniversario in a match against Blue Panther.

Andrade comments on jumping between AEW and WWE and relationship with Tony Khan

Andrade El Idolo holds the distinct record for jumping back and forth between WWE and AEW the most. Originally being introduced to WWE in 2015 as Andrade Cien Almas, Andrade eventually established himself on the main roster before leaving WWE in 2021 to jump to AEW, where he competed until 2023.

By 2024, Andrade made a high-profile return to WWE at that year's Royal Rumble, before his momentum fizzled out and he shockingly returned to AEW again in 2025. Sitting down for an interview on Close Up with Renee Paquette, which was transcribed by Wrestling Inc., Andrade commented on his relationship with Tony Khan and the experience of jumping between the two promotions.

Personally, it was good, while professionally it was a little sad because one, my AEW contract was expired at this moment, so I need to pick a decision, like I need to go for a personal life because I was married at this moment, or my professional life, so I picked my personal life, he explained during the Close Up with Renee Paquette interview, as transcribed by Wrestling Inc.

Andrade maintained that his exit from AEW was done professionally and that he still had a good relationship with AEW following his exit, and that he's grateful for being able to return to the promotion and doesn't plan to take his second chance for granted.

Now is the moment to show who I am! I need to pay to Tony, you know, like I need to pay with my work in the ring? he proclaimed, according to the Wrestling Inc. transcription of Close Up with Renee Paquette. Andrade further notes that he wants to uplift AEW while also giving back to the locker room. This is how I need to pay Tony for this second chance, second opportunity.

Since his 2025 return, Andrade has won the AEW National Championship.

Cash Wheeler explains FTR's advantage over MCMG in AEW series

Cash Wheeler believes FTR has the advantage heading into the remaining matches of their best-of-five series against the Motor City Machine Guns. The two teams are currently tied at 1-1 after Alex Shelley and Chris Sabin picked up the opening victory at AEW WrestleDream. FTR evened the series with a win on the following episode of AEW Collision.

During a recent interview with Going Ringside, Wheeler explained why he believes longer matches and extended rivalries play directly into FTR’s strengths. Wheeler stated that he shockingly sees it working out in FTR’s favor because everybody knows that the longer a match or series goes, the better off FTR is since they thrive in marathon style matches. He noted that they have done 58 minutes with Jay White and Juice Robinson and 45 minutes with Mark and Jay Briscoe.

Wheeler continued by pointing to FTR’s experience in lengthy matches, suggesting that the Motor City Machine Guns may struggle once the action goes beyond the usual time frame. When getting into deep waters and past the point of a normal match into a new realm, Wheeler believes that is FTR's milieu and they thrive there while questioning if Motor City can say the same thing. He added that while MCMG might be more explosive in the first 15 or 20 minutes, FTR is very good at dragging the match out to get that advantage, which plays in their favor.

Wheeler also addressed FTR’s loss in the opening match at WrestleDream, noting that both he and Dax Harwood were still recovering from their physically demanding ladder match at AEW All Out that took a couple of years off their careers each. He explained that they are still getting back to full health and getting their shoulders back into a healthy range of motion, concluding that if the series goes the full five matches, it heavily favors FTR.

The third match in the best-of-five series between FTR and the Motor City Machine Guns is scheduled for AEW Fright Night Dynamite.

Jeff Jarrett comments on the future of AEW Clockwork Carnage match

Jeff Jarrett thinks AEW’s Clockwork Carnage match has a future beyond its WrestleDream debut. He said the concept exceeded his expectations and could become a regular part of the company’s match lineup.

Discussing WrestleDream on My World with Jeff Jarrett, Jarrett said the participants convinced him the bout could stand out before it began. During the pay-per-view event, Will Ospreay retained the AEW World Championship in the main event.

Co-host Conrad Thompson took a different view of the match’s placement on the card. He felt it would have been better suited to a television special than a major spot on a pay-per-view event.

The Dogs discuss short preparation time for AEW double chain match

The Dogs recently opened up about the challenges they faced regarding their short preparation time for the double chain match against Jay White and Juice Robinson at AEW Redemption. Speaking on What Happened When with Tony Schiavone and Conrad Thompson, David Finlay and Clark Connors pulled back the curtain on the behind the scenes realities of putting together such a high risk stipulation under a tight schedule.

During the discussion, Finlay flatly rejected the suggestion that the team had been given extensive preparation time for the encounter. Instead of trying to steal the show or overshadow another violent match scheduled later in the event, Connors and Finlay explained that they intentionally tried to work around the rest of the card to ensure the entire show flowed effectively.

Reflecting on the execution of the match, Connors stated that he remained happy with the final product, though both men acknowledged there were elements they would change in hindsight. Among the notable moments discussed was a dangerous spot where Connors performed a fall from the top rope onto the steel steps, an impact that unfortunately went unseen by viewers due to a camera switch.

Finlay added that they ultimately worked within the specific tools and limits they had been given by management. However, he admitted to wondering afterward whether they should have been more selfish in their approach to the match, prioritizing their own explosive moment rather than accommodating the broader structure of the event.

Ilja Dragunov comments on WWE's usage of GUNTHER after retiring John Cena

One of the most acclaimed rivalries to have ever come out of the now defunct WWE NXT UK was between former WWE star Ilja Dragunov and the former NXT United Kingdom Champion GUNTHER, who previously competed as WALTER. Refusing to give up, despite having it all stacked against him, Dragunov was the only man to topple GUNTHER of his historic 870-day reign to become the new champion at WWE NXT TakeOver 36. Since their brutal rivalry, GUNTHER went on to retire the likes of Hall of Famers Goldberg, AJ Styles, and the former Never Seen 17 World Champion, John Cena. But now, it seems GUNTHER's push has plateaued, which fans, along with Dragunov, have seen as of late.

Speaking in a recent interview, GUNTHER's former on-screen adversary shared his candid perspective on his peer's current trajectory. I guess my feelings are the same feelings as a lot of other people share. If you are the person to retire John Cena, it's kind of like a standard that you should keep a certain level of that, in my opinion, Dragunov stated. For me, he is one of the best. He will be one of the best of all time. He's a dear friend of mine for, I don't know, maybe since 2014, 2015...We are dear friends to each other... I know he's good. I know he will always do good and whatever he does, it's going to be awesome. But I can, of course, imagine that after the standard that he set and the standard of how he got betrayed, that this is not necessarily the level that we're working at right now.

Not able to reach a contractual agreement, Dragunov left the WWE after seven years this past August to join AEW, where he made his first appearance for the promotion at WrestleDream. As for GUNTHER, he is still waiting in the wings for another shot at the Undisputed Championship. In the meantime, he is keeping busy by feuding with Finn Balor.

Eddie Kingston considered leaving wrestling following tragic deaths of AEW colleagues

AEW star Eddie Kingston has opened up about the emotional toll of losing Andy The Butcher Williams and Ben PAC Satterley, revealing that he considered walking away from professional wrestling following their deaths. Williams passed away on September 6, 2026, while Satterley died on September 27, which was the exact same day a memorial service was held for Williams.

Kingston addressed these devastating losses following his match against Luke Jacobs at PROGRESS Wrestlings Chapter 199 Bonestorm event on Sunday, October 25. After defeating Jacobs in a hard-hitting bout that left his opponent bloodied, Kingston delivered an emotional post-match speech in which he admitted that the deaths of his friends left him questioning his future in the wrestling business.

When you lose people, and you gain fake people, and you lose real people, life is a fcked up place, Kingston told the crowd. I wake up every morning and do this because of the fans.

Kingston went on to reveal how difficult the weeks following their deaths had been for his mental health. After Andy and Ben passed away, I didnt leave my house for five days, he shared. I wanted to leave this business.

Despite wanting to quit, Kingston took time to thank wrestling fans for their continued support throughout his career, particularly during the past several difficult months. He concluded his heartfelt address by giving credit to Luke Jacobs for his tough performance in the ring during their encounter.

🔄 המשך Tony Khan addresses status of The Hurt Syndicate in AEW

During a media call ahead of the 2026 AEW WrestleDream pay-per-view event, AEW President Tony Khan was asked about the current status of The Hurt Syndicate within the company.

Addressing their booking and character motivation, Khan noted that the group operates under a for-hire philosophy. In the ring, they have positioned themselves as mercenaries who could show up anyplace and anytime for the right amount of money.

Khan referenced their recent interactions involving Big Boom AJ, Big Justice, and the Rizzler, noting that while the group initially had a precarious relationship that led to brawls at Brawl in the Ballpark, they ultimately found common ground. He compared their selective business model to The A-Team, explaining that they will not work with just anyone, though STP previously attempted to hire their services after being bested.

Concluding his remarks, Khan expressed high regard for Bobby Lashley, Shelton Benjamin, and MVP, reiterating that they remain dangerous free agents who could potentially align with anyone on the roster as hired muscle.

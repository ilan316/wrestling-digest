Jonathan Coachman claims he would be a top three promo in modern WWE or AEW

Jonathan Coachman believes his microphone ability remains strong enough to make him one of the top three promo performers in either WWE or AEW today. Coachman made the confident assessment while discussing his transition from interviewer to heel character during an appearance on Developmentally Speaking, where he was asked when WWE first realized he could be more than just the guy standing backstage with a microphone.

Coachman believes working with The Rock early in his career helped people inside the company start seeing something more in him. He also believes his physical presentation worked in his favor at the time, noting that he was in really good shape back then. Furthermore, Vince McMahon apparently saw something in Coachman, liking and trusting him enough to give him opportunities that went well beyond conducting backstage interviews. Coachman noted that there was a specific reason he went to Afghanistan and was able to sit next to Vince on a plane where he was talking really serious business, emphasizing that Vince simply liked and trusted him.

After a couple of years, Coachman says WWE figured out just how good he could be on the microphone. That led to his biggest claim, stating that the exact same ability would put him near the top of either major wrestling company today, adding that he would be one of their top three promo givers the minute he walked through the door. Coachman pointed to his past heel run as proof of his abilities, recalling how he was getting heavily booed by audiences their asses off even though most of the time he was not physical. Eventually, however, talking that much trash meant somebody was going to have to beat him up.

Vince McMahon made sure Coachman understood that part of the deal. If Coachman was going to become physically involved in storylines, he needed to learn how to actually work, which is what the industry calls wrestling. Coachman described the wrestling training as the hardest thing he had ever attempted, but the experience ultimately gave him a lot more confidence and made him realize through the training that he is a lot tougher than he thought he was. Previous coverage has noted Coachman previously calling MJF one of wrestling is elite talkers, making his current top-three ranking claim particularly notable, though the ranking is ultimately Coachman is personal opinion rather than an objective measure of current WWE or AEW promo ability.

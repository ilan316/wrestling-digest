Kyle Fletcher Explains Decision Behind AEW International Title Design

While he might not have gold around his waist at the time of writing, 2026 has been quite the year for Kyle Fletcher. The Protostar has held the AEW TNT Championship, he has been one third of the AEW World Trios Champions alongside Mark Davis and Kazuchika Okada, and he held the AEW International Championship until AEW All In London 2026 where the aforementioned Kazuchika Okada took the title from him.

The TNT Championship belt has been personalized multiple times since it was introduced in 2020 to fit the character of whoever is holding it, and during his first reign, Fletcher famously carried a pink title. However, he decided not to edit the current International Championship belt. During a recent media appearance, Fletcher explained that his refusal to change the belt was simply to annoy the people who made him edit the TNT Championship in the first place.

I feel like last time I gave into the masses, Fletcher said, explaining that the masses wanted the pink title and this time everyone was demanding it again. His response to the persistent fan demand was sheer defiance, as he wanted to mess with people who expected him to repeat the design choice. To prove how much he enjoys playing against expectations, Fletcher admitted that if the fans had actually wanted the International Championship to be left alone, he would have felt far more motivated to make it pink just to be contrary.

Despite losing his title at Wembley Stadium, Fletcher has bounced back with a pair of tag team victories alongside Kevin Knight. Most recently, the duo defeated The Young Bucks in a grueling tables match on the Rebel Heart edition of AEW Dynamite. However, tensions are still building within The Don Callis Family, especially between Fletcher and Okada as they both believe they are the true AEW International Champion.

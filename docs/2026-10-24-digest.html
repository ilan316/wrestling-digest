🔥 Samantha Irvin's WWE non-compete expiring and potential AEW interest

Former WWE ring announcer Samantha Irvin, who now goes by her real name Samantha Johnson, is set to become a free agent as her unique non-compete agreement with WWE comes to an end. Johnson originally departed WWE in October 2024 after choosing to leave the company before completing her three-year contract.

Her departure came with an unusual contract arrangement that set her apart from most wrestling stars. While released talent often deals with 30-day or 90-day termination periods where they continue to receive pay while their agreements wind down, Johnson faced an official two-year non-compete restriction for quitting early, and she reportedly received no payment during this time.

With the restriction nearing its expiration, speculation has naturally turned toward a potential move to All Elite Wrestling. Internal discussions regarding bringing Johnson into the company for an on-screen role have taken place on multiple occasions. Johnson is married to AEW star Ricochet and has maintained a strong relationship with the promotion, having been backstage at dozens of AEW events over the past two years.

Despite the backstage interest and her connections, fans hoping for an immediate television appearance should lower their expectations. Reports indicate that an immediate debut is not in the plans, and any potential involvement with the promotion would likely happen further down the road rather than immediately following the expiration of her agreement. Furthermore, no official deal has been confirmed.

When she eventually makes her next move in the wrestling industry, it will likely not involve a return to ring announcing. Johnson has previously expressed a lack of interest in stepping back behind the microphone to introduce wrestlers. She has focused primarily on her music career in the meantime, releasing multiple singles including the song Make Me. Her next career step remains open as she prepares to officially explore her options in pro wrestling once again.

🔥 AEW's Willow Nightingale provides injury update on broken hand

Former AEW Women's World Champion Willow Nightingale has shared a new recovery update regarding her broken hand. Taking to her Instagram stories during a fan Q&A session, Nightingale addressed her healing process, revealing that her hand is feeling better but is still not one hundred percent healed. She posted the update alongside an image of herself behind the wheel, explaining that she continues to use a medical bone stimulator daily, even fitting the treatment in while stuck in traffic.

Nightingale's hand injury first became a concern in the weeks leading up to AEW All In London. Despite the setback, she competed at the pay-per-view event in August, ultimately losing her AEW Women's World title to Mercedes Mone. Following that defeat, she battled Thekla at AEW All Out in September in an effort to earn another shot at the gold, though Thekla emerged victorious. The broken hand marks her second major injury of the year, following a shoulder injury in May that forced her to forfeit her spot in the Owen Hart Foundation tournament and vacate the TBS Championship.

Despite the physical toll, the injury has not kept Nightingale away from television or the ring completely. Management wanted to keep her involved in weekly programming, allowing her to remain part of ongoing storylines while working her way back to full health. Her most recent match was an eight-person mixed tornado tag team contest recorded on October 6 and later aired on AEW Collision Grand Slam Paris on October 10. In that bout, Nightingale teamed up with her Conglomeration stablemates Kyle O'Reilly, Orange Cassidy, and Roderick Strong in a loss against the Death Riders team of Marina Shafir, Claudio Castagnoli, Daniel Garcia, and Wheeler Yuta.

This week also marks the four-year anniversary of Nightingale's AEW signing being officially announced. Over the years, she has grown to become one of the most popular babyfaces in the company and achieved the historic milestone of being the first women's triple crown champion in AEW history, having held the Women's World Championship, TBS Championship, and Women's Tag Team titles at various points in her career.

AEW Dynamite viewership ratings for October 21

New ratings data has been released for the October 21 episode of AEW Dynamite on TBS, reflecting the show's performance on cable television and a minor week-to-week shift. Reports indicate that the program averaged 661,000 viewers on TBS. This number follows the October 14 episode, which was a three-hour broadcast where the Dynamite portion alone drew 715,000 viewers, while the overall show drew 614,000 viewers.

In the key 18 to 49 demographic, Wednesday's episode scored a 0.15 rating. This is up from the 0.12 average seen the prior week, while just the Dynamite portion scored a 0.14 demo rating the previous Wednesday. Compared to the October 14 episode, this represents a decrease of 54,000 total viewers and an increase of 0.01 in the key demographic.

When looking at year-over-year metrics, the growth is substantial. AEW Dynamite previously drew 477,000 viewers and a 0.08 demo rating on October 22, 2025, compared to 661,000 viewers and a 0.15 demo rating on October 21, 2026. That represents a year-over-year increase of 38.6 percent in viewership and 87.5 percent in the demographic rating. These figures reflect only linear TV viewership on TBS and do not include streaming or digital viewership. Notably, the episode was also simulcast on HBO Max, though viewership information for that streaming platform is not available.

The broadcast featured several high-profile segments that resonated online, as reflected by the top five most viewed AEW Dynamite videos on YouTube. Steven Borden returned and declared Blood and Guts, pulling in 252K views. Ilja Dragunov made his first AEW Dynamite entrance with 243K views, followed by his first match in the company against Speedball Mike Bailey, which brought in 88K views. Additionally, Mercedes Mone and the AEW Women's World Title were targeted by Marina Shafir, generating 129K views, while Davis and Doyle destroyed and defeated the Young Bucks in under three minutes, capturing 101K views.

Reason for AEW stars missing October 21 Dynamite

The absence of Kofi Kingston and Austin Creed from the October 21 episode of AEW Dynamite reportedly had nothing to do with creative decisions, ongoing storylines, or any change in the creative direction for their group, The New Level. According to Dave Meltzer in the Wrestling Observer Newsletter, both wrestlers missed the television taping to attend the wedding of their longtime friend and former WWE stablemate, Big E.

Big E married Kris Yim, the sister of WWE Superstar Mia Yim, also known as Michin, in a ceremony attended by family, friends, and several familiar names from the professional wrestling industry. Kingston and Creed were among those in attendance, maintaining a close friendship with Big E following their years together as members of The New Day, one of WWE's most successful factions. Despite their careers taking different paths after working together as a trio, their bond has continued beyond their time in WWE.

With Kingston and Creed unavailable for the broadcast, Swerve Strickland represented The New Level alone during a backstage segment involving the new AEW World Trios Champions, Hangman Adam Page and Brodido, the team of Bandido and Brody King. The segment followed The New Level losing the AEW World Trios Championships to Page and Brodido at WrestleDream on October 17.

Strickland confronted the new champions and challenged them to a rematch at AEW Full Gear, which Page accepted. However, the interaction became more personal when Strickland brought up their violent history and proposed making the championship rematch a Texas Death Match. Page ultimately rejected the stipulation, recognizing that Strickland had ulterior motives for wanting to revisit their past rivalry. This exchange allowed AEW to successfully continue building the storyline between the two sides while Kingston and Creed were away for the wedding, ensuring the group remains firmly involved in the championship picture heading toward Full Gear.

Tony Schiavone reveals backstage concerns over AEW Clockwork Carnage match

AEW introduced a unique match concept at WrestleDream 2026, but at least one familiar voice within the company wasn't entirely convinced it would work. During a recent episode of his What Happened When podcast, longtime AEW commentator Tony Schiavone reflected on the Clockwork Carnage match that headlined the October 17 pay-per-view.

The high-profile main event featured eight competitors battling under an elimination and points-based format, with Will Ospreay ultimately retaining the AEW World Championship against a star-studded field including Samoa Joe, Adam Copeland, Ricochet, Kazuchika Okada, Darby Allin, Konosuke Takeshita, and Mike Bailey.

According to Schiavone, there was widespread uncertainty about how the complex match would play out, even among those who had been briefed on the rules and format beforehand. He admitted that nobody in the back truly knew what to expect despite holding meetings to explain the proceedings, leading him to privately think that the ambitious experiment could really not turn out too good.

Despite his initial reservations and doubts going into the broadcast, Schiavone came away thoroughly impressed with how the match unfolded in practice, particularly praising the dramatic moments during its closing stretch. He explained that while he appreciates the athleticism, technical aspects, work rate, and selling that go into making a good wrestling match, the storytelling and drama are equally important to him. He felt the bout delivered heavily in that regard and considered the experimental format a major success.

Schiavone concluded by acknowledging that he purposely avoids looking at social media and therefore remained unaware of how the experimental match format was received online by the broader fan base.

Tony Khan discusses Adam Copeland and Christian Cage in AEW

AEW CEO Tony Khan recently addressed major updates regarding Adam Copeland and Christian Cage during a media call following an emotional week for professional wrestling fans. October 14 brought the revelation of Adam Copeland's plan to officially retire from in-ring competition in 2027. While no definitive time or place has been attached to the sendoff yet, Khan assured that the time leading up to it will be memorable.

Absolutely I knew Adam was going to say that 2027 is his last year, Khan said on the AEW WrestleDream media call, referencing Copeland's announcement made on television. I'm very close with Adam. I speak to Adam very regularly, and of course, as the producer of the show, I'm going to talk to him about what he wants to say on the microphone.

I think he'll have a great run here throughout the next year, Khan continued. Into 2027, I don't know how much longer exactly Adam will want to go, but I would say this is what he talked to me about, that 2027 was going to be the last year he would want to wrestle. I've accepted that. I think that Adam is somebody that has accomplished so much in wrestling.

Copeland, a former world champion in WWE, initially retired from wrestling in 2011 due to spinal stenosis before making a surprise return in the 2020 Men's Royal Rumble. After wrestling for WWE for an additional three years, he jumped to AEW in the fall of 2023. In 2026, he reunited with his long-time friend Christian Cage, and the duo enjoyed a reign as AEW Tag Team Champions.

Their reign came to an end at All Out 2026 in a brutal match that saw Cage sustain multiple broken ribs and a collapsed lung. The injuries occurred after Cage was thrown into a ladder about five minutes into their AEW Tag Team Championship Ladder Match on September 26. Khan addressed Cage's ongoing absence from programming during the media call.

Christian Cage is recovering from those injuries, Khan said. He's one of the toughest people, but he fought through and had one of the greatest wrestling matches ever. When you know that he hurt himself and he tried to power through, and frankly, shake off the medical team, saying he wasn't hurt and downplay it and say that he could continue. Really, nobody realized until after. Then to be worried about him, somebody that outside of the ring I think we all love and inside of the ring we all love, Adam said it so well, that was something we weren't expecting.

Copeland delivered the news of Cage's status during his recent promo. On the same media call, Khan confirmed that he originally factored Cage into the subsequent pay-per-view, WrestleDream, on October 17. However, Cage's injuries render him unable to compete right now, and the timeline for his recovery remains unclear.

In Cage's absence, Copeland entered the Clockwork Carnage match at WrestleDream with the aim of capturing the AEW World Championship. His pursuit came close to success, but ultimately fell short when defending champion Will Ospreay clocked him with a Hidden Blade.

Conrad then mentioned his close ties with Cody Rhodes and Charlotte, as well as having one of my best friends who helps run creative at WWE. Declaring himself Switzerland regarding any allegiance to a wrestling company, Thompson stated that he just shared his personal wrestling opinion and people got bent out of shape about it while he found the whole situation amusing.

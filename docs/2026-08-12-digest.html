🔥 New Day Jericho Cruise AEW

Kofi Kingston and Xavier Woods Make Jericho Cruise Debut Amid AEW Speculation

Just over three months after leaving WWE, Kofi Kingston and Xavier Woods have resurfaced with a notable appearance on Chris Jericho's upcoming Rock 'N' Wrestling Rager at Sea. Chris Jericho officially announced the additions on Instagram, with Kingston now billed simply as "Kofi" and Woods using the name Austin Creed. The announcement read: "It's a brand new day and we are beyond excited to announce that Kofi and Austin Creed are making their Jericho Cruise debut! Get ready for Unicorns, Trombones, Gaming, Pancakes and so much more." Jericho leaned directly into their established entertainment personas and signature elements from their time together as The New Day.

The event, now in its seventh edition, will sail from Miami to the Bahamas from November 7 through November 11, 2026. Kingston and Woods have been announced as "Super Massive Guest Captains." The duo joins other newly announced additions including Harley Cameron, Lady Frost, and Myla Grace. The broader cruise lineup also features wrestling legends The Great Muta and "Million Dollar Man" Ted DiBiase, as well as The Hardys and Elayna Black from TNA, among numerous other performers.

Kingston and Woods are believed to have entered free agency around the beginning of August after departing WWE earlier in the year. The longtime New Day members left WWE in May after reportedly opting for their releases rather than accepting pay cuts. The pair surfaced publicly on July 29 with the release of their well-received animated short titled Daybreak, which depicts their escape from the circus and served as a creative nod that kept fans talking after their WWE exit. The video also honored their friend Big E, who remains with WWE in a broadcasting role after having to retire due to the broken neck he suffered in 2022.

The cruise booking has naturally reignited speculation about a possible AEW future for the former New Day stars. However, nothing has been officially confirmed regarding either wrestler signing with the promotion. With the duo's reported non-compete period now behind them, every move they make is likely to be viewed through a different lens. As of this writing, neither has officially confirmed where they will wrestle next.

The timing of the cruise announcement is particularly noteworthy given the broader context of speculation surrounding Kingston and Woods' next destination. Reports have repeatedly floated AEW as their likely landing spot, and the expectation is that they could eventually provide another boost to the company's tag team division, which recently added Motor City Machine Guns. Earlier reporting indicated that Kingston and Woods have discussed a new presentation they want to test in AEW and have a number of ideas for things they wanted to try and presentations they wanted to do that they never got to in WWE.

While the cruise itself is not exclusively an AEW show and wrestlers from outside the company have appeared on it before, the connection remains significant. Jericho is one of AEW's biggest names and most visible ambassadors, and the fact that Kingston and Woods are making their first Jericho Cruise appearance while free agents and amid constant AEW speculation keeps the rumor mill churning. Some observers have noted that the timing could hardly be better if they wanted to keep everyone guessing about their future direction.

The announcement has sparked considerable discussion among wrestling fans, though it still leaves one important question unanswered about the duo's next destination. Their appearance may not be a ticket to AEW Dynamite just yet, but it certainly demonstrates that Kingston and Woods remain active in exploring opportunities and maintaining their profile during this pivotal period. The breadcrumbs regarding their potential AEW future are not exactly getting harder to follow, but until an official announcement is made, any link between this cruise appearance and an eventual AEW debut remains speculation.

🔥 AEW Indie Promotion Partnership

On September 9, AEW will present a special "Rebel Heart" edition of Dynamite from Akins Ford Arena in Athens, Georgia to raise awareness and funds for ALS in honor of Rebel (Tanea Brooks), an AEW original who announced in May that she had been diagnosed with the terminal disease. The special show got even bigger when One Fall Wrestling (1FW) announced it will hold a special television taping prior to and following "Rebel Heart Dynamite."

1FW is owned by AEW Vice President of Show and Creative Coordination QT Marshall and was the first indie promotion to join MyAEW's streaming platform. In a press release announcing the 1FW taping, the promotion stated that this opportunity will allow their fans to see their favorite wrestlers on the biggest stage in 1FW's history while AEW fans can discover new talent and see familiar faces while also supporting Rebel.

The special television taping will take place both before and after AEW Dynamite, giving 1FW fans the opportunity to see their favorite stars compete on an expanded platform, while giving AEW fans the chance to see familiar faces and discover the champions and rising stars who have helped make 1FW Georgia's number one promotion.

1FW began fundraising efforts at their live event on August 7 and continued collecting donations at their live events through September 4. Fans who donate will be entered into one of three drawings to win tickets to the September 9 show. Donations raised by 1FW fans will be matched by the promotion's ownership and anonymous donors.

In conjunction with the Rebel Heart show, AEW will be making donations to charity organizations I Am ALS and the Team Gleason Foundation, two groups that are fighting to put an end to ALS (amyotrophic lateral sclerosis). The disease is fatal and does not currently have a cure. AEW also has Rebel Heart T-shirts and bracelets on sale to benefit these two charities.

QT Marshall expressed his gratitude for the opportunity. "I'd like to personally thank Tony Khan and AEW for giving 1FW the opportunity to be part of such an important night," Marshall said in the press release. "For Tony to allow us to showcase 1FW before and after Dynamite means a great deal to everyone at 1FW. More importantly, this is about coming together for Rebel and supporting the fight against ALS."

There is no Collision taping the night of the Rebel Heart show, which allows 1FW to have this spot. The Saturday, September 12 edition of Collision will take place live from MassMutual Center in Springfield, Massachusetts instead.

Anthony Henry AEW Surgery

AEW and ROH star Anthony Henry is going under the knife once again. Henry revealed in an Instagram reel that the past two years of his career have been rough to say the least, and is set to have surgery to correct a complication that stemmed from a previous procedure to repair his torn bicep.

He has since developed Heterotopic Ossification, a condition where bones grow in places they shouldn't, such as in soft body tissue, or in Henry's case, on top of other bones. This has caused Henry to not fully rotate his right arm properly as a crossbridge has formed in his forearm, making it virtually impossible to turn his right hand palm up.

The AEW star explained that he has been able to wrestle with this condition, but he has only been able to perform at around 60 percent of what he actually wants to perform at. Henry is hoping that this surgery will be the answer to all of his problems.

Henry is one half of The Workhorsemen tag team with JD Drake, who have wrestled sporadically on ROH on HonorClub throughout 2026. The duo last appeared on AEW programming during the Beach Break edition of AEW Dynamite where they lost to Jon Moxley and Will Ospreay, but that was their only AEW appearance so far this year. Henry has been able to wrestle in a handful of singles matches despite his condition, with the most recent coming against Juice Robinson on the July 31 episode of ROH on HonorClub, but the problems he has faced have caused him to work a lot less this past year.

Henry has had a lot of bad luck with injuries over the past few years, with the most notable being a broken jaw that he suffered in early 2024. This was notable as he was actually released from his AEW contract while he was injured, but AEW President Tony Khan quickly backtracked and offered Henry a new deal.

🔄 המשך 🔥 Darby Allin AEW World Title Run

Darby Allin has made peace with the brevity of his AEW World Championship reign, which lasted just 39 days before he lost the title back to MJF at Double or Nothing. Speaking on The Mark Hoke Show, Allin explained his philosophy around the short but intense title run, emphasizing that the length of the reign mattered far less than how he spent it.

"You live by the sword, you die by the sword," Allin said. "So a short and sweet championship run—that's my motto in life. You just got to bring it, man. But I honestly have no regrets. I would not change a single thing."

Allin revealed the emotional toll of achieving his longtime goal. He had spent years chasing the world championship since AEW's inception in 2019, finally winning it in his hometown. By the time he held the title, he had exhausted himself emotionally and physically during his promo before the match.

Val Venis is now blaming a COVID vaccine for the heart problems that ended Triple H's wrestling career, and he isn't buying Triple H's own explanation for what happened.

Venis took to Twitter and claimed, without providing evidence, that Triple H's 2021 cardiac issues were caused by a COVID vaccine, calling the vaccine a "Hebrew poison jab." He even directly challenged Triple H's previous statement that genetics played a role.

"I highly suspect the career of WWE star Triple H was cut short by the 'Hebrew' poison jab. Paul can claim his heart issue was 'genetics' all he wants. It's a shame he does not warn others to not take the 'Hebrew' poison jab."

That's a pretty wild accusation to make about someone else's medical emergency, especially when Triple H has already explained what happened to him.

The Game suffered a serious cardiac event in September 2021 that ultimately ended his in-ring career. He later revealed that he had a defibrillator implanted in his chest and officially announced his retirement the following year. Triple H gave fans another update in April 2025 and said the device had never needed to activate because his heart was doing perfectly fine.

"I have a defibrillator in my chest. I don't think about it… and it's never gone off and my heart's 100%. And I'm healthy."

Venis apparently has his own diagnosis. Triple H says genetics played a role in the heart problems that ended his career. Venis says he doesn't believe him and is blaming the COVID vaccine instead. He didn't provide any evidence for that claim, but that certainly didn't stop him from putting it on Twitter.

🔄 המשך Mickie James ALS Support

Mickie James has now completed the Ice Bucket Challenge fundraiser she launched in July to benefit ALS research. The TNA Hall of Famer shared video footage on social media showing the contest winner dumping the ice bucket on her live at WrestleCon Minneapolis.

"If you know me you know how much i HAAAATEEE cold," James wrote in the caption. "But this was worth it for such an amazing cause! Just reminding ya'll you can still support Rebel Tanea Brooks & I AM ALS. Thank you again to everyone who turned out in support!"

The fundraiser allowed fans to donate $5 for digital raffle entries, with one winner selected to perform the ice bucket challenge on James at the wrestling convention. All proceeds benefited I AM ALS, a patient-led organization dedicated to advancing ALS research, advocacy, and community support. James created the challenge specifically to support her friend Rebel, who is currently battling terminal ALS, while raising awareness and critical funds for the disease.

🔄 המשך GCW Avery Styles Future

GCW promoter Brett Lauderdale has weighed in directly on the Avery Styles situation with a series of tweets defending the young wrestler while acknowledging the unique pressures he faces.

Lauderdale stated that while Styles is understandably under a microscope because of his famous father, he deserves the same consideration afforded to any teenager starting their wrestling career. He acknowledged that Avery will make mistakes along the way, as all rookies do, but noted the difference is that he will be held to a higher standard due to his lineage.

The GCW promoter directly addressed claims that Avery is receiving bookings based on nepotism. Lauderdale countered that he has never seen a wrestler with fewer than 10 matches display such natural talent and ability. He emphasized that Avery was highly recommended and endorsed by people whose opinions he values, and stated plainly: "There is no privilege here. If he sucked, he wouldn't be here."

Avery's first two GCW matches occurred at the end of July and start of August, with a victory over Alexander Lev followed by a loss to Marcus Mathers. With AJ Styles now in a behind-the-scenes role at WWE following his retirement from active competition earlier this year, speculation has emerged that family tag team matches could potentially entice the legendary father to return for occasional special appearances.

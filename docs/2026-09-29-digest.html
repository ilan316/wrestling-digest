🔥 Andy Butcher Williams Memorial Plans

Plans for a permanent memorial honoring the late Andy Williams, widely known to wrestling fans as AEW is The Butcher and to music fans as the guitarist for Every Time I Die, have been officially unveiled. The memorial, featuring a statue of Williams sitting on a bench with his tattooed arm thrown around whoever sits next to him, is planned for Gratwick Riverside Park in his hometown of North Tonawanda, New York.

Jordan Buckley, a founding member of Every Time I Die alongside Williams, announced the project on social media, writing that they are building a statue of Andy and encouraging fans to get bricked up for the big man by putting their names and messages on the path leading to his bench. The memorial will be located along the Niagara River Greenway, honoring Williams impact across both the music and wrestling worlds. Williams was born in Buffalo and grew up in the North Tonawanda area, establishing deep ties to the Western New York community long before his wrestling career. Before becoming familiar to wrestling audiences, he built a successful career in music as a founding member of the Buffalo-based hardcore band Every Time I Die in the late 1990s, later bringing his distinctive presence from the music stage into the wrestling ring.

Fans have an opportunity to become part of the permanent memorial themselves. Personalized bricks are available for purchase on the AndyWilliamsForever website, allowing supporters to add their name or a custom message to the pathway leading toward the bench. Each custom brick costs $206, and a variety of other merchandise is also available, with 100 percent of the proceeds being donated by the Williams family to various organizations and charities of their choice.

The memorial announcement came shortly after family, friends, and peers gathered in Buffalo for a public celebration of Williams life, featuring speeches, personal tributes, and musical performances that were also livestreamed. Williams collapsed on September 5 after a match in Millvale, Pennsylvania, and was pronounced dead at Allegheny General Hospital on September 6. The September 12 edition of AEW Collision was dedicated to his memory, featuring a Butcher Battle Royal won by fellow Buffalo native Daniel Garcia. With the win, Garcia earned the right to donate $100,000 to a charity of his choosing in Williams name, ultimately selecting MusiCares, an organization that provides financial assistance, healthcare, mental health support, addiction recovery, and disaster relief to everyone working in music. The upcoming North Tonawanda memorial ensures that for a man who made a massive impact both onstage and inside the ring, his legacy will remain firmly planted in familiar territory.

Riho's AEW Return Status

Current Status of Riho’s AEW Return Revealed After Lengthy Absence

Riho’s AEW return still doesn’t appear to have a timetable, and right now there isn’t even a clear indication that creative plans are being put together for the former AEW Women’s World Champion. She remains under contract with the company, but her lengthy disappearance from television continues with no confirmed return date in sight.

Sean Ross Sapp addressed Riho’s status during a late September Q and A after being asked when fans could expect to see her back. Sapp said he hasn’t heard anything recently pointing toward Riho returning, and he hasn’t even heard about her being backstage at AEW events.

I haven’t heard a single thing about her returning as of late. I haven’t heard of her being at shows or anything like that.

The biggest mystery is why Riho has remained away for so long. Sapp said he doesn’t know whether AEW is dealing with a visa situation, an injury, or whether Riho is simply taking extended time away, but he confirmed that her contract with AEW is still active.

I do not know if it’s a visa thing, an injury thing or she just has time off, but she’s still under AEW contract. I just haven’t heard any creative plans mentioned for her so far.

That leaves Riho in a strange spot. She hasn’t officially left AEW, but there also doesn’t appear to be a storyline waiting for her based on what Sapp has heard, and there’s currently no indication that her comeback is around the corner. Sapp also looked back at just how little Riho has wrestled over the past several years. He recalled her making a handful of appearances last year, including a match with Mercedes Mone, but noted that even those matches came after another very long stretch away from the ring.

She last wrestled or appeared, I want to say like October, November of last year. So that’s, that’s the last. I haven’t heard of much of anything else. I know there were a lot of talent have visa issues, but I don’t know if she’s one of them.

Riho’s sporadic schedule has become the bigger story. Even when she resurfaced last year, Sapp noted that she had already gone well over a year without wrestling before those appearances.

But even when she was around last year, I, I want to say she did two matches in September, maybe three because she did face Mercedes as well. I remember that she did a couple in November and she had not been around and wrestled at all in like 14, 15 months before that.

When everything is added together, Sapp said Riho has essentially been removed from regular AEW activity for years.

So really, Riho has been pretty much out of it for two and a half years at this stage.

For now, Riho is still an AEW wrestler on paper, but fans waiting for her comeback don’t have much to grab onto. There’s no confirmed injury explanation, no confirmed visa explanation, no return date, and most importantly, no creative plans that Sapp has heard about, leaving one of AEW’s original women’s division stars stuck in limbo for the time being.

MJF Tops PWI 500

Pro Wrestling Illustrated released its annual PWI 500 list, with former AEW World Champion MJF claiming the number one spot for the very first time. Despite the milestone, MJF expressed that the accolade was overdue.

I will be honest, my first thought was what took so long, MJF said in an interview. My second thought was some people are slow learners. But we got there, we got where we needed to be, we got MJF the number one spot. Is there an argument it should have happened a long time ago? Sure, people are talking about it but that's fine. We got there.

The evaluation period for the list spanned from August 1, 2025, to July 31, 2026. During this timeframe, MJF solidified his status as a central figure in All Elite Wrestling by headlining four AEW pay-per-views with the AEW World Championship on the line, winning every single one of those bouts. He accumulated two reigns with the AEW World Championship totaling 154 days, alongside nine successful title defenses. Beyond AEW, MJF captured the CMLL World Light Heavyweight Championship on August 1, 2025, holding it until mid-September before dropping it to Mistico in one of CMLL's most critically acclaimed matches of 2025.

Having previously finished sixth in 2023 and seventh in 2024, MJF dethroned former mentor Cody Rhodes, who dropped to second place after topping the list for the previous two years. Former rivals CM Punk and Mistico rounded out the top four at third and fourth respectively, with Konosuke Takeshita taking fifth. Another major rival from the past year, Hangman Page, placed sixth, followed by Yota Tsuji in seventh. The remainder of the top ten featured Trick Williams in eighth, Oba Femi in ninth, and GUNTHER in tenth.

Former WWE Stars Signing with AEW in 2026

With the end of 2026 approaching, former WWE stars continue to feature in future plans for AEW, as seen in a recent addition. Dani Luna is far from the only former WWE star to sign a contract with the company, with others having put pen to paper following their departure.

Dani Luna is the most recent addition to the roster, debuting at All Out in a losing effort to TBS Champion Persephone. Luna is a former WWE and TNA Wrestling star who was released from her WWE contract in August 2022, going on to sign with TNA Wrestling the following year and becoming Knockouts World Tag Team Champion with Jody Threat.

Tommaso Ciampa, a two-time WWE Tag Team Champion, exited the company after his contract expired towards the end of last year. He debuted with AEW in January and became TNT Champion shortly thereafter. After a run in 2005 that ended in 2007, Ciampa returned to WWE in 2015 alongside Johnny Gargano, with the pair being known as #DIY. A two-time NXT Champion, Ciampa also held the NXT Tag Team Championship before being called up to the main roster in 2022.

The Rascalz, featuring Dezmond Xavier, Zachary Wentz, and Myron Reed, have been All Elite since January of this year, making their debut on the Maximum Carnage episode of AEW Collision. Xavier and Wentz were known to WWE fans as Wes Lee and Nash Carter, respectively, serving as two-time NXT Tag Team Champions under the name MSK. Wentz was released from his WWE contract back in April 2022, later returning during his run in TNA Wrestling alongside Xavier, who was released in October of last year.

In December of last year, Mick Foley revealed he was cutting ties with WWE at the end of his legends contract, going on to make his AEW debut at Double or Nothing in May. Foley later revealed that it was a difficult decision to make when exiting WWE, having been associated with the company multiple times over the decades and for lengthy stretches.

Previously Kofi Kingston and Xavier Woods, the stars now known as The New Level made their AEW debuts at All In: London. For The New Day, their departure from WWE came after the veteran stars were offered a restructured deal that would have included a substantial pay cut, reported to be much higher than the 50 percent mark. Kofi and Austin Creed have wasted no time since debuting, now teaming with Swerve Strickland as AEW World Trios Champions.

The Motor City Machine Guns, consisting of Chris Sabin and Alex Shelley, signed with AEW in July of this year after being released from their WWE contracts in April. During their time in WWE, the pair held the Tag Team Championship for a period of 42 days starting shortly after their September 2024 signing. They competed in their first and, at the time of writing, only AEW match on September 5, with reports revealing that their absence was due to the pair wanting to finish their music tour as members of the rock band Grppling first.

The pipeline of talent between WWE and AEW remains open for good reason. For AEW, the chance to sign some of the more prominent names following their WWE departure makes sense, not just to attract fans, but when it comes to television ready talent. Ilja Dragunov is one such star expected to make an AEW debut soon, and there is every chance that he won’t be the only one as we approach the end of another year.

Top Young AEW Stars Under 30

It should come as no surprise that AEW has a wealth of talent yet to turn 30 years old, with Dani Luna being a recent addition. A former WWE and TNA Wrestling star, Dani Luna made her debut at AEW All Out, with the 27-year-old star answering the open challenge for Persephone’s TBS Championship. For Luna, she joins AEW at a time when many of its top stars are under the age of 30, including a number of standout male roster members.

Kyle Fletcher, born in December 1998, is just 27 years old and has held the AEW International Championship, along with being a two-time TNT Champion and a former World Trios Champion. Over in Ring of Honor, his accolades include the ROH World Tag Team Championship and World Television Championship. On a career high in AEW, the star has previously spoken about opting to sign with Tony Khan over a deal with WWE during an interview with Chris Van Vliet. Describing how AEW was the right fit for himself and Mark Davis, Fletcher would reflect on the vibe in AEW and his ability to produce the wrestling that he wants to deliver. Fletcher continues to excel in AEW and it can only be a matter of time before that top title ends up in his hands.

At 29, Gabe Kidd forged an impressive career in NJPW before signing with AEW earlier this year. Already an AEW World Trios Champion, alongside fellow members of The Dogs, David Finlay and Clark Connors, Kidd is also a member of Death Riders, offering even more opportunity to showcase the star with his brash attitude that matches his in-ring style. At some point, AEW would do well to deliver Gabe Kidd versus Will Ospreay, particularly with the animosity both stars have shown towards each other. In the meantime, there are plenty of championships that Kidd could challenge for as a singles competitor.

Another star at 29 years old, Jack Perry is an AEW OG, one of the four pillars and currently on a quest to become world champion. A former TNT Champion, National Champion, FTW Champion and World Tag Team Champion, Perry has all the tools to succeed but is yet to get over the line when it comes to the top title. Gone is the Jungle Boy persona that won him fans during his earlier years, with the star having passed through his Scapegoat phase following the CM Punk fallout and been reborn as Jungle Jack Perry, incorporating nods to both of his past gimmicks.

Known as the Cold-Hearted Handsome Devil of AEW, HOOK made his debut in November 2020, being introduced as a silent and aggressive machine. No longer silent, HOOK has gone on to be a three-time FTW Champion and a vital component in The Opps, losing none of the technical prowess he brings to the ring. At 27 years of age, HOOK is another star that Tony Khan clearly sees as a valuable asset for the future of AEW and it wouldn’t be surprising to see titles around his waist in the next few years.

Just 21 years of age, there is no stopping Nick Wayne, who signed a contract back in February 2022 and made his debut after finishing school in July the following year. A former AEW World Trios Champion, Wayne has seen his talents brought to NJPW, offering an opportunity that is open to very few young talents from outside Japan. Most recently competing as a singles competitor, Wayne may have recently lost to Chris Jericho on the September 16 episode of Dynamite, but he is likely to rise up through the ranks to one day stand alongside the veteran star.

It doesn’t end there for the under-30 division. Also yet to turn 30 are the likes of Dante and Darius Martin, Hologram, Komander, Action Andretti, Daniel Garcia, and others currently under AEW contract. Across the board, in a dense men’s roster, used in the right way, any of these stars could be elevated to even greater heights on television long before their thirtieth birthday.

Will Ospreay vs. Kenny Omega All In Match Standard

Jeff Jarrett believes Will Ospreay and Kenny Omega gave All Elite Wrestling a match that could become a standard for the promotion. He stopped short of declaring their All In main event the company definitive in-ring performance, pointing instead to the match timing and the meaning it held for the people around Ospreay. Jarrett discussed the August 30 Wembley Stadium match on a September 24 clip from his podcast, My World with Jeff Jarrett.

Co-host Conrad Thompson asked whether Ospreay AEW World Championship win could stand as the promotion gold standard. Jarrett responded that only time will tell, explaining that he uses that simplistic reply because nobody knows what the future holds. Jarrett mentioned he really enjoyed talking to Andy Quildan, the promoter of RevPro and the person who really gave Will Ospreay the real platform to develop his career. Hearing Andy talk about the significance that he wanted to be on the Wembley Stadium floor watching it live led Jarrett to ponder if this will be the event that plants the flag in the ground and becomes the gold standard.

Jarrett also compared the match with the body of work AEW has produced since its launch. He saw the competitive dynamic and Ospreay winning on home turf as reasons the performance could endure beyond one night. When considering if this will be the match that sets the standard bearer of AEW, Jarrett noted that you have to compare the last five, six, or seven years of wrestling, acknowledging there have been a lot of great matches. However, he believes the unique set of circumstances, the desire to go out there and tear the house down, and the drive to be as competitive as possible allowed both men to deliver.

They both dug deep and went out there knowing they were going to leave it all in the ring, culminating in Will getting his hand raised on his home turf. Jarrett thinks it definitely has the opportunity to be the standard bearer because the timing of the event is significant, not just internally for AEW but around the world, noting that it simply came at the right time.

Jarrett likened the role of timing in wrestling to Hulk Hogan and Andre the Giant at WrestleMania 3. His point was strictly about whether a major performance lands at a moment people continue to remember, rather than a claim that the two matches were identical. He reiterated that the match definitely has an opportunity, but only time will tell, emphasizing that Will and Kenny did everything in their power for people to even be able to sit around and have that conversation.

Ospreay defeated Omega to win the AEW World Championship in the All In main event. Jarrett assessment was ultimately about the match possible place in company history rather than a prediction about either wrestler next program.

Volador Jr joined on March 30, 2026, when Rocky Romero announced on CMLL's social media channels that Volador Jr had joined the Don Callis Family, teaming with El Clon and Hechicero in Arena Mexico.

Kevin Knight joined on June 3, 2026. The latest member of the Don Callis Family, the Jet joined the Family following his betrayals of Darby Allin and Speedball Mike Bailey earlier this year. 🔄 המשך 🔥 PAC Passing and Tributes

New details and widespread industry tributes continue to emerge following the sudden passing of Benjamin Satterley. Authorities in Des Plaines, Illinois, reported that Satterley was found alone and unresponsive in a rental vehicle parked outside a local restaurant near O'Hare International Airport on Sunday afternoon. First responders arrived following a 911 call from a passerby, and while an initial autopsy has been conducted, his official cause of death remains pending further testing that could take several weeks. Police emphasized that there were no signs of foul play, physical trauma, or suspicious circumstances surrounding the incident.

In the wake of the tragedy, wrestling promotions and colleagues have mounted extensive tributes celebrating his career and character. WWE opened Monday Night Raw with a memorial graphic honoring Satterley under his Neville moniker, accompanied by personal statements from Chief Content Officer Triple H and peers like John Cena, Rusev, and Enzo Amore, who reflected on their close friendship and his immense humility.

In AEW, President Tony Khan delivered an emotional ten-bell salute while wearing a black armband during Ring of Honor television tapings, announcing that Wednesday's episode of Dynamite in Pittsburgh will serve as a dedicated tribute show with voluntary roster participation. Ring of Honor and CMLL have also opened their archives to stream classic matches in his honor, while international promotions including New Japan Pro-Wrestling, Dragon Gate, and CMLL joined countless wrestlers in mourning a performer widely regarded as one of the most talented and respected of his generation.

🔄 המשך 🔥 Steven Borden Injury at AEW All Out

Backstage details and reactions continue to emerge following Steven Borden's frightening mid-air collision and subsequent referee stoppage.

According to backstage reports, Borden was very much on board with attempting the coast-to-coast spot with Kevin Knight and pushed to include it in the match. The visual inside the ring was particularly alarming for those watching live, as Borden briefly appeared to display a fencing response after hitting the canvas.

The incident immediately ignited external debate over whether the less-experienced wrestler was thrust onto a major pay-per-view stage prematurely. Defenders have pushed back on that narrative, highlighting that Borden has spent over two years training, accumulated roughly three dozen matches, and possesses a Division 1 athletic background that makes him exceptionally gifted physically.

The spot also drew heavy criticism from external figures, including former referee Jimmy Korderas, who blamed management for allowing such a dangerous sequence. That criticism prompted a sharp response from AEW's Ricochet, who blasted the former referee on social media and insisted that no one is at fault. Drawing parallels to his own famous mid-air collision with Logan Paul, Ricochet noted that extensive rehearsal does not eliminate risk, emphasizing that injuries can happen on both high-risk and low-risk maneuvers every time performers step into the ring.

🔄 המשך Ilja Dragunov, Tommaso Ciampa and more pay tribute to PAC

As the wrestling community processes the tragic and sudden loss of Benjamin Satterley, more stars from across the industry have come forward to share their heartbreak and memories of the beloved performer.

Tommaso Ciampa penned an emotional, lengthy farewell recalling a conversation they shared when another colleague passed. Ciampa remembered Satterley as a unicorn, describing him as the most humble person he had ever met, and expressed deep sorrow over losing a cherished friend whose voice he could still vividly hear.

Ilja Dragunov remembered him as generational, unbelievable, and one of a kind. WWE veteran Natalya reflected on his time in NXT, noting his passion for the industry and the immense respect he commanded from everyone around him. TNA star Ash By Elegance, who worked with him for years under the name Neville, praised his unmatched athleticism and professionalism, calling him a true class act.

Additional tributes poured in from across the global wrestling landscape. Anna Jay expressed that she was feeling sick over the news, while former WWE star Sheamus, The Usos, AEW World Champion Will Ospreay, Rhea Ripley, Paige, and numerous others shared their condolences and celebrated the lasting legacy of a universally respected competitor.

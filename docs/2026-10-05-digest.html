🔥 The Rock responds to report about WWE reaction to his AEW praise in PAC tribute

Dwayne The Rock Johnson has addressed rumors that people within WWE were surprised by his decision to openly praise AEW while paying tribute to the late PAC. The situation stems from a video tribute Johnson released following the death of Benjamin Satterley, known widely as PAC and Neville, who passed away at the age of 40. In his original message, the current TKO Board of Directors member spoke about the bond shared by wrestlers regardless of promotion, describing AEW as a phenomenal wrestling company and shouting out his friend Tony Khan.

Following the tribute, reports emerged indicating that some individuals within WWE were very surprised by Johnson's wording. The issue was reportedly not the tribute itself, but the specific praise given to AEW and the stamp of approval offered to the rival company, which some felt was not the message WWE wanted out. This led to online discussions, including an Instagram video addressing the situation and discussing the reported backstage reactions.

Johnson responded directly in the comments section of that Instagram video, making it clear how he views the matter and brushing off any corporate friction. He wrote that humanity matters to him, explaining that he was born into the business and has seen it all, but when one of our own passes away, he does not care nor does he see competition. Instead, he focuses on the wrestlers and how tragedy affects them, noting that he has been there and that humanity matters most above all else.

During his initial tribute, Johnson offered condolences to Satterley's family, Tony Khan, QT Marshall, the entire AEW locker room, and colleagues within WWE who knew PAC well. He emphasized that professional wrestling remains a tight-knit unit because everyone shares the same DNA, love, and passion for performing in the squared circle. This is not the first time Johnson has publicly spoken positively about WWE's rival, having previously confirmed during an April 2020 Instagram Live Q&A that he watched AEW and welcomed competition for creating hunger in the industry. Johnson joined the TKO Board of Directors in January 2024 and made his most recent WWE television appearance at Elimination Chamber in March 2025.

Samoa Joe thought his career was over after 2020 concussion

During a recent appearance at Grace Family Church, Samoa Joe looked back on the challenging period that began in February 2020 when he suffered a concussion while filming a commercial. Looking back on that period, the former world champion recalled dealing with the serious head injury, multiple firings, and wondering what came next before everything unexpectedly turned around.

The concussion forced him away from the ring and onto the commentary team. While he enjoyed the role, he admitted that, at the time, he thought his in-ring days were essentially over. Within the span of a couple of months, he had received the concussion, believed his career was done, and was transitioned to the commentary desk, which he described as an awesome and good time.

Things only became more chaotic from there as Joe was released by WWE during that period. He was later unexpectedly brought back in a corporate position. That second chance did not last very long, however, as another corporate restructuring led to him losing his job again just a couple of months later. He noted it was a rough morning, but his wife remained calm throughout the situation. When he told her he had lost his job again, she reassured him that everything would work out and that they had already prayed on it.

He admitted it took him years to develop that same level of faith, but eventually he realized she had been right all along. That confidence paid off almost immediately. Just four hours after being fired, Joe received an unexpected phone call from a friend he had not spoken to in upwards of 15 years, asking him to audition for a brand new television show for Peacock.

Joe landed the role in what would become Peacock's Twisted Metal. When he walked back into the room to tell his wife the news, she simply reminded him she had already told him everything would be okay. He told her he was now starring in a TV series, and she replied that she told him it would all work out.

Joe's story is a reminder of just how quickly things can change in the entertainment and professional wrestling industries. In the span of a few months, he went from believing his career was over and being fired multiple times to landing a starring television role before eventually returning to the ring and continuing one of the most successful careers in the business.

Jeff Jarrett names his AEW Mount Rushmore with Tony Khan at the top

Wrestling veteran and executive Jeff Jarrett has shared his picks for an AEW Mount Rushmore, though he believes company President Tony Khan deserves to be viewed as the mountain itself rather than just a face on the monument.

During a recent appearance on KickOut, Jarrett was asked to name the most important figures in the history of All Elite Wrestling. He began his selection by naming Chris Jericho, noting that the choice makes sense given his historical role, though he admitted the list could look entirely different three or four years from now. Jarrett then pointed to Sting as another essential figure who was difficult to leave off the list at this stage in the company's run.

Jarrett next turned his attention to the founding members and core stars of the promotion, specifically addressing The Elite. He noted that once you include The Young Bucks, it becomes impossible to leave out Kenny Omega and Hangman Adam Page. Rather than separating the group, Jarrett decided to count The Elite collectively alongside Jericho and Sting, describing that group as entirely integral to the promotion.

To round out his wrestler selections, Jarrett added MJF to the mix, highlighting his undeniable role in the growth and positioning of the company overall.

Despite naming several iconic wrestlers and key players, Jarrett reserved his highest praise for AEW President Tony Khan. Rather than placing Khan on the Mount Rushmore, Jarrett described him as the mountain itself, stating that none of this would have happened without his mindset and vision.

Jarrett joined AEW in 2022 and currently contributes to the company in both an on-screen and behind-the-scenes capacity. His extensive career in the professional wrestling industry, which includes launching and running TNA Wrestling alongside his lengthy in-ring tenure, was recently chronicled in a three-part Dark Side of the Ring special.

AEW's FTR explains how they fell in love with tag team wrestling

AEW stars Cash Wheeler and Dax Harwood of FTR recently explained what made them fall in love with tag team wrestling, revealing that their passion goes much deeper than simply finding a successful partner.

For Wheeler, his appreciation extends back to his childhood, where he and his brothers frequently fought in the backyard. In the year following, that passion for tag team wrestling evolved into something even bigger. Wheeler noted in a recent interview that the appeal comes from having extra bodies in the ring and utilizing misdirection behind the referee's back to draw attention where desired, highlighting that there is something special about having that type of chemistry when it is going well and done right.

For Harwood, his love for the art dates back to the era of the Super Nintendo. He recalled having friends over to play Saturday Night Slam Masters, where two of them could play on the same team while a third friend pretended to be the manager without a controller. Harwood stated that as weird as it sounds, that small bond made through the video game with three friends down the street is where he first fell in love with tag team wrestling.

Wheeler and Harwood originally joined forces in WWE as The Mechanics before becoming known as The Revival. During their time there, they captured the NXT, Raw, and SmackDown Tag Team Championships, making history as the first Triple Crown Tag Team Champions in WWE history.

Since signing with All Elite Wrestling in 2020, Wheeler and Harwood have continued to make history as FTR, becoming three-time AEW World Tag Team Champions. Their latest reign came to an end in May 2026 when Adam Edge Copeland and Christian Cage defeated them in an I Quit Match.

AEW's Thunder Rosa reveals conditions to do a Blood & Guts match

Thunder Rosa is no stranger to hardcore wrestling matches, but that does not mean she is yearning to step into another extreme bout anytime soon. During a recent interview, the former AEW Women's World Champion revealed what it would take for her to compete in another grueling stipulation match such as Blood & Guts.

Addressing the possibility of future extreme matches, Rosa explained that she prefers to take a step back from constant violence. She stated that she is going to chill on that one. While she loves doing those types of matches because she can be very creative, she feels that wrestlers can tell stories in a different way. According to Rosa, it would have to be a meaningful storyline for her to end up in a Lights Out match kind of thing. She noted that she simply does not want to randomly announce a Street Fight where everybody is bleeding just for the sake of it, emphasizing that she wants it to really mean something.

Earlier in her AEW career, Rosa famously battled Britt Baker in a brutal Lights Out Match that resulted in both competitors bleeding heavily. La Mera Mera ultimately won that acclaimed bout after sending Baker crashing through a table with a Fire Thunder Driver. In 2024, Rosa also tackled a violent Texas Bullrope Match against Deonna Purrazzo and again emerged victorious. Still, she admits that those physical affairs have sometimes caused her to question herself.

When asked if she has ever doubted her choices regarding such dangerous matches, Rosa admitted that she certainly has. However, she noted that her mindset shifts toward making the moment memorable. She wants the violence to mean something not only for herself and her partner, but also for the overall story and what they are trying to communicate to the audience.

Last year, twelve AEW stars competed in the first-ever women's Blood & Guts match, with Mercedes Mone, Thekla, Skye Blue, Megan Bayne, Marina Shafir, and Julia Hart picking up the win as a team. Whether the grueling stipulation will circle back to the women's division in the future, and possibly include Thunder Rosa, has yet to be seen.

Maria Kanellis talks abrupt departure from wrestling and future door

Maria Kanellis, known for her prominent stints in WWE, Ring of Honor, and All Elite Wrestling, recently opened up about her unceremonious exit from professional wrestling following the conclusion of her time with Tony Khan's company at the end of January 2025. Since stepping away from the squared circle, Kanellis has shifted her primary focus toward motherhood and building new business ventures outside of the sports entertainment industry.

During a recent appearance on the Wrestling Life Hall of Fame, as transcribed by Wrestling Inc., Kanellis spoke candidly about her current standing with the industry. She noted that she is not very involved with wrestling at all right now, though she was quick to clarify that the door is not completely shut on a future return because she is truly enjoying her new path.

Kanellis has successfully established her own florist business and has started working in event management. Additionally, she revealed that she is currently in the process of writing a book. Reflecting on her departure, Kanellis admitted that the abrupt manner in which her wrestling career concluded was not how she originally envisioned walking away, and it required significant time for her to process and work through those emotions.

She explained that it just kind of abruptly ended, and it took her a long time to move past that and be like, but I spent twenty years in it, why don't I have this send-off? She added that she ultimately did not receive a formal farewell, which she accepts, noting that it took a while to come to terms with the reality of it. However, she has reached a point where she is thankful for the abrupt ending because it provided her with the opportunity to explore and try other things.

While Kanellis believes there is a proper time and place for closure, she has not seen any compelling opportunities to return to the industry yet, mentioning that no company has reached out with a project that genuinely excites her. Even so, she remains open to the right scenario. She stated that if something comes along that she thinks will bring her joy, or if it gives her the opportunity to work with her Kingdom one more time, that would be the deciding factor for a potential comeback.

So I figured that as long as I was lucky enough to have this job, chances are I’d be floored by another wrestler gone too soon. And last Sunday it happened when the announcement came that Ben PAC Satterley was gone.

A lot of us watched him break down over the sudden loss of another wrestler, Andy The Butcher Williams, only a couple weeks ago. It only then dawned on me how rarely PAC, or Neville as he was known earlier in his career, broke character, and I know I wasn’t alone in that realization. I know I wasn’t alone in any of the emotions I experienced after we learned PAC died last Sunday afternoon, or while reading his friends and colleagues remembrances and memorials to him, or after watching an amazing, cathartic television broadcast. Because I shared that with all of you here in this community.

Now, admittedly the idea for this article came to me when I realized I’d done nothing for our new Mailbag feature this week, after promising it would be a weekly feature when we launched it just a couple weeks back. But I did already tell you I’ve been in a bit of a glass case of emotion. Anyway, this collection of comments and community posts about PAC isn’t a best of, nor does it include everything the community created this week that made me cry, smile, laugh, or shout, while helping me not focus on my sadness and anger, but to instead appreciate Satterley, and the other people and things I enjoy, or that otherwise make my life better or easier.

Hopefully you have or will find them somehow therapeutic, too. Share anything you came across from your fellow community members that was helpful or that you’d like to shout out. Thanks for being a part of it.

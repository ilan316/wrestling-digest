🔥 Chris Jericho on no longer main eventing AEW

Chris Jericho Discusses His Role in AEW's Evolution and Future

Chris Jericho spent AEW's early years planted near the top of the card, but he says those days are over and he is perfectly fine with it. Jericho believes AEW has finally built enough legitimate main-event talent that he can now wrestle anywhere on the show without feeling like the company needs him carrying the biggest programs.

During an appearance on Wrestling Observer Radio, Jericho started talking about just how many wrestlers are currently sitting on AEW's roster that he could still work with. He mentioned Hechicero, Andrade, Kevin Knight, Ricochet, Tommaso Ciampa, Jack Perry, Mark Davis, Kyle Fletcher and The Rascalz before making it clear that he will take whatever Tony Khan throws at him.

"I've always said, whatever Tony wants me to do, I'm happy to do it. Whatever, man. Whatever you want," Jericho stated.

That was not always possible. Jericho admitted that four or five years ago, AEW still needed him around the main event because the company was trying to establish enough wrestlers who could eventually take over those spots.

"Even four or five years ago, I pretty much had to be in the main event or one of the main-event components because we were still building other main eventers," he explained.

Jericho believes that job has now been done. AEW has enough wrestlers capable of leading the company that he does not care whether he opens the show, wrestles in the middle or closes the night.

"Now we're getting to the point where there's so many guys that can solidly lead the charge that I can be opening match, middle match, semi-main event, main event, sometimes—whatever it is you want me to do," Jericho said.

Jericho does not see moving around the card as a demotion either. After 36 years in wrestling, he believes his value comes from being somebody AEW can throw into just about any situation and trust him to make it work.

"Because I have the experience of 36 years to know how to handle that, it still makes me valuable on the roster. Like, whatever you need, I'll make it good," he stated.

That mindset is nothing new for Jericho. He said Vince McMahon used to give him basically the same marching orders in WWE, even when the creative sounded completely insane.

"That's what Vince used to say. Like, 'I just booked it. You make it good.' Like, how am I supposed to make this good? You've got the Planet of the Apes coming down and a cake that's supposed to be thrown in Stephanie's face. 'I don't know, Chris. Make it good.' And you do your best, and sometimes it's good and sometimes you tried," Jericho recalled.

Jericho said that is essentially where he sees himself in AEW now—the veteran Tony Khan can hand something to when the company needs somebody capable of making an idea work.

"At least, you know, like, okay, we need something done. Give the torch to Jericho and he can go handle it. So it's kind of the way that I feel about AEW right now because there's so much depth on that roster," he explained.

Jericho clearly knows AEW does not need him occupying a main-event slot every week anymore, and he seems to view that as proof the company has grown rather than a knock against his own position. After helping AEW establish its first wave of stars, Jericho now appears comfortable moving around the card and filling whatever role the company needs.

The Decision to Stay in AEW

Chris Jericho says his history with AEW played a major role in his decision to remain with the company. While discussing his decision to stay during his appearance on Wrestling Observer Radio, Jericho explained that he weighed his options before ultimately deciding that his investment in the promotion made staying the right choice.

"When it was time to kind of start thinking about coming back, obviously you weigh your options. And there is a lot of factors, but the bottom line is this, and this was the one that really clinched it for me, was I've got skin in the game in AEW," Jericho stated.

Jericho pointed to his involvement with AEW from the beginning, noting that the company was not established before he became one of its first major stars.

"I helped start AEW, and in a lot of ways, if there was no Chris Jericho, there might not have been an AEW. And there certainly wasn't one before me. There was a WWE before Chris Jericho. There was a New Japan before. There was a CMLL before. And there'll be an AEW after I leave, obviously," he said.

The former AEW World Champion added that he remains excited about the company's future and enjoys working with AEW President Tony Khan. He also acknowledged that the two sides had some things to work through before reaching an agreement.

"And I can't wait to see how much bigger the company gets over the next 10 years, etc. But when it came down to it, it was like, I like working for Tony. We had to work some things out, which we did. It wasn't hard to do," Jericho explained.

Future Prospects and Available Opponents

During his Wrestling Observer Radio appearance, Jericho revealed several wrestlers in AEW that he would still like to work with. He mentioned that he had pitched the idea of wrestling Kevin Knight on an episode of Dynamite from Las Vegas.

Jericho later named Ricochet, Jack Perry, Mark Davis, Kyle Fletcher and The Rascalz as people he could potentially work with, either wrestling against or teaming with them.

"Kevin Knight, I would love to do more with him. I'd love to be a tag team with Ricochet. I'd love to be a tag team with Ciampa. I mean, I could do something with Jack Perry. I could work with Mark Davis. I could work with Kyle Fletcher. I could work with The Rascalz. The Rascalz could be like my minions," Jericho said.

He also expressed interest in having matches with Hechicero and Andrade, further demonstrating his enthusiasm for exploring fresh matchups on the AEW roster despite his shift away from main event prominence.

🔶 AEW News

Billy Gunn refused to ask Tony Khan to hire his sons

Billy Gunn says he refused to get his sons hired by AEW. A part of the promotion since 2020, Billy Gunn was never in favor of his sons getting hired by Tony Khan before they graduated from college. Speaking in a recent interview, Gunn opened up and shared that he refused to use his influence to get his sons hired by AEW, and instead wanted them to finish college first.

I am not that guy anyway. Like if you suck, I am telling you, you suck. I wanted my kids to actually suck so they did not do this job. But unfortunately, their dad was in DX. But that doesn’t matter. Just because I am good at something doesn’t mean they are going to be good at something, Gunn said.

First of all, they had to graduate from college. That was number one. Don’t even come to me. Don’t even attempt to do this job if I don’t get that. Austin, the day he graduated, walked from the stage up the balcony where I was sitting, handed me this and goes, When do we start?

He further discussed that while his other son, Colten, had everything planned out for him till he was 60, it still shocked both him and his wife when Colten also decided he wanted to wrestle.

They have known that because they have lived it their entire life, is me never being around. Like never being home. It is a little easier now, but when I first started, I was on the road 300 days out of the year, Gunn said when asked why he didn’t want his sons in wrestling. That is insane. That is zero life at home. That is zero seeing your kids do anything. That is zero seeing anybody do anything because when you go home, you sleep the entire time because you don’t do that on the road. So I don’t want that for my kids. I want better things for my kids like we all do.

Despite training his sons, Gunn wanted them to earn an AEW tryout first. Saying he handed his sons nothing, Gunn added that he did teach and train them up until a certain point to where they were getting the feel of it.

And then I would send them away to people that I trusted to kind of just get a different perspective. But they had to go to Tony. They had to ask to get a tryout. They had tryouts. They did all that. I didn’t go, Hey, would you hire my kids because they are my kids? No, that ain’t me, he shared.

The veteran added that he did not visit AEW asking them to hire his kids, maintaining the belief that one had to earn their opportunity and work for it.

Too many people just get it handed to them and they don’t understand the struggle it takes to do this job.

The Gunn family has been a prominent fixture in AEW since 2020, with Austin and Colten establishing themselves as major players in the tag team division and eventually winning both the AEW World Tag Team Championship and the AEW World Trios Championship.

Ilja Dragunov opens up about suffering panic attacks during matches

Ilja Dragunov recently revealed that suffering panic attacks during his wrestling matches has become a consistent occurrence throughout his career. While speaking with Ariel Helwani on The Ariel Helwani Show, Dragunov opened up candidly about his personal experiences with these episodes, explaining that they happen to him multiple times every week.

Reflecting on how his perspective has evolved, Dragunov noted that when you are younger, you always try to fix everything and find simple answers to complex questions, which the phrase itself explains is not possible. He shared that he found his way through professional wrestling and ultimately realized that in life, sometimes a person needs to give up in a certain way and accept things just the way they are. According to Dragunov, accepting these realities brings peace and removes the burden of feeling the need to fix every single aspect of oneself.

Dragunov emphasized that this condition is not entirely negative. He explained that the panic and intense feelings he carries with himself also push him to perform harder, put more effort and devotion into his work, and love more deeply. When Helwani asked about his worst panic attacks, Dragunov admitted that they happen at least multiple times every week to the point where he barely pays attention to them anymore because he chooses not to strive to be a polished person. As soon as he accepted himself and recognized the positive sides of these struggles, understanding that nothing is simply black and white and that life is full of gray areas, it became much easier for him to cope.

When Helwani asked if he faced similar challenges while waiting in the gorilla position before his matches, Dragunov explained that acceptance is key to moving forward. He shared that he has experienced panic in the middle of a fight, during his entrance, and even just seconds before walking out when he felt like he was forgetting his entire match.

Despite those terrifying moments, Dragunov takes immense pride in his resilience, noting that he has worked so hard for his skills that he remains capable of doing his job. He expressed that this ongoing battle and his refusal to give in are the reasons he feels deeply proud of himself on certain days, as accepting and dealing with the panic has ultimately made his in-ring performances feel even more real. Following the conclusion of his time with WWE, Dragunov recently transitioned to All Elite Wrestling, making his official debut at WrestleDream 2026.

I had a mark out moment where after Willow and Mercedes...I congratulated Willow via text, Foley said. And she says Mick, are you still here? I'd like to see you. And I was like She wants to see me?

Ilja Dragunov expresses desire to wrestle in Japan

Ilja Dragunov says he needs to wrestle in Japan, believing his in-ring style would connect with the people. During an interview with TMZ on Tuesday, the new AEW signing was asked who he would still like to work with in his career. Dragunov instead said it wasn’t a matter of who he wanted to work with, but rather where.

Dragunov stated that he wouldn't say it is necessarily who he wants to work with, but where he wants to work. He recalled that back in the day when he started with wXw, they had a working relationship with Big Japan Pro Wrestling, but that time had passed when he joined. He emphasized that the one thing he has not done in his career so far, among multiple things, but specifically something he needs to do, is to wrestle in Japan. He stated that he needs to wrestle in Japan because that is the place where he thinks his style belongs, and he believes he would connect with the people and the culture for professional wrestling in a whole different way.

Dragunov previously wrestled on three wXw shows co-promoted with Combat Zone Wrestling and Big Japan Pro Wrestling in 2013. However, all three of those events took place in Germany. During his illustrious time with wXw, Dragunov established a decorated resume, becoming a one-time wXw Unified World Wrestling Champion, a three-time wXw World Tag Team Champion, and a two-time wXw Shotgun Champion.

The conversation follows his recent major career move. Dragunov officially made his AEW debut at WrestleDream 2026 in Orlando on Saturday, where broadcaster Renee Paquette introduced him to the live crowd as the company’s newest signing.

Ilja Dragunov unconcerned about standing out on AEW's roster

Ilja Dragunov is unconcerned about becoming just another name on aew's ridiculously stacked roster. The former WWE star maintains that getting lost in the shuffle is not a fear he possesses at all, because he has spent his entire professional career betting on himself.

During a recent interview, Dragunov was asked whether the loaded roster made him think about how he would stand out from everyone else. For Dragunov, that is apparently not something he spends much time worrying about. He explained that he did not consider how stacked the roster is because that is a fear he simply does not have. This confidence also played a pretty big role in Dragunov walking away from WWE. He believes in trusting his instincts, and leaving the company was another example of him being willing to take that gamble on himself. He noted that he was always betting on himself when he left WWE, he is still betting on himself, and he trusts what his gut is telling him.

Dragunov also believes aew has already shown that it sees something different in him. He pointed directly to the way the company presented his arrival at wrestledream, where his debut was treated like a major deal before Tony Khan officially confirmed that he was all elite. For Dragunov, the way the company introduced him speaks for itself and proves that he sticks out from all the rest in that roster.

For Dragunov, standing out is not about trying to manufacture something just because the locker room is crowded. He believes wrestlers who are genuinely different naturally pull people's attention toward them. That is also where Dragunov shared an interesting observation about wrestling today, suggesting that too many wrestlers are trying to look or act like everyone else, which is when getting overlooked becomes a real problem.

Dragunov does not believe he falls into that category, and he has fourteen years of experience to back up that confidence. As far as he is concerned, standing out has never been the problem, noting that he has not been overlooked once in his fourteen year career. While aew certainly is not short on talent, meaning Dragunov will have plenty of competition for television time as his run gets going, he simply does not sound interested in worrying about any of that, especially when being different has worked out pretty well for him for the last fourteen years.

🔄 המשך 🔥 Medical examiner details released surrounding PAC's death

Newly released Cook County Medical Examiner records provide a timeline of Benjamin PAC Satterley's final hours on September 27. Satterley had spoken with his wife, Natalie Taylor, less than three hours before he was found unresponsive in the driver's seat of a running rental car near a restaurant in Des Polaines, Illinois. During the police investigation at the scene, Taylor attempted to call her husband's cell phone, only for authorities to answer.

Taylor informed investigators that her husband had told her he was stopping to grab food before heading to O'Hare Airport to fly home following his match at AEW All Out. She also noted that he had a previously diagnosed medical condition requiring medication, though the specifics were redacted from the files, but emphasized that he had no history of illicit drug or alcohol abuse. Toxicology testing revealed nothing extraordinary, and the medical examiner officially ruled the death natural due to bronchial asthma.

Inhalers were recovered from the vehicle, though the report could not establish whether Satterley was able to access or use them during the medical emergency. Taylor later shared an emotional tribute online detailing the plans they had made for his return home.

In the wrestling community, tributes and memories continue to pour in. During a recent episode of Being The Elite, Nick Jackson opened up about a vivid and emotional dream he experienced involving a last-minute singles match with Satterley, noting that he felt his presence during AEW WrestleDream.

🔄 המשך Ilja Dragunov responds to MJF mocking his AEW debut

Ilja Dragunov has heard MJF taking shots at his AEW debut, and he is not interested in firing back.

While MJF may have been fishing for a heated online feud, Dragunov made it clear he has no intention of biting.

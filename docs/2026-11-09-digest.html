🔥 Tommaso Ciampa renames AEW National Championship 'Ruby'

Tommaso Ciampa has given the AEW National Championship a new nickname following his victory over Andrade El Idolo on the episode of AEW Collision filmed aboard the Norwegian Jewel as part of the Chris Jericho Cruise. Declaring himself the company newest belt collector, Ciampa has set his sights on even more championship gold.

Following the match, a backstage video was released featuring the new champion reflecting on his accomplishments since joining the promotion ten months ago. The former WWE Superstar highlighted his rapid success in AEW, pointing to his previous TNT Championship victory and his latest achievement of capturing a title during a televised wrestling event aboard a cruise ship.

Ciampa stated that he is rewriting the playbook and called himself a history maker, noting his debut achievement as the first man ever to win a singles title in his first match in the company, followed by becoming the first man in professional wrestling history to win a title on television on a cruise ship.

Ciampa made it clear that winning the National Championship is only another step toward his ultimate goal of becoming AEW World Champion. Having already held the TNT Championship, he intends to continue adding titles to his collection before pursuing the company top prize. He emphasized that he is taking every title until he takes the world title, noting that his newest acquisition is special and that her name is Ruby.

The new champion concluded the promo by formally introducing his latest championship companion, singing to the belt and asking everyone to say hello to Ruby.

With his victory over Andrade, Ciampa becomes the fifth wrestler to hold the AEW National Championship since the title was introduced in November 2025. The title lineage began when Ricochet became the inaugural champion at AEW Full Gear 2025 by winning a Casino Gauntlet match. Jack Perry subsequently won the title in a Blackjack Battle Royale at AEW Revolution, before dropping it to Mark Davis at the Fairway to Hell edition of AEW Collision. Davis then lost the belt to Andrade El Idolo at AEW Redemption in Montreal, leading directly to Ciampa historic victory on the high seas. Ciampa latest championship success continues his momentum in AEW, while his declaration that he intends to collect every available title suggests that his ambitions extend far beyond his newly acquired National Championship.

🔥 Booker T reacts to Adam Copeland's upcoming AEW retirement

WWE Hall of Famer Booker T has shared his thoughts on Adam Copeland's plans to retire from professional wrestling in 2027, while also questioning one of the more dangerous moments from the AEW star's recent career. Copeland recently announced his intention to step away from the ring next year, bringing an end to his remarkable second chance in the wrestling business.

The former WWE Champion originally hung up his boots in 2011 due to spinal stenosis and neck injuries. Following years of recovery and training, he earned an opportunity to return to action, making an epic in-ring comeback at the 2020 WWE Royal Rumble. The Rated R Superstar has since competed for both WWE and AEW, where he now seeks to capture the AEW World Championship before his final farewell. During his AEW stint so far, he has held the TNT and Tag Team Titles, the latter of which he enjoyed alongside his long-time friend Christian Cage.

During a recent episode of his Hall of Fame podcast, Booker T praised Copeland for being able to conclude his career on his own terms. He did it his way. You got to respect that, man. You got to give him a lot of respect for that, Booker said, referencing Copeland having to leave the game earlier due to injury, getting cleared to come back, doing the WWE run, and then going to get that paper and retirement package.

You know how Ric Flair say, You can stay all night, stay a little longer? That's what that's been about, too, Booker added of Copeland's last run. To be able to do the thing with Christian, the way you came in, the way you go out, it's a lot to be said about that. I envy that part, as far as those guys being able to really being able to keep it together as a team for all these years. I commend those guys. Booker also expressed admiration for Copeland and Christian maintaining their partnership throughout the decades and stated he was happy to see Copeland making the decision to retire.

The conversation eventually turned to Copeland's steel cage match against Malakai Black in AEW, which featured a dangerous dive from the top of the cage that resulted in a broken leg. Booker questioned whether Copeland might look back on that particular moment differently as his career comes to an end. To me, only thing in AEW that I think maybe he'll regret is doing that dive off the cage where he broke his leg.

The two-time WWE Hall of Famer elaborated on how difficult it can be for veteran wrestlers to recognize their physical limitations, particularly when attempting high-risk moves they might have performed earlier in their careers. That's probably, I don't know what he was thinking, but your brain tells you you're still young. Your brain tells you you can still go out there and do it like you did once before, until you go out there and do it. And then something pops or breaks, and you go, What the hell was I thinking? he said.

Despite Booker's speculation, Copeland has previously made it clear that he doesn't regret the decision. Speaking with Ariel Helwani earlier this year, Copeland clarified that he does not look back on the high-risk cage dive with regret. The road to Copeland's retirement has officially begun, with an International Championship match at AEW Full Gear set as the next stop on his farewell tour.

Kyle Fletcher talks about his AEW singles push

Ahead of his World title match against Will Ospreay, Kyle Fletcher opened up about his AEW singles push and how quickly his career trajectory shifted. A couple of years ago, Fletcher turned heel, shaved his head, and joined the Don Callis Family. Shortly after making these significant presentation changes, Fletcher began his singles run and soon won the AEW TNT and International titles.

Speaking on What Happened When with Tony Schiavone, he addressed this defining period of his career. Fletcher shared that a singles run was not discussed at all when he and Mark Davis joined AEW as Aussie Open. It really was something that just kind of happened, and it was never really discussed beforehand, Fletcher explained. He added that he always had the belief that if it was to ever happen that he would succeed regardless.

Fletcher noted that Aussie Open was doing great things all over the world, which ultimately led to them getting signed with AEW off the back of that success. Because of that momentum, the tag team was always his priority and at the front of his mind, with his main focus simply being to make that act as good as it could possibly be.

While Fletcher always expected to transition into a singles wrestler at some point in his career, he was ultimately surprised by how quickly it actually happened. He figured that it would happen eventually and just had that belief in himself that everything would work out. Looking back, he realizes that the major singles push happened a lot faster than he expected.

Ex-WWE writer claims AEW had backstage cliques during CM Punk controversy

Former WWE writer Robert Karpeles recently shared his thoughts on the AEW backstage environment during the CM Punk Brawl Out controversy, pointing to deep-seated locker room divisions as a major concern.

During a recent appearance on the Two Man Power Trip of Wrestling podcast, Karpeles reflected on how Vince McMahon historically ran WWE almost single-handedly, noting that the modern wrestling landscape features many different backstage management figures. While discussing this shift in corporate structure, Karpeles looked back at CM Punk’s infamous post-All Out 2022 media scrum comments and the physical backstage brawl that immediately followed. He admitted that the situation made him feel nervous about the overall direction of AEW at the time.

Karpeles stated that when a professional wrestling locker room is fractured into multiple factions, nobody pulls in the same direction or works toward a common goal. To prevent this, he believes a major wrestling company desperately needs a central authority figure who is not afraid to make decisive choices.

Elaborating on AEW's internal dynamics during the controversy, Karpeles described the company as feeling very cliquey with distinct camps of talent. He noted that individuals backstage were typically aligned with specific groups, whether they were in Cody's group, part of Kenny and the Young Bucks, with Jericho and his crew, or simply part of everybody else.

According to Karpeles, having these internal factions means everyone stops working toward the same goal. He emphasized that whether through inspired leadership or fear, a company needs someone front and center who will make decisive choices where everyone knows that, good, bad, or indifferent, it is ultimately that person's call.

AEW Promoting Survivor 51 Contestant From ROH

Following his outing on the islands of Fiji, Brady Booker looks to make a splash in the lands of All Elite Wrestling and Ring of Honor next.

During Saturday's edition of AEW Collision, a video package highlighted Booker's recent quest on Survivor 51, where he emerged as the first member of the jury. Clips showed the former WWE NXT star swimming in the international waters, pushing a crate through a net, and wielding a machete all before his vote-off on the November 4 episode of the CBS reality competition. The package also showed Booker in action in AEW and ROH rings, with the developmental wrestler exclaiming that he was ready to take on the elite competition that AEW has to offer.

Outwit, outplay, outlast, outwrestle, AEW wrote on social media. After being pushed to his limits on Season 51 of Survivor, AEW and Ring of Honor developmental star Brady Booker is ready to turn his focus to the competition in AEW.

Booker has competed nearly two dozen times under the AEW-ROH umbrella, largely in dark matches. His limited AEW-ROH television experience includes matches against Serpentico and Dak Draper on AEW Dark in 2023. More recently, he participated in the Ring of Honor TV tapings in September and November 2026. Booker previously performed for WWE as Bodhi Hayward, too.

Booker became the third professional wrestler to compete on the Survivor franchise, behind only John Morrison and the late Ashley Massaro. Morrison, who currently performs under the name Johnny TV for AEW and ROH, placed 12th on Survivor: David vs. Goliath. Contestants voted Massaro off second in Survivor: China.

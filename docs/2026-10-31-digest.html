AEW Dynamite Viewership Drops Against World Series

AEW Dynamite viewership and ratings information for the October 28 episode has been released. Reports indicate that AEW Dynamite on October 28 averaged 620,000 viewers on TBS. This number is down from the 661,000 viewers the show drew on October 21. Wednesday’s episode scored a 0.14 rating in the 18 to 49 demographic, down from the 0.15 demo rating the week prior.

The episode was up against stiff competition, airing against Game 5 of the World Series between the Milwaukee Brewers and Tampa Bay Rays. Furthermore, AEW Dynamite was simulcast on HBO Max, although viewership information for HBO Max is not available.

Backstage Details on Komander's Current AEW Absence

Backstage Details On Komander's AEW Absence

AEW star Komander hasn't been seen on AEW programming since the beginning of September, and his most recent match was for CMLL a few weeks after that. For fans missing the star, Dave Meltzer offered an update on the situation.

According to Meltzer in the latest edition of the Wrestling Observer Newsletter, the star is suffering from an injury, but he didn't report exactly what the injury is. There is hope within the company that he will be back soon.

Komander last performed at Relevos Increibles on September 20, in a loss alongside ROH World Champion Bandido in a match against Mascara Dorada and Soberano Jr. He also recently faced Titan for the CMLL World Welterweight Championship, a match he also lost, the day before.

His most recent bout in AEW was a loss to Nick Wayne on an episode of Collision. Before that, he competed at AEW All In London in the Casino Gauntlet match, ultimately won by Andrade El Idolo, to determine who earned a contracted shot at the AEW World Championship.

Komander made his AEW debut, wrestling without a contract, in the Face of the Revolution ladder match in March 2023. He officially signed with the company the following month, after being released from his contract by Kaoz Lucha Libre. Prior to his signing with Tony Khan's company, WWE also reportedly had interest in the luchador.

🔄 המשך 🔥 Ilja Dragunov Reflects on Joining AEW and Entering the Continental Classic

Beyond his desire to compete in the round-robin tournament, Ilja Dragunov has shared new details regarding his swift transition from WWE to All Elite Wrestling.

Speaking about his departure, Dragunov explained that his new deal came together very quickly after his previous contract expired, despite some initial visa hurdles. He admitted that he had been questioning whether WWE was the right place for him for quite a while. While he expressed respect for the seven years spent with his former employer, he emphasized that he is emotionally driven and listens to his gut feeling when a creative direction no longer aligns with his goals. This ultimately led him to seek out new opportunities, resulting in his debut at WrestleDream.

Turning back to the Continental Classic, Dragunov noted that the competition begins on the November 18 episode of Dynamite in Denver, Colorado, with the finals scheduled for Worlds End on December 19 at the Target Center in Minneapolis, Minnesota. He argued that omitting him from the lineup would be second-hand embarrassing for the promotion, given how closely his personal intensity aligns with the tournament's legacy of featuring past winners like Jon Moxley, Kazuchika Okada, and Eddie Kingston. He also teased potential future clashes with Kingston given their previous history in the ring. Despite his strong campaign, Dragunov has not yet been officially announced as a participant by AEW officials.

🔄 המשך Former WBD Executive Doubts Skydance Will Pick Up AEW Option Year

Despite Tony Khan beaming with optimism following recent high-level meetings in Los Angeles with Skydance leadership and David Ellison, a new warning has emerged regarding the promotion's television future.

According to industry reporting, a former Warner Bros. Discovery executive believes AEW could be in for a rude awakening when its option year arrives. The core issue is not the quality of the wrestling itself, but rather a perceived lack of content diversification. The source suggests that if Khan expects a renewal simply by delivering more traditional wrestling shows, Skydance's new leadership may ultimately decline to pick up the option year.

This note of caution paints a starkly different picture from the glowing reports circulating just days prior. Khan had flown to the West Coast following positive discussions and previously praised Ellison during a post-show media scrum, emphasizing how excited the executive was about partnering with AEW.

However, the new report highlights that simply maintaining the status quo might not satisfy incoming leadership. It remains unconfirmed what specific types of non-wrestling or expanded content Skydance might actually want from the company, or if this particular former executive has direct insight into current decision-making. No official announcements regarding the option year have been made yet, leaving it as an industry warning that adds significant intrigue to Khan's ongoing network negotiations.

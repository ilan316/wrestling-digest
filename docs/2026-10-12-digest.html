🔶 AEW News

Tommaso Ciampa and Charles Mason Kiss at Indie Show

AEW star Tommaso Ciampa and independent standout Charles Mason created an unexpected moment by kissing each other during their match at House of Glory's Salvation event on October 9. Tommaso Ciampa made his rare independent appearance facing off against Charles Mason in the main event. During the match, Ciampa and Mason engaged in a peculiar display of dominance by locking lips before throwing fists at each other. Wrestling is a place where anything can happen, and this psychological power play certainly made for an unusual moment. The two competitors threw everything at each other, but the kiss is what has the wrestling fans talking.

Ultimately, Ciampa won the match, picking up an indie win in approximately a decade outside WWE and AEW. That said, The Psycho Killer offered fans an opportunity to see him perform outside major promotions again. After the match, Mason commented on the unusual moment on social media. He wrote, I think he likes me. The feeling is mutual.

Tomasso Ciampa is currently entering a feud with Andrade El Idolo in AEW. Ciampa confronted Andrade after the latter successfully defended his AEW National Championship at Collision: Grand Slam France in Paris in October 2026. The feud follows Ciampa’s high-profile earlier rivalry with Chris Jericho. Jericho and Ciampa have faced off twice previously, with both men holding one victory each.

Meanwhile, Charles Mason has established himself on the indie scene. Known as The Root Of All Evil, Mason built a legendary legacy in House of Glory, becoming the first-ever HOG Grand Slam Champion, winning every major title in the promotion. Mason held the HOG Crown Jewel title for 595 days, the longest reign in the championship’s history. Needless to say, it was interesting to see the two dark, sadistic characters go head-to-head.

WrestleZone

Swerve Strickland Discusses Feud With Hangman Page

Out of all the rivalries and feuds that have happened in All Elite Wrestling, arguably none of them have been filled with as much hate as the one between Swerve Strickland and Hangman Adam Page. Their feud has seen Page drink Strickland's blood and burn down his childhood home, while Strickland has broken in to Page's home while his family have been asleep and choked Page with a steel chain, and AEW fans still can't get enough of it. The feud has now reached the AEW trios division, and despite already being considered a classic rivalry by fans, Strickland stated in a recent interview that he wants the feud to be remembered as even more than just a great AEW story.

I hope one day it goes down as one of the greatest in wrestling history, Strickland said. I want it to be that big and that important and that impactful you know. Like when two guys tag in, and the entire arena stands up like as a wave of people standing up to see what happens next, that's when you know you've got something special, and that's when you know like that doesn't come around very often.

Strickland went on to say that there's a vulnerability that he and Page share as individuals that makes the feud so captivating, with both men knowing which buttons to press on the other man. We wear our hearts on our sleeves and we exploit the emotions from one another, Strickland noted. We know what makes each other tick, we know what pisses them off and pushes it past that boiling level. We know it and the fans know it too. Now we have it to the point they're anticipating it, they're anticipating when the dynamite actually goes off because the fuse has already been lit, when does it actually blow up? That's where we can like take them on that ride with us no matter where we go.

🔄 המשך 🔥 AEW WrestleDream 2026 Match Card and Title Changes

Following the Grand Slam France events, the AEW WrestleDream 2026 card has expanded significantly with new championship matches and faction developments. Andrade El Idolo successfully defended his National Championship against Michael Oku before being confronted and attacked by Tommaso Ciampa, setting up a title match between the two for the pay-per-view. To preview that bout, Andrade and AEW World Champion Will Ospreay will team up to face Ciampa and Samoa Joe on this week's special three-hour television block.

In the women's division, Wrathlander consisting of Kris Statlander and Harley Cameron secured a surprise victory in a three-way tag team match to earn a shot at the AEW Women's World Tag Team Championships held by The Brawling Birds. Furthermore, it was revealed that the champions, Jamie Hayter and Alex Windsor, have officially joined Will Ospreay's expanding United Empire faction alongside recent Clockwork Carnage qualifier Mike Bailey. Meanwhile, Mercedes Mone is officially set to defend her AEW Women's World Championship against Thekla, and Hangman Adam Page and Brodido will challenge Swerve Strickland and The New Level for the World Trios Championship in a two-out-of-three falls match.

The upcoming three-hour go-home broadcast has also filled out its supporting card. Aside from the remaining Clockwork Carnage qualifiers, the October 14 special will feature Dr. Britt Baker and Persephone taking on Dani Luna and Hyan, a six-woman tag match pairing Mercedes Mone and Divine Dominion against the Triangle of Madness, and a six-man tag bout pitting the Death Riders against the Don Callis Family.

🔥 TV series in development based on Chris Jericho's early career

Chris Jericho announced during a recent interview on Skratch N Sniff while promoting Fozzy's new single, I Know Evil, that a television series based on the early days of his professional wrestling career is actively moving forward. The project is nearing the pilot filming stage. The series will adapt his 2007 autobiography, A Lion's Tale: Around the World in Spandex, which chronicles his life and his extensive travels across various international wrestling promotions before he eventually arrived in WWE in 1999.

Jericho explained that a standard feature film would not be enough to capture his life story, jokingly noting that even sixty-two-year-old Brad Pitt would be too old to portray him. Because he always likes to push the envelope, a multi-episode television format was chosen to properly explore the different stages, promotions, and countries of his grueling early journey in the wrestling business.

Addressing who might star in the lead role, Jericho dropped a major hint regarding the casting process. He revealed that the production team has identified an actor from the Canadian comedy series Letterkenny who shares a striking facial similarity to what Jericho looked like when he first broke into the industry. While he opted to keep the specific actor's name under wraps for now, Jericho confirmed that the physical resemblance is uncanny.

A Lion's Tale was originally released on October 25, 2007, detailing his upbringing and the brutal early struggles of his wrestling career. It was later followed by two more autobiographical books, Undisputed: How to Become the World Champion in 1,372 Easy Steps in 2011, and The Best in the World: At What I Have No Idea in 2014. However, this new television adaptation focuses specifically on the foundational first book as it moves rapidly toward production.

🔥 Tommaso Ciampa attempts RKO on AEW star in Randy Orton mix-up

Tommaso Ciampa has revived his long-running obsession with hitting Randy Orton with an RKO, but his latest attempt in St. Louis, Missouri, didn't quite go according to plan. Taking to Instagram, Ciampa posted a video from St. Louis, where Fright Night Dynamite and Collision were recently held. Remembering that St. Louis was the hometown of his former co-worker, Orton, Ciampa decided it was time to settle unfinished business and finally hit the Viper with his signature move out of nowhere. This continues a running gag that Ciampa started during his WWE days, when he and his friend and DIY training partner Johnny Gargano repeatedly tried to catch Orton off guard backstage, often merging the bits with their classic NXT content. Those attempts usually ended with Ciampa crashing into objects rather than successfully hitting the former WWE Champion.

Even after moving to AEW, where Tony Khan's company does not employ Orton or Gargano, the Psycho Killer clearly hasn't abandoned his mission. In the video shared online, Ciampa explained his mission by stating that the Viper lives in St. Louis and he had 24 hours to get him, declaring he was going to RKO Randy Orton. The footage then jumped forward 12 hours, with Ciampa claiming he had finally located his target. AEW does have a young star on the roster, Kyle Fletcher, who has been frequently compared to Orton by fans due to his bald head, full left arm sleeve, and recent character changes. Approaching the unsuspecting wrestler from behind in the trainer's room without checking for the right arm tattoos, Ciampa prepared to deliver the RKO while shouting out of nowhere. His attempted attack ended with Ciampa crashing onto a trainer's table, while Fletcher was left wondering what had just happened.

A befuddled Fletcher questioned the bizarre assault, asking Ciampa what he was doing, bro. Realizing his mistake, Ciampa offered a brief explanation, admitting he thought Fletcher was someone else and telling him to never mind. Fletcher wasn't convinced by the explanation and could only respond by asking who. Fletcher let the whole thing go, leaving Ciampa to groan in pain on the trainer's table, proving that Ciampa's RKO problem is still very much alive and Orton remains off his highlight reel for now.

As eventful as Ciampa's adventures were prior to the show, he had just as much going on when the cameras started rolling for television. Despite losing to Andrade El Idolo at WrestleDream, where he also previously made waves by assaulting Andrade's pants, Ciampa called out the luchador for a rematch while declaring himself the best signing in AEW history. This prompted recent AEW signee and former Ciampa opponent Ilja Dragunov to come down to the ring and challenge Ciampa to a fight. Ciampa refused in order to focus on his Collision match with Matt Sydal, though it is expected that he and Dragunov will face off again sooner than later.

Rob Van Dam defends Ric Flair's lifestyle and drinking habits

Rob Van Dam has weighed in on the growing concerns surrounding Ric Flair's health, lifestyle choices, and continued drinking following a recent high profile fall. Speaking on his podcast alongside Katie Forbes, the WWE Hall of Famer defended the wrestling legend and questioned whether he should be judged for wanting to live life on his own terms.

The discussion followed an incident on October 23 where Flair suffered a bloody fall outside a Florida establishment, leading to a hospital stay at Tampa General Hospital. The health scare was compounded by revelations that Flair was also dealing with a serious blood clot in his leg. Despite these warnings, Flair returned to the same establishment just one day later upon his discharge, and was later spotted wearing an eye patch.

Van Dam suggested that Flair fully understands the risks associated with his lifestyle but consciously chooses enjoyment over longevity. Presenting what he believed to be Flair's perspective, RVD stated that the legend would rather live the way he wants and die when it is his time than possibly get another couple of years without enjoying life or doing what he wants to do.

RVD compared the real life situation to the movie The Wrestler starring Mickey Rourke, noting the painful reality of a veteran going out on his own terms while family and friends experience distress. He asked if Flair, of all people, does not deserve that autonomy. Van Dam contrasted this with his own life, explaining that taking reckless risks would be extremely selfish if he had young children depending on him at his current age. However, for someone in their late seventies who has lived out a normal human life expectancy, RVD expressed more understanding, noting that all one might want to do at that stage is smoke and drink.

Drawing on personal experience with his late friend Sabu, Van Dam discussed the limitations of constantly confronting someone struggling with destructive habits. He realized that repeatedly acting as an adversary only creates separation and resentment, whereas choosing to be a supportive friend who does their best to watch over them as much as allowed is a better approach.

Ultimately, while acknowledging the pain Flair's decisions may cause his family members, including his daughter Charlotte Flair, Van Dam maintains that the Nature Boy must be allowed to make his own choices and live out his remaining years the way he sees fit.

Orange Cassidy reflects on PAC rivalry and AEW difficult period

During a recent interview on Going Ringside, Orange Cassidy reflected on several of his most memorable feuds since joining All Elite Wrestling, singling out his longtime rivalry with PAC as something he believes will never be surpassed. Cassidy acknowledged multiple significant opponents from his career, including the Death Riders, Jon Moxley, Wheeler Yuta, and Chris Jericho. However, he emphasized that his chemistry with PAC was truly unique. When they got in the ring together it was special, and Cassidy feels confident that PAC will forever remain his greatest rival.

Cassidy and PAC shared the ring in five singles matches under the AEW banner. Their history began at AEW Revolution 2020, where PAC emerged victorious in a match rated three and three-quarter stars by Dave Meltzer. Cassidy later defeated PAC for what was then known as the AEW All-Atlantic Championship on the October 12, 2022 edition of Dynamite in Toronto, in a bout that earned four and one-quarter stars. Their final singles encounter took place on the October 7, 2025 Title Tuesday episode of Dynamite, with Cassidy picking up the victory in a four-star match.

Beyond his in-ring reflections, Cassidy addressed the heavy emotional atmosphere within the locker room following the recent deaths of PAC and Andy Williams. He noted that being surrounded by his fellow wrestlers has provided an important source of comfort during a particularly difficult period for the company. Cassidy explained that being with other people helps take away some of the pain caused by these losses because the talent can support each other and be reminded of all the good things those individuals did for them. Ultimately, Cassidy emphasized that the relationships among AEW talent have helped everyone navigate the grief together, with the backstage support system making a meaningful difference.

Bobby Lashley announces $10,000 high school wrestling challenge

AEW wrestler Bobby Lashley recently opened up about his plans for a massive $10,000 wrestling challenge aimed at high school athletes. An amateur wrestler himself, Lashley pursued amateur wrestling during his time in college and later in the military before successfully transitioning to professional wrestling.

As one of the most popular names in the professional wrestling industry, Lashley recently spoke to Xavier Deguzman to share his plans for the $10,000 challenge. Lashley revealed that he recently visited Junction City because he wanted to spread some goodwill to the local wrestling team.

He further elaborated on the concept by stating that he plans to walk into the wrestling room and declare that if any one of the high schoolers can take him down, he will write a check for $10,000 for the team. However, despite Lashley's noble intentions, no official confirmation has been shared or passed around yet about the execution of his $10,000 wrestling challenge.

Outside of his community outreach and amateur roots, Lashley remains an active competitor on the All Elite Wrestling roster. His most recent match was a two-on-three handicap match during the Fright Night Collision taping.

Young Bucks praise Tony Khan as AEW tag champions

Now once again AEW World Tag Team Champions, the Young Bucks appear to be having the time of their AEW careers, prompting them to pay homage to their boss, Tony Khan. On the post-WrestleDream episode of Being the Elite, Matt and Nick Jackson were discussing how proud they were of their WrestleDream match against Kyle Fletcher and Kevin Knight when Nick began talking about a brief moment where he looked in the camera and told Khan he wasn't sure how much longer he could perform at this level. He admitted he had no idea why he said it, but used that as a segue for both him and Matt to praise Khan as a boss and as a person.

I would do anything for Tony at this point, Nick said. I love that man. We've given our entire careers to him, and he's given us so much. I don't think he gets enough praise and credit from us.

I texted him on his birthday about that, Matt said. I said You changed my life dude. I'll never forget you.

Alongside Kenny Omega, Cody Rhodes, and Brandi Rhodes, the Bucks and Khan were the key figures in launching AEW in 2019, with the Bucks noting in their autobiography that Khan had approached them about the idea for AEW the previous summer. Despite some bumps in the road, most notably the animosity between the Bucks and former AEW star CM Punk back in 2022 and 2023, the Bucks have remained one of the top acts of AEW since its launch, with their recent tag title victory at All Out setting the record for most AEW World Tag Team Championship reigns in company history.

🔄 המשך 🔥 WWE SmackDown and AEW Dynamite scheduled head-to-head on New Year's Day

WWE has locked in its holiday television plans, deciding that the historic head-to-head showdown on New Year's Day 2027 will not prompt any changes to its routine. Rather than organizing a live broadcast to directly combat AEW and its live New Year's Smash edition of Dynamite, WWE will stick to a previously scheduled double television taping.

The SmackDown episode airing on January 1 will actually be filmed a few days earlier on Monday, December 28, at the Benchmark International Arena in Tampa, Florida, right after the final Raw of 2026. Company sources indicate that the primary motivation behind this decision is to grant talent and production staff a well-deserved day off to spend the holiday with family and friends.

While the prospect of going head-to-head with a live rival show might typically trigger aggressive counterprogramming, management is refusing to disrupt holiday plans. Internally, there is an expectation that the broadcast could draw lower numbers than a standard Friday night episode, though officials attribute this dip to the holiday season itself rather than any threat from the competition. Ultimately, WWE is entirely comfortable moving forward with its recorded show, showing no concern over ceding the live advantage to AEW for the holiday slot.

🔄 המשך Jack Perry health update following AEW Dynamite bumps

Following his brutal encounter on Dynamite, Jack Perry has emerged with a promising health update. After taking a series of punishing landings against Kyle Fletcher, most notably a terrifying powerbomb directly onto the ring frame, Perry is reportedly doing okay. Reports indicate that Perry has been telling people backstage that he is simply sore from the physical toll of the match.

Given the visual severity of the spots, particularly the way Perry impacted the ring frame on his neck and back, analysts expressed shock that he avoided a catastrophic injury. Despite the dangerous nature of the landings, Perry managed to push through and finish the bout, which ultimately concluded when Fletcher hit a top-rope Brainbuster to secure the win.

While the match certainly pushed physical boundaries and left fans holding their breath, the current indication is that Perry dodged a major bullet. He suffered no serious medical issues, proving remarkably resilient after taking some of the most dangerous bumps seen on television in recent memory.

The full lineup for this Saturday's Fright Night edition of AEW Collision is now official following the show's taping in St. Louis. Ilja Dragunov will go one-on-one with The Beast Mortos for the first time ever, building on his debut victory last week against Mike Bailey.

These new additions join the previously announced Artist of Stardom title match featuring the Triangle of Madness defending against Hazuki, Koguma and Fuwa-chan.

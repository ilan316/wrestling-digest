🔥 AEW Hires Neil Rosan to Production Team Ahead of Full Gear

All Elite Wrestling has made a new addition to its behind-the-scenes production staff, with Neil Rosan announcing that he has officially joined the company as an Associate Producer of Motion Graphics. Rosan confirmed the news on Tuesday, November 10, sharing a brief statement on his official X account expressing excitement about beginning his new role with the promotion. All in for my next chapter, Rosan wrote. I am excited to start as an Associate Producer of Motion Graphics with AEW. The announcement marks a new professional opportunity for Rosan as he joins AEW’s production department, which is responsible for various visual elements featured across the company’s television programming and other presentations, including promotional packages and on-screen visuals.

His arrival comes during a very busy period for AEW as the promotion prepares for its upcoming Full Gear 2026 pay-per-view. AEW is scheduled to present a special three-hour combined edition of Dynamite and Collision on Wednesday, November 11, serving as the final television broadcast ahead of Full Gear. The show will take place at the Maverik Center in West Valley City, Utah, and will air live beginning at 8/7c on TBS and HBO Max, featuring the regular edition of Dynamite followed by a one-hour Collision.

The go-home broadcast serves as the final television stop before Full Gear 2026 takes place on Saturday, November 14, at the Mortgage Matchup Center in Phoenix, Arizona. While AEW has not announced specific projects Rosan will be working on beyond his new title, his appointment adds another member to the company’s production team as it continues preparing its weekly television programming, major events, and overall visual presentation. AEW continues to make adjustments both in front of the cameras and behind them, and Rosan is clearly ready to get started in his new role.

🔥 Jim Ross Undergoing Another Surgery Following Health Issues

Legendary wrestling announcer Jim Ross is undergoing yet another surgical procedure as he continues dealing with complications involving his brain shunt. The 74-year-old AEW commentator initially had the shunt inserted in August to help drain excess fluid from his brain and address ongoing balance issues. Although the procedure initially provided significant improvement, Ross subsequently experienced blistering headaches and underwent another operation in October to correct the issue.

Conrad Thompson, who co-hosts the Grilling JR podcast with Ross, announced via social media that the WWE Hall of Famer was scheduled for another surgery on Tuesday morning. Good ole JR is having shunt surgery this morning, Thompson wrote, adding that everyone should pray for his speedy recovery. Jim deserves a break, Thompson continued, asking fans to flood Ross's timeline with positive vibes while he is in recovery.

The latest procedure follows a difficult stretch for Ross, who was hospitalized for unrelated reasons following his October surgery when a chest cold resulted in low oxygen levels. During an episode of his podcast, Thompson provided an encouraging update revealing that Ross had been released from the hospital and was recovering at home, though additional work on the shunt remained necessary. Thompson later indicated that another procedure was being planned to repair the shunt, noting that the device had reportedly not yet been reactivated following the second operation in October.

Ross's numerous medical hurdles are only a few of the issues he has dealt with throughout the year, including a fall he suffered just a day before appearing at AEW Double or Nothing in May. Prior to his October surgery, Ross also revealed he was undergoing several biopsies to determine if he had contracted skin cancer, which he previously battled a few years ago, and shared that an X-ray had found a spot on his kidney.

Despite the relentless health battles, Ross's primary goal has been to get back to the AEW commentary desk once he is recovered. He remains part of the AEW team, where his role focuses on helping with commentary at pay-per-view events, and he has frequently praised AEW CEO Tony Khan for how well he has been treated throughout his ongoing health challenges.

Jon Moxley and Renee Paquette Mourn Loss of Their Dog Blue

Renee Paquette and Jon Moxley are mourning a difficult loss in their household following the passing of their dog, Blue. Blue passed away on November 9 after being in the couple's lives for nearly 11 years. Paquette took to Instagram to share the heartbreaking news, noting that although pet owners know this day will eventually come, she truly believed Blue was a superhero who could go on forever, remaining spry and full of life until his very last day.

Blue held a profoundly special place in the family history, having joined Moxley and Paquette one year before they got married. He even served as one of only two attendees at their fairly spontaneous wedding in 2017. Following the birth of their daughter Nora in 2021, Blue was an important part of her life from the very moment they brought her home from the hospital. Paquette described him as her soul dog and their shadow in every room, noting that he loved getting affection from her and loved to wrestle with Jon.

Reflecting on the heavy void left behind, Paquette expressed peace in knowing that Blue was deeply loved and that the family was able to be by his side to say their goodbyes. We have a giant Blue shaped hole in our lives right now, Paquette wrote, expressing gratitude that they were with him at the end. She concluded her touching tribute by recalling some of his most endearing and amusing quirks, including his turkey gobble bark, his habit of forcefully giving a paw to beg for snacks, and even his rotten, room clearing farts, signing off with a final farewell to Blueberry J. Good.

Amid this difficult personal time, Moxley and Paquette are set to be on the road for upcoming television tapings. They are scheduled to be in West Valley City, Utah for upcoming shows ahead of a major pay-per-view event in Phoenix, Arizona, where Moxley and the Death Riders are slated to compete in an Anarchy in the Arena match.

Chris Jericho Discusses Playing Gorgeous George in 'The Greatest' Series

Chris Jericho is bringing one of professional wrestling's most influential figures back into the spotlight. The AEW star portrays Gorgeous George in the new Amazon Prime Video series The Greatest, which explores the life and career of boxing legend Muhammad Ali. During a recent interview, Jericho discussed the preparation that went into portraying the wrestling icon, whose flamboyant personality and larger-than-life presentation helped inspire Ali's famous promotional style. Jericho appears in the fourth episode of the series, with his scenes highlighting the influence George had on Ali's development of the charismatic public persona that helped make him a global superstar.

Despite George's enormous popularity during his heyday, Jericho discovered that finding footage of rhy legendary performer was more difficult than expected. People were buying televisions just to watch Gorgeous George, but because a lot of his work got taped over from week to week, tapes are not easy to find. A key resource for Jericho was the 1949 film Alias the Champ, which featured George and gave him an opportunity to study the wrestler's mannerisms, voice, and personality. Jericho watched the movie repeatedly to pattern his speech after George, focusing on his drawl, slight Texas accent, higher voice, and flamboyance. He wanted to stay true to who George was, noting that he was not like Jim Carrey on set demanding to be called Andy Kaufman, but rather just locked into the character when the cameras rolled.

Although his role in the series is relatively brief, Jericho believes the experience demonstrated that Gorgeous George's life could provide enough material for an entirely separate production. He stated that the moment they did those scenes, he really felt they could do a Gorgeous George spinoff. Jericho pointed out that while almost everyone in wrestling knows the name, no one really knows much about him anymore, and he is becoming a forgotten figure over time. He emphasized that there is a compelling story to tell beyond the wrestling connection, pointing to George's massive success where people were buying televisions just to watch him, contrasted with his rough life and young death from alcoholism. Jericho also revealed that he filmed his scenes for The Greatest in 2025, more than a year before the series release.

In addition to discussing his television appearance, it was noted that a series based on Jericho's own life is currently in early development. The project is set to be based on Jericho's first book titled A Lion's Tale. Not much is known about the show yet, past Jericho revealing that an actor from the series Letterkenny has been tapped to play him.

Romeo Malverde Wins Jericho Cruise Oceanic Championship

A new champion has been crowned on the Chris Jericho Rock N Wrestling Rager at Sea with Romeo Malverde capturing gold. Romeo Malverde, formerly known as Santos Escobar in WWE, revealed that he won the Jericho Cruise Oceanic Championship during the current Jericho cruise.

On social media, he wrote that he is the Emperor of the Ocean and celebrated the surreal experience of being onboard the cruise before confirming he will return next year to defend the title. He added that in the meantime the championship will be defended with no limits, no borders, and no forbidden doors, while expressing gratitude to the cruise organization and Chris Jericho.

Malverde defeated Mo Jabari to win the title. He previously earned his title shot by defeating Ricky Knight Jr in a number one contenders match. Malverde is currently operating as a free agent following his departure from WWE, where he was released in April.

🔄 המשך Steven Borden Discusses AEW All Out Injury and In-Ring Return

Addressing the mid-air collision that brought a sudden halt to his pay-per-view match alongside Darby Allin against Kevin Knight and Kyle Fletcher, Steven Borden has shared his perspective on the scary incident. After being checked by medical staff in the ring, Borden was stretchered out and sent to a local hospital, though Tony Khan provided a reassuring update later that night confirming his CT scans and tests came back good.

Reflecting on the ordeal during a recent interview, Borden explained that while the brain is critically important, he felt physically fine the moment he woke up because he had avoided injuries to his back or neck. He emphasized that he did not want to run away from difficult circumstances or pressure, comparing the recovery process to messing up during public speaking where the only solution is to get right back out there and do it again.

Darby Allin supported this aggressive approach to comebacks, arguing that wrestlers should return at full force rather than tiptoeing back into the squared circle. That philosophy directly influenced Borden's rapid return to television, as he chose not to waste time easing back into action and instead jumped straight into the high-stakes Blood & Guts match earlier this month.

🔄 המשך AEW Dynamite Ticket Sales Update for November 10, 2026

Heading into this week's crucial go-home episode, ticket movement continues to pick up pace for the upcoming dual AEW Dynamite and Collision taping at the Maverik Center in West Valley City, Utah.

Wednesday night serves as the final broadcast opportunity to build toward Full Gear. Several major championship matches are already locked in for the weekend event, including The Young Bucks defending the tag team titles against Davis and Doyle, and Mercedes Mone putting the women's world championship on the line against Marina Shafir. Additionally, The Brawling Birds will defend their women's tag titles against Wrathlander, while Adam Page and Brodido defend the trios titles against Swerve Strickland and The New Level in a Texas Death Match, alongside a world title defense by Will Ospreay.

AJ Francis to Host Houston Hardy Party Featuring Queen Sharmell

AJ Francis is set to host the Houston Hardy Party on September 22, according to an official announcement from TNA. The event will also feature appearances by Queen Sharmell and various guests from Reality of Wrestling, joining forces with the legendary tag team Matt and Jeff Hardy.

The festivities are scheduled to begin at 7 p.m. Central time at Pitch 25, located at 2120 Walker Street in Houston, Texas. This special gathering is part of a previously announced series of Hardy Party appearances taking place in Houston and San Antonio.

TNA’s official ticket listing advertises an intimate question and answer session for attendees, along with several distinct admission options to suit different fan experiences. The VIP package includes guaranteed seating, a photo opportunity with both Matt and Jeff Hardy, and one autograph from each brother. For those purchasing priority admission, the package includes a post-show photo opportunity, while general admission provides standard entry to the venue and event.

Fans who purchase their tickets through TNA’s official merchandise store are instructed to bring their printed receipt to the venue, which will serve as their valid ticket for entry. Organizers have clarified that the newly announced host, AJ Francis, and the special guest appearances apply specifically to the Houston appearance.

Gisele Shaw Relinquishes MLP Canadian Title After NWA Signing

Following her recent exclusive contract signing with the National Wrestling Alliance, Gisele Shaw has relinquished the MLP Women’s Canadian Championship. Shaw officially dropped the title to Priscilla Kelly on MLP Mayhem.

According to industry reports, Shaw was originally expected to hold the championship until October. However, her new contract with the National Wrestling Alliance strictly prohibits her from working on television shows for other promotions. Analysts noted that Shaw’s sudden loss of the championship was primarily due to her new promotional deal rather than her past split with MLP owner Scott D’Amore earlier this year.

Despite that prior split, D’Amore still booked Shaw strongly throughout the summer, consistently presenting her as the focal point and face of the company’s women’s division. Shaw actually lost the title to Kelly during an August 8th television taping, though she did successfully retain the championship against B3CCA during a subsequent TV taping on August 15th. Following these events, Shaw officially bid farewell to MLP with a social media post.

Shaw now focuses entirely on her ongoing run with the National Wrestling Alliance, where she is currently reigning as the NWA World Women’s TV Champion, having captured that belt from Sirena Veil in late July 2026.

Steph De Lander Compares Creative Freedom in WWE, TNA, and ROH

Steph De Lander compares creative freedom in WWE, TNA, and ROH

Are there really any creative freedom differences amongst WWE, ROH, and TNA? Steph De Lander recently had the answer to it during an interview with TMZ Sports Inside The Ring, where she was asked what she enjoys in ROH when compared to TNA.

De Lander expressed her joy at being back in the physical side of wrestling, calling it incredible and what she has wanted to do her whole life and spent the last ten years doing. Having that almost taken away from her, she overcame a neck injury and returned to the ring within a few months. She returned from her injury in April and by August was on a Ring of Honor pay-per-view, noting that she is not just back in the ring, but on TV and pay-per-view, wrestling world champions, working for a new company, and exceeding all of her expectations and goals.

De Lander further opened up and praised ROH’s creative freedom, explaining how she can come up with all sorts of stuff and let them bleed out in her matches. She highlighted her love for discussing and brainstorming ideas, noting that being able to come into Ring of Honor with a bunch of ideas for her matches, gimmick, storyline, and whatever else allows her to be creative with her art. Even though she has only had a few matches there, she can already see the way it works and is excited to work with different people.

The 29-year-old Australian further shared that while she was allowed to be creative in TNA, she did not wrestle much for the promotion due to being injured for the majority of her time there. Despite the injury, she was very lucky to be featured in a huge major storyline. She participated in an engagement and a wedding, where she was able to write her own wedding vows and plan out a whole television wedding. They eventually got a divorce because her husband went insane in real life and quit the company, followed by her own departure later. She emphasized that there were many moments where she was allowed to be really creative at TNA.

However, while both TNA and ROH allowed her creative freedom, De Lander claimed WWE was a lot stricter that way. She believes it is good that WWE is strict with their creative because it is television and operates as the biggest show on that scale, so she understands why there are different levels of restraints on things. Concluding her thoughts, she reiterated that she was allowed to be creative in TNA and is excited to be creative in Ring of Honor, which remains her favorite part of the business.

De Lander recently faced Zayda Steel on the September 8, 2026 edition of Ring of Honor.

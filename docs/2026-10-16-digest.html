🔥 Rebel named first honorary board member of I AM ALS

Former AEW wrestler Tanea Brooks, widely known to wrestling fans as Rebel, is taking on an enhanced leadership role as she continues her advocacy work for I AM ALS. The non-profit group issued a press release announcing that Brooks has been named the very first honorary member of its board of directors.

The announcement follows the final congressional passage of the Accelerating Access to Critical Therapies for ALS Reauthorization Act of 2026, known as ACT for ALS. The U.S. Senate approved the bill by unanimous consent just ahead of the law's expiration, extending programs created by the landmark legislation through fiscal year 2031 and representing up to an additional 500 million dollars for ALS research and treatment access. The bill was co-written by I AM ALS co-founder Brian Wallach.

Brooks joined I AM ALS as an official ambassador in June 2026, just weeks after publicly sharing her ALS diagnosis following a long health battle to figure out her symptoms. Upon joining, she launched a call-to-action public service announcement encouraging fans to step into the ring with I AM ALS and help spread awareness of the legislative efforts. Her advocacy was instrumental in getting the bill across the finish line as she personally rallied fans to sign a petition urging Congress to reauthorize the act.

As an honorary board member, Brooks will continue to elevate her story, lived experience, and platform to advance the mission of I AM ALS and activate fans nationwide to take action on behalf of people living with the terminal neurodegenerative disease. Advocacy for ALS is a major priority in her personal battle, and she plans to continue fighting for a cure and expanded access to treatments.

In the months since her announcement, Brooks has transformed her platform into a powerful force for the community, turning career celebrations into opportunities to drive awareness, raise critical funds, and mobilize action. All Elite Wrestling released Rebel Heart-branded merchandise in her honor, including a shirt, bracelet, and a Rebel Micro Brawler figure. Additionally, Upper Deck released a limited edition Rebel trading card, with all proceeds going to I AM ALS and Team Gleason. On September 9, a special Rebel Heart episode of Dynamite aired live on TBS and HBO Max from Athens, Georgia, honoring Brooks and raising funds for both organizations.

I AM ALS CEO Andrea Goodman praised Brooks for her dedication. Tanea turned a devastating moment into immediate action, calling us soon after her diagnosis to join the movement, Goodman said. She uses her platform, humor, vulnerability, and unwavering honesty to make people pay attention and take action. Welcoming Rebel to our board as Congress reauthorizes ACT for ALS is a powerful reflection of what she has helped us achieve and what this movement is built on, which is collective power and visibility of the very people living with the disease.

Reflecting on the outpouring of support she has received, Brooks noted that one silver lining of her diagnosis has been seeing how much people care. The greatest thing ALS has given me is to be able to see how much people care and love and support me before I am gone, she shared earlier this year. Supporters can donate to the cause at the organization official website.

Tony Khan discusses Skydance corporate leadership reaction to AEW

During the media call ahead of the pay-per-view event, AEW President Tony Khan shared an overwhelmingly positive outlook regarding the promotion's future under its new corporate structure. Following Skydance’s acquisition of Warner Bros Discovery and its merger with Paramount to form a new mega-media corporation, Khan discussed his recent talks with top executives and explained why he believes the ownership change will greatly benefit AEW and its fans.

At the center of Khan's optimism is his relationship with Skydance CEO David Ellison. According to Khan, Ellison has been very excited and positive about AEW, strongly believing in the product and the company being built. Khan noted that having a solid foundation with Ellison is crucial since Skydance now holds massive influence over AEW's primary television partner. Khan expressed deep respect for Ellison, calling him a fantastic leader who has engaged in productive discussions regarding the future strategy of the company.

While acknowledging that corporate changes are coming, Khan is confident they will only help AEW grow stronger. He pointed out that several familiar executives who have championed AEW in the past will continue to play pivotal roles in the new corporate hierarchy. Khan specifically praised Channing Dungey, who has been a great leader for Warner Bros Television and signed a new deal to lead television for both WBD and Paramount under Skydance. Dungey has long been recognized as a staunch supporter of the wrestling promotion.

Khan also highlighted JB Perrette as another critical ally within the newly formed corporate structure. Having grown closer to Perrette during the 2024 television renewal process, Khan praised his supportive approach and expressed excitement about him taking on a massive leadership role in streaming and operations for Skydance.

Beyond corporate relationships, Khan emphasized tangible business success as a driver for his confidence, noting that AEW has continued to grow its footprint on HBO Max alongside sold-out live events. He believes that Skydance's immense scale will allow AEW to package its programming, including Dynamite, Collision, and pay-per-views, alongside a broader content library to reach even larger audiences. Ultimately, Khan feels that the merger represents the best path forward for AEW, its fans, and its broadcast partners.

Mike Bailey reflects on AEW Montreal homecoming

Mike Bailey recently reflected on his emotional Montreal homecoming during AEW Redemption. Earlier this year, Bailey participated in and won the AEW International Title number one contender ladder match at the event.

Recently speaking to Wrestling Classic, Bailey reflected on that moment and winning the match in his hometown, describing it as absolutely amazing. He noted that it was not just the fact that he was back home in the arena where he first went to see professional wrestling, but that the connection was even deeper, emphasizing how tight and close the Quebec wrestling community truly is.

Literally, I remember standing on top of that ladder and seeing in the audience my family, my mother and my cousins and my uncles and aunts had come to see me do pro wrestling for the first time in my life, he shared. Bailey added that while he has been wrestling for 20 years, that was the first time his mother ever came to a wrestling match. Seeing his family and close friends meant something special to him, especially because they did not tell him beforehand and were simply there to surprise him.

Seeing faces of people from the Quebec wrestling community all over the crowd that I knew personally, not just people that I have met in passing, but people that I know literally in every direction, seeing people that I am close with and then grabbing that contract that guaranteed me a title shot at Grand Slam was definitely a highlight of my career, he finished.

Bailey is currently scheduled to participate in the Clockwork Carnage match at AEW WrestleDream 2026 for the World Championship.

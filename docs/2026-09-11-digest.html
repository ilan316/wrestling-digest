🔥 Will Ospreay discusses new match concepts, All In entrance, and Kenny Omega match

Will Ospreay and Tony Khan are very possibly close to bringing brand-new match concepts to AEW. At AEW All In, Ospreay defeated Kenny Omega to win the AEW World title. Shortly after, Ospreay appeared for an interview with Chris Van Vliet to discuss his future with the AEW World Championship, sharing details about discussions he had with Khan regarding new match ideas. Ospreay explained that he has spoken to Khan about trying out different matches that have not been done before by combining elements of matches he likes, much like Khan's ingenious Casino Gauntlet idea. Ospreay loves sports-based wrestling, tournaments like the World Cup, and seeing people come from behind to secure a victory, inspiring him to develop new match styles. He also noted that he would love to test himself against the old pillars of AEW and would like to revisit a match with Chris Jericho, the promotion's first ever champion. Ospreay is currently scheduled to face former World Champion Jon Moxley at AEW All Out on September 26.

Reflecting on his match against Kenny Omega at Wembley Stadium, Ospreay stated that they are currently tied with two wins each in their rivalry and may never clash in the ring again. Omega won their first two matches in PWG in 2015 and NJPW in 2023, while Ospreay won at Forbidden Door 2023 and All In. Ospreay expressed that he is perfectly fine with leaving it tied, calling it poetic. He also commented on the physical toll it takes on Omega following his battle with diverticulitis, noting that people do not always realize the preparation required for such big matches. Ospreay considered it one of the biggest honors of his career that Omega allowed him to kick out of the One Winged Angel, a move Omega has protected for 14 years, with Kota Ibushi being the only other person to ever kick out of it.

The main event nearly looked different due to a last-minute change. Ospreay explained that just two or three matches before their bout, they found out another match had used their planned spots. Pressured to improvise under a time crunch, they came up with Plan B. The new sequence featured Omega kicking out of the One Winged Angel at one, Ospreay kicking out of the Stormbreaker at one, and Ospreay finally securing the pin after a Hidden Blade. Ospreay praised the alternative plan as being way better than their original idea.

Malakai Black remembered his friend and fellow musician as the definition of a good person, working his own recent matches with a heavy heart. A celebration of life for Williams, who was well known outside the ring as the guitarist for Every Time I Die, is scheduled for September 27 in his hometown of Buffalo, New York.

The wrestling world also suffered another major loss on the exact same day with the passing of Memphis territory institution Superstar Bill Dundee at the age of 82. While Dundee lived a long and accomplished life after battling dementia, the sudden loss of Williams at 48 has left a profound void among fans and peers alike, with numerous stars sharing their final respects.

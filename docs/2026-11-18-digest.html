🔥 Anthony Bowens expected to remain with AEW

Anthony Bowens is expected to continue his career with All Elite Wrestling. Following the expiration of his contract in early October alongside former tag team partner Max Caster, Bowens' future had been the subject of backstage speculation. However, internal sources indicate that the promotion plans to re-sign him, and he may have already finalized a new agreement or will do so very soon.

Unlike Caster, whose profile was promptly removed from the official AEW roster page after his departure—leading to him competing against Moose at TNA Bound for Glory 2026—Bowens was never taken off the company website. Speculation about a potential reunion as The Acclaimed was also recently put to rest when a fan asked if the former partners would ever team up again, to which Bowens offered a brief, one-word response: no.

Contract discussions experienced some delays in October, partly due to the tragic deaths of fellow performers PAC and The Butcher, which left the locker room dealing with much more serious matters. Bowens previously addressed his uncertain future during an October interview, noting that he preferred not to speculate publicly, stating he was taking things one day at a time while appreciating fan support, adding that he knew deep down what he wanted to do.

Before his absence from television, Bowens spent much of 2026 working alongside Samoa Joe, HOOK, Katsuyori Shibata, and Action Andretti as part of The Opps. His most recent match took place during the All In London Buy-In in August, when the group lost to Lio Rush and The Rascalz. He also worked matches on Ring of Honor and in a dark match ahead of Dynamite in July, including teaming with HOOK at Brawl in the Ballpark. While backstage expectations strongly point toward his retention, the exact status of his new contract remains officially unconfirmed, and it remains unclear when he will return to television or if he will continue as part of The Opps.

🔥 The Young Bucks and Nick Jackson on their original vision for AEW as 'PWG on illegal steroids'

During a recent Q&A session at an event ahead of AEW Full Gear, AEW co-founders and Executive Vice Presidents The Young Bucks, Matt and Nick Jackson, reflected on the origins of the company and how the legendary independent promotion Pro Wrestling Guerrilla influenced their vision. Nick Jackson asked the audience if there were any PWG fans before offering his striking description of the promotion they helped build. I would like to say AEW became PWG on illegal steroids, Nick joked, drawing laughter from the crowd. Matt humorously chimed in, asking if he was referring to the non-doctor-prescribed ones. Nick continued by explaining that this was what they envisioned AEW could be, and sure enough, years later, every show features PWG-style matches booked while the crowds get hotter and hotter.

PWG was a Los Angeles-based independent wrestling promotion that is currently on an extended hiatus since 2023 with no signs of an imminent return, though the Young Bucks have previously expressed interest in potentially bringing it back someday. The brothers spent over a decade there between 2007 and 2018, holding the PWG World Tag Team Championships four times for a total of 631 days. At its height, PWG brought in talent from all around the world, presenting unique cards featuring comedy and satire amidst incredible, high-flying, fast-paced action in front of an enthusiastic fan base, blending various wrestling styles together. Their extensive tenure and experience continue to inspire their AEW presentation and showings. AEW commentator Excalibur was the voice of PWG and previously noted that around 80 employees of AEW worked at least one match in the indie promotion, including major names like Ricochet, Kenny Omega, the Briscoe Brothers, El Generico, and Kevin Steen.

During the session, Matt and Nick also looked back on their initial conversations with AEW CEO Tony Khan. Nick admitted that he initially did not believe Khan at all when discussing the plans for a new major wrestling company, humorously adding that Khan still hates him for that. Of course, Nick now apologizes for his early doubts. Meanwhile, Matt felt that Khan's contagious energy and excitement made his gut tell him that starting AEW was the right move over any other options they had on the table. Now nearly eight years into the company existence, Matt feels like he is living a dream, noting that it all worked out and he has to pinch himself sometimes at how wild the journey has been. Today, the industry veterans are currently AEW World Tag Team Champions on their fourth reign, proving that their indie history and roots have played a pivotal role in shaping a cornerstone of modern professional wrestling.

🔥 Video game 'Foxhole' features tribute to late AEW star PAC

A unique tribute to the late AEW star PAC, whose real name was Benjamin Satterley, has emerged in the gaming world with the multiplayer war game Foxhole introducing a new weapon bearing his initials and iconic moniker. The weapon, named the P-A-C Bastard Burst Launcher, is set to arrive as part of the game's upcoming Engines of War update, scheduled for release on December 7.

The launcher is designed as an anti-tank weapon capable of firing three independently propelled White Ash rockets in rapid succession. According to the official description released by the game developers, unleashing a rapid barrage of three independently propelled White Ash rockets, the P-A-C Bastard Burst Launcher brings a powerful new anti-tank weapon to the Warden infantry arsenal. Its unusual design earned it the name Bastard due to the dangerous development process involved in creating White Ash canisters capable of withstanding the launch. Within the lore of the game, developers noted the name also stems from all the bones burned to ash while developing it, matching PAC's famous wrestling nickname, The Bastard.

Originally released in 2022 following an early access period starting in 2017, Foxhole is a massively multiplayer online war game available on Steam for $29.99 in which thousands of players participate in persistent conflicts. Players contribute through frontline combat, reconnaissance, logistics, base building, and coordinating military operations, with battles unfolding across a shared online world where every individual soldier is a player shaping the outcome of a war lasting for weeks.

This gaming tribute arrives nearly two months after PAC passed away on September 27 at the age of 40 in Des Plaines, Illinois. Bronchial asthma was subsequently identified as his official cause of death. PAC established himself as one of professional wrestling's most accomplished performers, competing at a high level in WWE before becoming a prominent, highly respected member of the AEW roster. Known for his explosive athleticism, hard-hitting offense, and technical wrestling ability, his passing prompted numerous tributes from across the wrestling industry.

His wife, Natalie Satterley, shared an emotional social media tribute reflecting on his legacy and marking ten weeks since she last embraced him, while expressing gratitude for the outpouring of support. She noted that one of the truest testaments to his legacy is reflected in the kindness, care, and support received from family, friends, work colleagues, and his many fans, which has helped his family get through such a difficult time. The inclusion of the P-A-C Bastard Burst Launcher in Foxhole represents an unusual crossover between professional wrestling and gaming, offering another meaningful way for supporters to remember the late star.

🔥 Matches set for the November 18 episode of AEW Dynamite

Tony Khan has announced several high-stakes matches for this week’s AEW Dynamite, taking place at the Mission Ballroom in Denver, Colorado. With just hours left until showtime, Khan has unveiled an initial set of Continental Classic matches as well as a major championship bout.

The Continental Classic kicks off with an intriguing Blue League showdown between former AEW World Trios Champions Hangman Adam Page and ROH World Champion Bandido. The timing is particularly interesting because Page and Bandido were just on the same side during AEW Full Gear, where they teamed with Brody King in a brutal Texas Death Match against Swerve Strickland, Kofi Kingston, and Austin Creed. After losing their championships in a violent bout featuring weapons, blood, and punishment, and with Bandido showing off a nasty head wound following the event, the two partners must now immediately square off for crucial tournament points. This marks the Continental Classic debut for both men, who join a Blue League field that also features Brody King, Jay White, Ricochet, and Daniel Garcia.

Meanwhile, Gold League action will feature current champion Jon Moxley going one-on-one against Orange Cassidy. Moxley versus Cassidy takes place after the Death Riders teamed with the Conglomeration in Anarchy in the Arena at Full Gear. In another Gold League match, Speedball Mike Bailey will square off against Nick Wayne. Bailey is returning to fight in the tournament after an incredible showing in the Gold League last year, now facing the red-hot rising star Nick Wayne.

In addition to the tournament bouts, Khan also announced a four-way match for the AEW TBS Championship. Queen Aminata will defend her title against AZM, Maya World, and Mina Shirakawa. After travel delays kept Maya World from her previous TBS Title shot, she will finally get her opportunity in this multi-woman bout, fighting the champion and two other challengers looking to dethrone her.

The Continental Classic follows a round-robin format, meaning everyone in each league will eventually face each other. Matches have a twenty minute time limit, and outside interference is prohibited. The tournament will continue through December, with the semifinals and final scheduled for AEW Worlds End on December 19.

Sheamus fuels AEW rumors after posting photos with Kazuchika Okada

Former WWE star Sheamus recently took to social media to share photos with AEW icon Kazuchika Okada, reigniting speculation that he could soon join the promotion. Sheamus posted the pictures while attending a red carpet event in New York City for The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping, the newest cinematic entry in the franchise which officially debuts in US theaters on November 20. For the outing, Sheamus wore a black suit while Okada donned a patterned jacket, with Sheamus captioning the post, The real life Hunger Games Sunrise on the Reaping.

Sheamus has been a free agent since early October when his WWE contract expired, bringing an end to his 19-year tenure with the company. He began his professional wrestling career on the European indie circuit in the early 2000s before joining WWE developmental territory Florida Championship Wrestling in 2006 and moving to the main roster three years later. During his lengthy career with WWE, he captured nearly every major title available, including three reigns as WWE Champion and one reign as World Heavyweight Champion.

Since becoming a free agent, Sheamus has remained tight-lipped about his next career move. While attending a meet-and-greet session earlier this month, he sidestepped direct questions about signing with AEW, telling fans instead that he is enjoying his time off and joking that his wife is the one making all the decisions regarding his future.

Prior to the Okada photo, rumors swirled around a potential surprise appearance at AEW Full Gear on November 14. Sheamus was scheduled for an appearance at Big Event NY that same day and reportedly asked about leaving early, citing a need to attend a wedding. However, subsequent backstage updates indicated he was never spotted at Full Gear and no deal had been finalized. Several prominent AEW wrestlers, including Adam Copeland and Claudio Castagnoli, have previously expressed interest in seeing him join the company, though prior reports indicated no deal was close.

The new photos with Okada have immediately sent wrestling fans into a frenzy, with many imagining a potential dream match between the two stars. Social media users have already begun fantasy booking, with some suggesting a lengthy match between Okada and Sheamus at an event like AEW Dynasty. While the photos do not confirm an imminent signing and neither wrestler has announced plans to work together, the timing certainly gives fans plenty to discuss while Sheamus continues to enjoy his break from the ring.

AEW and Mercedes Moné partner with USA Wrestling women's freestyle team

USA Wrestling is pleased to announce a major new collaboration with global pro wrestling superstar Mercedes Mone, media entrepreneur Keven Undergaro and All Elite Wrestling aimed at increasing visibility, opportunity and mainstream awareness for U.S. women's wrestling athletes. The initiative is designed to help promote U.S. female wrestlers, elevate the profile of women's wrestling and support athletes in building their individual brands and careers beyond competition. The 2028 Olympic Games, being contested on home soil in Los Angeles, provide a unique opportunity to connect the U.S. women's wrestling team with the mainstream American audience, a focal point of this partnership.

USA Wrestling expressed enthusiasm for the alliance, noting the impact of working alongside such a talented and passionate group to help increase the visibility of these amazing athletes and further the sport. As part of the leadership team, Mercedes Mone will serve as Chief Executive Envoy to the U.S. women's wrestling team, helping mentor, promote and raise awareness for U.S. female wrestlers and the women's wrestling program overall. Leveraging her incredible global fanbase and social media reach, Mone will spotlight athletes and help introduce new audiences to the sport.

Reflecting on the milestone, Mone compared the initiative to her past achievements in the industry. Ten years ago I was part of an evolution that transformed women's professional wrestling. Today marks the first day of the Women's Amateur Wrestling Evolution, an evolution I'm honored to lead along with Team USA. These athletes are world-class competitors and warriors who deserve the highest recognition.

All Elite Wrestling joins the initiative as the Official Content Partner to the U.S. women's wrestling team. AEW will help raise awareness for U.S. female wrestlers through social media promotion, collaborative content opportunities and exposure across AEW platforms. As AEW travels weekly across the country, select athletes will also have opportunities to attend events, engage backstage and connect with fans and talent.

Tony Khan, AEW CEO, GM and Head of Creative, shared his excitement over the partnership. On behalf of All Elite Wrestling, we are thrilled to be named the Official Content Partner of the USA women's wrestling team. It is a true honor to work with these amazing athletes and introduce them to a whole new audience across AEW's platforms, shining a global spotlight on their passion and dedication to the sport.

USA Wrestling serves as the national governing body for wrestling in the United States, operating as a member of the U.S. Olympic and Paralympic Committee and United World Wrestling, the international federation. The organization provides competition and programs for nearly 400,000 members of all ages, including athletes, coaches, officials, and club leaders. Alongside this governing body, the involvement of figures like Mone, an ESPY winner, actress, advocate, and widely regarded professional wrestling icon with historic milestones in the industry, brings unprecedented crossover appeal to the amateur athletic landscape.

Jeff Jarrett missed AEW Full Gear due to travel disruptions

Just hours before AEW Full Gear took place in Edmonton, Canada, widespread FAA-related travel disruptions impacted the entire United States. These travel issues caused problems not only for AEW, but also for WWE shows and TNA Destination X. Wrestling veteran Jeff Jarrett was among those caught in the travel chaos, preventing him from making it to the show.

Speaking on his podcast, My World With Jeff Jarrett, the veteran opened up about the ordeal and explained the exact chain of events that kept him away. Jarrett detailed how his Saturday travel itinerary included a connection in Minneapolis that initially seemed to be going smoothly before everything fell apart. I landed in Minneapolis 10 minutes early, Jarrett recalled. Get off, go to the lounge because I have got literally 65 minutes. Get down to the gate, ready to take off. We do not have a first officer, so we are going to be delayed 40 minutes. And that 40 minutes turned into five and a half hours.

As the hours ticked away, Jarrett realized he would no longer be able to reach the venue in time for the show. During that whole process, I kept ticking it down, he stated. I am like, wait a minute, I am going to land, and the Buy-In is going to be over. And so once we got to that point of no return, I said, how do I get back to Nashville? Realizing he had missed his opportunity to participate in Full Gear, he caught a flight home to Nashville instead. To add insult to injury, Jarrett revealed that while he made it back safely, his checked bag continued traveling without him and ended up in Phoenix.

Tony Khan addresses whether AEW Blood & Guts could become a PPV

Tony Khan does not have any intention of changing AEW Blood and Guts from a television special into its own pay-per-view event. Coming off the ratings success of Blood and Guts 2026, Khan appeared on the Battleground Podcast and was asked if we could ever see Blood and Guts become one of AEWs traditional pay-per-views. In his response, Khan emphasized how important it is for AEW to have television specials that draw in strong ratings and give fans something special to look forward to on standard broadcasts.

The Blood and Guts 2026 Dynamite episode, despite facing direct competition from WWE NXT, successfully averaged 792,000 viewers on TBS and drew a 0.24 rating in the key 18-49 demographic. Khan highlighted the significance of these numbers during his podcast appearance. I think it is great to have this episode of television that does so well for AEW, Khan said. This was the most viewers 18-49 on TBS for AEW in years, dating back to Blood and Guts 2024. It is about two and a half years. This is the most young viewers we brought into AEW in two and a half years since that previous Blood and Guts. And back then, we did not simulcast the show on HBO Max yet, so there is even more viewers on top of that, young viewers, viewers of all ages. For our total viewership, it is one of the highest we have had in years. In particular, for the young demos, this is the most young viewers AEW has had in many, many years.

Khan continued to explain the strategic value of keeping such high-profile matches on television rather than moving them behind a paywall. It is very exciting, Khan said. So to have a marquee episode of television that consistently delivers huge ratings for us, that is awesome. It is really important to have signature episodes of AEW Dynamite and AEW Collision that the fans can look forward to. Huge events that have a pay-per-view feel, like Grand Slam or AEW Blood and Guts.

Blood and Guts, which serves as AEWs corporate version of the traditional WarGames match style, has been a staple of the promotion since 2021. The 2026 edition of the brutal double-ring structure featured notable victories. On the mens side, the winning team consisted of Kazuchika Okada, Kevin Knight, Kyle Fletcher, Wardlow, Mark Davis, and Jake Doyle. On the womens side, the victory went to the team of Willow Nightingale, Kris Statlander, Harley Cameron, Alex Windsor, Jamie Hayter, and Queen Aminata.

Looking ahead at the schedule, AEW has several other marquee television specials lined up following the success of Blood and Guts. Winter is Coming 2026 is set to take place from Monterrey, Mexico, on Wednesday, December 9. This upcoming event marks AEWs first-ever show in Monterrey and represents the very first time the promotion has held an event in Mexico outside of Mexico City. Additionally, other upcoming television specials on the calendar include AEWs heavy Thanksgiving week programming, Holiday Bash 2026, and New Year Smash 2027.

🔥 AEW's Dr. Britt Baker teased for CMLL appearance at Arena Mexico

CMLL Women’s World Champion Persephone has strongly teased that AEW star Dr. Britt Baker, D.M.D. is coming to Arena Mexico for the promotion's upcoming show on Friday, October 16, as part of the Month of the Amazonas celebration. Following the Viernes Espectacular event, Persephone appeared in a backstage video released by CMLL to address fans who complain about how she wins matches and defends her title.

During her backstage comments, Persephone addressed her critics before dropping massive hints about her surprise guest. A doctor, or better yet, a female doctor, whom you might already know, Persephone stated. You have complained, you have cried, you have whined that you don't like how I win my matches, how I defend my championships. But I am still champion. And as the great champion I am, I have a surprise for you. Next Friday, since it's the Month of the Amazonas, I'm bringing you a brave, intelligent woman, D.M.D. Persephone then flashed the signature DMD hand signal and winked directly at the camera, leaving little doubt she was referring to Baker.

The relationship between Baker and Persephone was recently solidified on AEW television. The two teamed with Mercedes Moné on the September 30 episode of Dynamite, which featured a tribute to PAC, though they came up short against Marina Shafir, Hyan, and Thunder Rosa. Persephone last defended her CMLL World Women's Championship at the promotion's 93rd Anniversary show on September 18, retaining against Mina Shirakawa, Starlight Kid, and Tessa Blanchard in a four-way match via disqualification after pretending Blanchard struck her with the belt. Persephone's most recent AEW TBS Championship defense came at All Out on September 26, when she defeated Dani Luna, who answered her open challenge.

The tease follows a tough night for Persephone in the ring at Arena Mexico. She teamed with Reyna Isis in a two-out-of-three-falls match against Hera and Olympia, which she and Isis lost. Hera pinned Persephone off a flying splash in the first fall for bragging rights, and after the score was evened, Olympia press slammed Isis to win the match in the third fall. It remains unclear whether Baker will appear as a manager, wrestle in a tag team bout alongside Persephone, or potentially set up a world title defense against a challenger like Hera.

Brian Cage details working with Steven Spielberg on Disclosure Day

AEW stars Brian Cage, Lance Archer, and Chavo Guerrero recently stepped into the ring for the silver screen, making an appearance in Steven Spielberg's Disclosure Day, which was released earlier this year. During a recent podcast interview, Cage recalled how the opportunity came together and reflected on what it was like to work with the legendary director.

Someone else pointed out that Brian Cage worked for Steven Spielberg before John Cena or the Rock, Cage noted during the discussion. He credited Chavo Guerrero with helping him get the job. According to Cage, he was originally set to work on a television show alongside Guerrero, but that project was postponed. In the meantime, Guerrero, who has increasingly become a bridge between Hollywood and professional wrestling, suggested that Cage might be the ideal fit for the Spielberg film.

Some time after submitting his audition, Cage received word that Spielberg loved his charisma and distinct look, ultimately wanting him for the role. Cage recalled his reaction by saying he was shocked that Steven Spielberg was watching his stuff, adding that the director saw him and exclaimed that was what a pro wrestler looks like and that he needed that guy in his movie.

Cage also shared that Spielberg revealed himself to be a wrestling fan. Because of this, the director even allowed Cage to pick his own on-screen opponent, a choice that ultimately went to Lance Archer. Both wrestlers make their appearance during the film's opening sequence.

Looking ahead at other potential Hollywood aspirations, Cage expressed that he would have loved to play a Wolverine variant in Deadpool and Wolverine. Furthermore, he noted that he would be entirely satisfied with any other small appearances in the Marvel Cinematic Universe, or participating in a high-stakes fight scene with a legendary action star like Jet Li.

Steph De Lander reflects on leaving TNA for Ring of Honor

Steph De Lander was one of many former TNA stars to request and receive their release earlier this year, and it didn't take long for De Lander to land on her feet, as she's begun to compete regularly in Ring of Honor. In a recent interview, De Lander revealed she's quite happy to find herself in ROH now, both because it allows her creative freedom, and because she is able to wrestle again, having previously missed significant time due to a severe neck injury. I returned from my injury in April, De Lander said, and by August, I'm on Ring of Honor PPV. She noted she is not just back in the ring, but on TV, on PPV, wrestling world champions, and working for a new company, having exceeded all her expectations and goals. She highlighted that the best thing about Ring of Honor is the creativity, because she can come up with all sorts of stuff and let that bleed out into her matches, expressing excitement to work with different people and be creative with her art.

The creative freedom in ROH is the same type of freedom De Lander had with TNA, though that ultimately wasn't enough to override the bad feelings that led to her requesting her TNA release. Several months later, there is still no thaw between her and her former employer. At the moment, there is no relationship, De Lander said, adding that there is no communication and there has been no communication. The last communication was her demanding her release papers right now, which she received in an email, and that was that. Since her departure, De Lander has run into some TNA higher ups from her time, revealing that they had ignored her and vice versa in order to avoid confrontation. Despite the bad feelings, De Lander didn't dismiss a TNA return, though she believes it is a long way off, if it were to happen at all. The way that wrestling is is everyone ping pongs all over the place, De Lander said, noting she will never say she will never do this thing, but for right now she does not see it as an option in the near future and conversations would have to be had that she does not care to have anytime soon.

Turning back to her current time with Ring of Honor, De Lander was asked if her recent appearances, including challenging Deonna Purrazzo for the Ring of Honor Women's Pure Championship at Death Before Dishonor, meant she was under contract with either ROH or AEW. According to De Lander, that is not the case yet. As of right now, I'm not contracted, De Lander said, explaining she is doing pay-per-appearance at Ring of Honor, which she has done at TNA and ROH, and companies have done that with a lot of talent. She acknowledged that becoming contracted is probably a reality at some point because she is not going to be a free agent forever, but as for the specific details, everyone is just going to have to wait and see together.

AEW TNT Champion Darby Allin climbs Mont Blanc

AEW TNT Champion Darby Allin Ascends Mont Blanc: Nice To Be In The Mountains Again

If you thought Darby Allin scratched his itch to climb some of the tallest mountains on Earth like he did last year at Mount Everest, and was done, think again.

Still craving more, the now three-time TNT Champion recently conquered Chamonix-Mont-Blanc. On his Instagram Stories, Allin posted a picture from the mountain saying, Nice to be in the mountains again. It's interesting to note that Mont-Blanc, meaning White Mountain, is the highest mountain in the Alps and in Western Europe, rising to 15,772 feet on the border between France and Italy.

Prior to checking off another groundbreaking feat on his personal bucket list, Allin held another hard fought TNT title defense where he rose victorious against The Protostar Kyle Fletcher at AEW Dynamite Grand Slam France on Wednesday. This week marks the second title defense he's had since securing the championship at All In London against then champion, Kevin Knight.

Allin previously pushed his limits to ascend Mount Everest on May 18, 2025. Though he faced exhaustion, oxygen shortages, and psychological distress, Allin proudly planted the AEW flag at the top of the mountain, making him the first professional wrestler in the entire industry to reach the summit of Mount Everest.

🔄 המשך 🔥 Clockwork Carnage qualifier matches set for 3-hour AEW Dynamite special

AEW has officially filled out the roadmap for the remaining spots in the upcoming WrestleDream pay-per-view main event by scheduling five massive qualifying matches for an upcoming three-hour special episode of Dynamite and Collision.

The special broadcast will feature high-stakes action with championship implications tied directly to the eight-person bout. TNT Champion Darby Allin will put his title on the line against Hechicero, and International Champion Kazuchika Okada will defend his belt against Myron Reed, with both titles serving as secondary stakes in their respective qualification bids.

Rounding out the newly announced qualification matches for the Orlando event, Ricochet will face Ace Austin, Konosuke Takeshita will collide with Brian Cage, and Adam Copeland will go one-on-one with Josh Alexander.

In addition to the qualification bouts, AEW has stacked the three-hour television special with two major six-person tag team matches. Mercedes Mone will join forces with Divine Dominion members Megan Bayne and Lena Kross to take on the Triangle of Madness trio of Thekla, Skye Blue, and Julia Hart. Meanwhile, the Death Riders group consisting of Jon Moxley, Gabe Kidd, and Daniel Garcia will lock horns with the Don Callis Family representatives Mark Davis, Jake Doyle, and Wardlow.

🔄 המשך Kris Statlander & Harley Cameron become #1 contenders for AEW Women's World Tag Titles

Kris Statlander and Harley Cameron have officially punched their ticket to a championship opportunity.

During the October 10 episode of Collision Grand Slam France in Paris, the team earned the status of number one contenders by surviving a brutal three-way match against the Sisters of Sin and Divine Dominion. Divine Dominion dominated the early portions of the bout, keeping Cameron isolated before she managed to make the hot tag to Statlander.

In the chaotic closing moments, Cameron sent Skye Blue crashing off the top turnbuckle and into her opponents on the floor. That paved the way for Statlander to capitalize, connecting with the Saturday Night Fever on Julia Hart to secure the decisive pinfall.

With the victory in France, they have cemented their title match against champions Alex Windsor and Jamie Hayter at the upcoming WrestleDream pay-per-view in Orlando.

🔶 AEW News

🔥 AEW stars react to WWE Men's Money in the Bank match

Some pro wrestlers will get tribal when it suits them, but when they are not working, most seem to put their professional fellowship with other performers above allegiance to the company they are signed to. Mostly, we see that around tragedy, as we have over the past month around the deaths of Andy The Butcher Williams, Ben PAC/Neville Satterley, and Jeff Cannonball Guerriero. But sometimes wrestlers get so excited by what we all see on our screens that they can not help but act like fans themselves. We saw two examples of that from the main event of Saturday night's Money in the Bank premium live event in New Orleans, Louisiana.

That main event was this year's men's Money in the Bank ladder match. Je'Von Evans did not win it, but he was a focal point of the match. Evans was his usually impressive self throughout and perfectly took a crazy bump from the top of a ladder in the ring through the roof of an ambulance parked beside it. This moment crashed into over four million views across socials as the wildest bump of the year.

It popped someone who has been known to take a wild bump or two and is not usually in the habit of saying flattering things about WWE. Ricochet made no mention of the company, just the bouncy brother turning into a star for it, posting that he is honestly a Je'Von Evans fan.

Another AEW name who spent time at WWE popped for the ending of this year's men's ladder match. Andrade El Idolo could not help shouting out his lucha brother after Penta claimed the case in New Orleans. Despite now working for rival promotions, Andrade is showing some love to a former AEW colleague following a major WWE victory. The reigning AEW National Champion took to social media to congratulate Penta on winning the match. Congratulations to a good friend and teammate Penta, Andrade wrote via X, adding long live Mexico, you bastards and sharing the Spanish phrasing of felicidades a un buen amigo y compañero Penta viva Mexico cabrones.

Andrade and Penta never had a singles rivalry during their time together in AEW, but the two shared the ring on several occasions in tag team and multi-person matches. Andrade also has plenty of experience competing in Money in the Bank ladder matches himself, having participated in the annual bout during his previous WWE runs in 2019, 2024, and 2025.

As for his current AEW commitments, Andrade is scheduled to return to action this Wednesday night when he teams with AEW World Champion Will Ospreay against Samoa Joe and Tommaso Ciampa. The tag team showdown will headline the special three-hour combined episode of AEW Dynamite and AEW Collision from the Wolstein Center in Cleveland, Ohio.

The premium live event on October 10 also featured other major moments. It kicked off with Roman Reigns standing tall over LA Knight in a World Heavyweight Championship bout. Liv Morgan defeated Stephanie Vaquer and Becky Lynch in a triple threat match to kick off her fourth reign as Women's World Champion. Additionally, Tiffany Stratton won the women's Money in the Bank ladder match, while Penta secured the men's division victory to add another milestone to his career.

Anthony Bowens provides knee injury and future update

During a recent interview with Michael Fairman, former AEW World Tag Team Champion Anthony Bowens provided a candid health update detailing the various injuries he has tallied up throughout his professional wrestling career. Among his current ailments, Bowens revealed that he is dealing with a slightly torn meniscus in his right knee.

Listing his past injuries off the top of his head, Bowens shared that he has suffered a few diagnosed concussions alongside others that likely went undiagnosed, experienced a seizure in the ring, and previously tore his left meniscus which required surgical repair. He also noted that his MCL had to be repaired at one point and that he once bruised nerves in his lower back, laughing as he added that there is probably a lot more he could not immediately remember.

Beyond his physical condition, Bowens addressed his professional future following reports that he and Max Caster became free agents after their time with All Elite Wrestling. Bowens was last in action for the promotion at the 2026 All In event, where he competed on The Buy In pre-show.

When asked about what lies ahead for his career, Bowens laughed and expressed hesitation to speak too openly to avoid triggering widespread speculation across various wrestling dirt sheets. He stated that he does not yet know where things are going to go and is choosing to take everything one day at a time. Expressing gratitude for the overwhelming support and feedback he has received from fans wanting him to stay, Bowens concluded that he knows deep down what he wants to do and will see if it works out.

AEW's Anna Jay to undergo surgery

AEW star Anna Jay is facing another extended absence as she prepares to undergo shoulder surgery. Jay has not been seen on television since June, when she and former tag team partner Tay Melo challenged for the AEW Women's World Tag Team Championships on an episode of Collision. Her absence was further explained after AEW President Tony Khan noted back in July that the star was awaiting an MRI.

During a Twitch stream in late September, Jay provided a personal update regarding her health. She shared that she would be undergoing surgery within a couple of weeks, meaning she has either already undergone the procedure or will soon. Jay admitted she initially hoped to avoid the operation and sought out multiple opinions from different doctors and surgeons before deciding it was the correct way to proceed. She explained that if she did not get the issue fixed, her shoulder would just keep getting dislocated. While she is a little sad about the situation, she is trying to stay positive and focus on her recovery.

When asked about the recovery timeline, Jay stated that she expects it to be a pretty typical timeframe for a shoulder issue, though she chose not to publicly put a specific timeframe on her return to avoid getting her hopes up. This marks the second time Jay has faced this specific procedure during her time with the company, having previously undergone shoulder surgery back in March 2021 when she was a member of the Dark Order after suffering an injury in training. Neither Jay nor Khan have publicly revealed how she sustained the injury this time around.

Jay's name made wrestling headlines throughout the first half of the year as fans speculated about her status with the company. It was widely rumored that her contract was coming to an end in May, a detail Jay herself confirmed. However, Khan later confirmed during a media call that Jay had recently signed a contract extension, though the exact length of the new deal was not disclosed.

Now 28 years old, Jay first debuted for AEW during the pandemic era in 2020, establishing herself on television not long after beginning her career in the ring. She originally started training in 2018 and had her first official match in 2019.

Jim Ross back in hospital due to low oxygen

Legendary wrestling commentator Jim Ross has shared that he is back in the hospital due to low oxygen levels. Taking to his social media pages, Ross provided a brief update on his condition, writing that he is back in the hospital due to low oxygen, weighed 198 pounds, and needs a break.

This latest health setback comes shortly after Ross underwent a successful brain procedure on October 7th. The medical procedure was performed to take care of an ongoing shunt issue.

The 74-year-old broadcasting icon was last seen on commentary for All Elite Wrestling at the Double or Nothing pay-per-view event in May. At that event, he lent his iconic voice to call the Continental Championship match between Jon Moxley and Kyle O'Reilly.

Further updates regarding Jim Ross and his health status will be provided as they become available.

Skye Blue confirmed for MyAEW PPV in the UK

AEW performer Skye Blue has been confirmed for an upcoming Pro Wrestling EVE MyAEW PPV on Sunday, November 8 from The Hanger in Wolverhampton, England. Blue will be challenging for the new Spirit of EVE Championship, which was won by Lucy Deathmatch back at All In weekend. Deathmatch became the inaugural Spirit of EVE Champion by defeating both Emersyn Jayne and Skye Blue in that match, and this will probably be a similar bloody brawl.

Several other AEW names have already been confirmed for the show, including EVE Champion Kris Statlander, Marina Shafir, Nyla Rose and The Brawlin’ Birds. Marina Shafir will be challenging TJPW great and EVE International Champion Miyu Yamashita, whilst Statlander will be putting her EVE Championship on the line against former WWE name Kay Lee Ray/Alba Fyre.

The following matches have been confirmed for Pro Wrestling EVE on MyAEW PPV for Elite Encounters 2. The EVE Championship Match features Kris Statlander defending her title against Kay Lee Ray. The EVE International Championship will be contested between Miyu Yamashita and Marina Shafir. The Spirit of EVE Championship match pits Lucy Deathmatch against Skye Blue. Additionally, the card features HyperActive versus The Brawlin’ Birds, as well as a 10 Woman Riot of Violence match.

Fans can catch the action live in-person or watch live online as the event gets underway with doors opening at 2pm, a 3pm start, and a 6:30pm finish.

🔄 המשך 🔥 PAC's wife Natalie shares tribute to late husband

Following the tragic passing of Benjamin Satterley, his wife Natalie has shared an emotional public statement reflecting on their life together.

In a personal social media post, Natalie detailed their long history, noting they spent 21 years as friends, 19 years as a couple, and 11 years in marriage. She expressed the profound cruelty of having their future stripped away, sharing the everyday moments they were eagerly looking forward to upon his return home. Those plans included setting up a new home gym, walking their dog Wilfie, getting pizza at Rudy's, attending a live show, and shooting hoops with his close friends.

Tributes from across the professional wrestling community have continued to pour in. Speaking on Busted Open Radio, fellow wrestler Enzo Amore highlighted Satterley's deep devotion to his wife, describing them as true soulmates who lived for each other and extending his support to the family.

Natalie concluded her message by acknowledging the immense difficulty of coming to terms with his absence. She stated that while she and her loved ones are trying their hardest to navigate the nightmare of his loss, the reality of him never walking through the door again remains beyond comprehension.

🔄 המשך Matt Hardy praises AEW tribute to PAC

Adding to the discussion surrounding the recent AEW tribute show dedicated to PAC, TNA star Matt Hardy has shared his perspective on the emotional broadcast.

Speaking on The Extreme Life of Matt Hardy podcast, the veteran wrestler explained that while he only caught the first thirty minutes of the show, he was deeply impressed by the tribute efforts. He specifically highlighted the video package celebrating PAC's life and the touching return of Bryan Danielson.

Hardy noted that the broadcast provided essential comfort for both the fans and the locker room as they processed the tragedy. By centering the entire night on PAC's legacy, the company created powerful feel-good moments that helped everyone grieve and find a sense of hope.

During the episode, Hardy also reflected on Jon Moxley's eerie post-All Out promo, where Moxley spoke extensively about death, loss, and perseverance. With PAC appearing as the final shot in that segment, Hardy admitted that the promo takes on a haunting tone in hindsight. H/T Wrestling Inc. for the transcription.

🔄 המשך Max Caster seems to have picked the wrong target for an ‘edgy’ bar in his TNA debut

Fresh off his departure from AEW, Max Caster wasted no time courting controversy in his new promotion. During his debut at Bound for Glory, Caster delivered an unapproved entrance freestyle that took aim at Moose with crude references and touched on Saudi Arabia with a line about not needing to fly to Riyadh. The remarks instantly infuriated officials, resulting in TNA scrubbing the performance from all official online channels.

According to backstage reports, Caster has massive heat with management, and his future with the company is hanging by a thread with upcoming tapings potentially disrupted. Industry observers note that the unauthorized lyric likely alarmed TNA corporate partners, particularly given the active working relationship with WWE, which has an upcoming premium live event scheduled in Riyadh. Whether Caster is already finished with the promotion or merely facing an immediate firing remains to be seen, but his explosive start has instantly put his entire TNA run in jeopardy.

🔥 Nyla Rose speaks out on Oklahoma Athletic Commission controversy and AEW status

During a recent interview, Nyla Rose reflected on her time in AEW, sharing some wild rejected ideas from her feud with Jade Cargill, including requests for a forklift and an airplane that were ultimately turned down due to budget, timing, and legal concerns. Rose and Cargill previously faced off in a singles match at AEW Full Gear 2022 for the TBS Championship.

More than two years after AEW and ROH's visit to Oklahoma in December 2023, Rose remains deeply critical of the Oklahoma State Athletic Commission. Following her match against Alejandra Lion at the December 20 taping in Oklahoma City, commission administrator Jim Miller raised issues regarding rules concerning transgender women competing against cisgender women. Rose has not wrestled in Oklahoma since the controversy and was absent when AEW returned to the state the following year.

Addressing the commission during her interview, Rose did not sugarcoat her feelings, calling them hateful bigots with no basis for their actions, describing the athletic commission as a scam used by greedy people to work people out of money using new laws and loopholes. Despite the difficult situation, Rose praised AEW President Tony Khan for standing behind her without hesitation. Khan had previously spoken out during an April 2024 media call, asserting that transgender people have rights and that the company stood firmly behind Rose. Rose admitted she was surprised and incredibly grateful for Khan's quick response, stating that it proved he knew the right side of history to be on and spoke volumes about his character.

Outside of the controversy, Rose addressed her lengthy absence from AEW television, admitting that it is a little hard and frustrating at times. Although she has not competed in an official AEW match in nearly two years, she remains under contract, contributes behind the scenes, and refers to her current independent run as her side-quest era. She has wrestled for Marigold in Japan, Pro Wrestling EVE, and is scheduled for a steel cage match against Charity King at Athena's Metroplex Wrestling event.

Reflecting on her time away, Rose opened up about experiencing moments of self-doubt, noting that the support from fans and peers helps immensely. She expressed high praise for ROH Women's World Champion Athena, calling her a living legend and expressing deep honor that Athena wanted her involved in upcoming independent events, which helps quell some of her personal doubts while she awaits her next opportunity on AEW programming.

Former TNA Wrestling star Dani Luna could be the latest talent to make the jump to AEW, according to recent reports. During her stint with the company, she became a two-time TNA Knockouts World Tag Team Champion alongside Jody Threat as part of Spitfire, though the duo was eventually forced to split up following a stipulation match loss to Ash and Heather by Elegance.

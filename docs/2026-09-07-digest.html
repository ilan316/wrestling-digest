🔥 Bronson Reed Returns and Ruins Bron Breakker vs. Oba Femi at SNME

At the first-ever WWE Sunday Night's Main Event on September 6, 2026, at State Farm Arena in Atlanta, Georgia, Big Bronson Reed made his long-awaited return to WWE programming. The return occurred during a highly anticipated singles match between Bron Breakker and Oba Femi, which was built to determine who truly deserved to be called the future of the company. Paul Heyman served as the de facto ring announcer after helping set up the match with Adam Pearce following weeks of arguments between Breakker and Femi over who represented WWE's future.

Right from the start, Breakker and Femi went to war, trading brutal shoulder tackles and fighting both inside and outside the ring. Action spilled over when Breakker launched himself over the commentary desk, while Femi later gained control by landing heavy uppercuts, a front kick, and a chokeslam that nearly secured the win. As the contest progressed, outside interferences began to mount. Austin Theory tried to get involved on behalf of Breakker, but Femi quickly stopped him by putting him through the announce desk. Logan Paul then ran down the entrance ramp with brass knuckles and was immediately ejected by the referee, though Maxxine Dupri secretly managed to hand another pair of brass knuckles to Breakker. Femi avoided the attempted shot and threw Breakker across the ring.

Just as the match neared its climax, Bronson Reed made his surprise return, hitting the ring and attacking Femi from behind with a steel chair. The referee called for the bell, awarding Femi the victory via disqualification. Reed had been sidelined for six months after suffering a distal bicep tear during an Elimination Chamber triple threat qualifier match on the February 23 edition of Raw, undergoing surgery shortly after. Industry reports had indicated his return was imminent, with Reed recently visiting WWE Headquarters to film promotional material and work on merchandise concepts.

Reed was not finished after the bell, delivering four Tsunami splashes to Femi while Breakker followed up with multiple Spears. Despite Adam Pearce and other staff members coming from backstage to separate them, the duo left Femi completely laid out in the ring. Later in the show, Cathy Kelley attempted to get comments from Paul Heyman, who said he had plenty to say before receiving a phone call and walking away. Following the event, Reed took to social media to share a photo of the fully reunited group consisting of himself, Bron Breakker, Logan Paul, Austin Theory, and Maxxine Dupri, with the caption reading, The Vision is whole.

While the finish protected both Femi and Breakker from taking a decisive loss, fans online expressed frustration over the interferences, wishing to see a clean conclusion to the powerhouse showdown.

🔥 Mick Foley Discusses Darby Allin's High-Risk Wrestling Style

Mick Foley knows a thing or two about putting his body through hell inside the wrestling ring, and now the WWE Hall of Famer has revealed that he personally warned Darby Allin about the risks he is taking in AEW. During a virtual signing hosted by Highspots, Foley was asked whether watching Allin perform dangerous stunts ever made him want to tell the AEW star to stop. The question came just one day after Allin was thrown off the steel cage by Wardlow during the November 4 edition of AEW Dynamite Blood and Guts. Foley revealed that he had already approached Allin about his wrestling style and suggested that he tone things down.

While Allin appreciated the advice, he made it clear that he wasn't interested in changing how he wrestles. Foley stated that he did not tell Allin to stop completely, but rather suggested he might want to calm down. According to Foley, Allin responded by saying he appreciated the feedback and the conversation, but ultimately he is going to keep doing what he does. Foley also noted that following the brutal match, Allin was walking a little gingerly.

Foley’s concerns carry plenty of weight considering his own legendary wrestling career, which included extreme punishment and infamous falls from the Hell in a Cell structure against The Undertaker at King of the Ring 1998. Allin has developed a similar reputation for putting his body on the line, regularly performing dangerous dives and taking massive bumps that leave viewers questioning how much punishment he can withstand. During the Blood and Guts match, Allin teamed with Will Ospreay, Adam Copeland, Steven Borden, and The Young Bucks against The Don Callis Family team of Kazuchika Okada, Kyle Fletcher, Wardlow, Kevin Knight, Mark Davis, and Jake Doyle. The match featured Allin being thrown from the top of the steel cage through tables positioned below. The Don Callis Family ultimately secured the victory after Fletcher threatened to drive a screwdriver into Ospreay’s neck, forcing a restrained Copeland to surrender to prevent the attack.

In addition to Foley's warnings, former WWE referee Jimmy Korderas publicly addressed the stunt in a video, arguing that Darby Allin has completely desensitized his audience to what he does now. Korderas noted that because Allin commits to so many crazy stunts, he no longer gets the massive shock reactions from crowds who now simply expect him to go through another table. Korderas believes this lack of evolution is hurting Allin and is reflective of a broader issue with original AEW talent failing to change their presentation over the years, which he suggests is hurting live attendance figures outside of pay-per-views. Despite advice from legends and critiques from industry veterans, Allin remains determined to continue pushing his limits inside the ring.

Adam Copeland Identifies First Star-Making Moment as Edge

AEW star Adam Copeland is currently winding down his career, having set his sights on the AEW World Championship to top off his time in the promotion and officially retire in 2027. Before arriving in AEW, Copeland first became a fan-favorite and sometimes the most hated wrestler as Edge during his lengthy WWE tenure.

During an appearance on The Mick And Kenny Show, Copeland looked back at his WWE career and identified the moment he believes he became a star. To answer your question about it feeling like the first star-making moment, it was actually in Cleveland, No Mercy, the ladder match against The Hardys, it was the first tag team ladder match ever, he recalled. When we got to the back, and we walked through the curtain, Mick Foley came up to all four of us and said, You just became stars.

Copeland added that the praise coming from someone like Foley made it all the more meaningful to them, even though they weren't deaf to the audience's roaring reaction. When you start to hear it from your peers, that's when you know you've kind of made your mark, he added. So, for me, that match was the one that really kind of set us on the path.

Another memorable moment in Copeland's WWE career saw him jump off of a ladder to spear Jeff Hardy at WrestleMania X-Seven, in their TLC Match for the World Tag Team Championship. Copeland shared that he didn't feel too nervous ahead of taking the leap. I honestly didn't think about it, to be honest. You just kind of got to go, he said. I don't think Jeff Hardy understands what fear is, so I don't think he was scared.

Claudio Castagnoli Appears at League of Legends World Championship

AEW star Claudio Castagnoli made a special guest appearance at the 2026 League of Legends World Championship Quarterfinals, bringing a crossover of professional wrestling and esports to the stage.

The event took place on November 6 at the Credit Union of Texas Event Center in Allen, Texas, featuring a high-stakes best-of-five series between T1, featuring roster members Doran, Oner, Faker, Peyz, and Keria, and Karmine Corp, featuring Canna, Yike, kyeahoo, Caliste, and Busio.

Before the opening game commenced, Castagnoli stepped up to the microphone as a special guest to hype up the crowd and officially announce both competing teams to the arena. Official tournament social media channels shared clips of his energetic introductions for both organizations.

Following the conclusion of the first game, which was won by T1, the professional wrestler participated in a brief sideline interview to discuss his passion for the game. When asked by the broadcast team how he originally got into League of Legends, Castagnoli explained that he got to play in a show match eight years ago and has been completely hooked ever since.

AEW Dynamite and WWE NXT Ratings Battle (Nov 4, 2026)

AEW Dynamite and WWE NXT went head-to-head in a notable ratings battle on November 4, 2026. Dynamite aired on TBS and featured the high-stakes Blood and Guts cage matches. Meanwhile, NXT on The CW Network countered with star-studded appearances by John Cena, CM Punk, and Cody Rhodes.

When the final numbers were tallied, AEW Dynamite averaged 792,000 viewers alongside a 0.24 rating in the key 18-49 demographic. In comparison, WWE NXT brought in 552,000 viewers with a 0.13 rating in the same demographic. Furthermore, Dynamite posted a strong 0.27 rating in the 25-54 demographic, marking its second-best result in that category.

Industry reporting noted that Dynamite's 18-49 rating was its best on the network in several years, performing better than even the special Pac tribute show.

Following the broadcast, AEW President Tony Khan took to social media to celebrate the success of the night. He shared a message stating, thank you all who watched Wednesday Night AEW Dynamite Blood and Guts! I thought that it was a tremendous show, and had excellent viewership, including AEW's most 18 to 49 viewers on TBS Network in years! See you at Saturday AEW Collision Rock n Rager TOMORROW and Full Gear next week!

AEW Dynamite Viewership Increase (Nov 4, 2026)

AEW Dynamite viewership and key demo numbers are in for the November 4, 2026 episode on TBS, marking one of the strongest increases of the year. According to reporting, AEW Dynamite on November 4, 2026 drew 792,000 viewers on TBS, with a 0.24 rating in the key P18 to 49 demographic. For comparison, the October 28, 2026 episode drew 620,000 viewers with a 0.21 rating in the key P18 to 49 demographic, an increase of 172,000 viewers and up by 0.03 in the demo.

Compared to the same week in 2025, AEW Dynamite drew 497,000 viewers and a 0.09 demo rating on November 5, 2025, versus 792,000 viewers and a 0.24 demo rating on November 4, 2026. That represents a year-over-year change of plus 59.4 percent in viewership and plus 166.7 percent in the demo.

These figures reflect only linear TV viewership on TBS and do not include streaming or digital viewership. Chris Jericho to Star in Horror Action Film

AEW star Chris Jericho is no stranger to horror films, with Terrifier 2 and Terrifier 3 among his acting credits, and this week, it was revealed that the former AEW World Champion will be starring in a new creature action horror film. According to reports, Jericho will appear in I Am Teeth, by Epic Pictures Group, directed by Scott B. Hansen.

The project features Jericho playing a bounty hunter known simply as The Hunter. The story synopsis reveals that the movie is about middle school children, searching for a lost pet, who end up trapping a werewolf who turns out to be more than just a creature. The kids are pursued by Jericho's character, as well as something even more sinister.

The film is set to go before buyers at the American Film Market. Select scenes will be shown, and production of the rest of the film is planned for the beginning of 2027.

I Am Teeth isn't Jericho's only upcoming project. In October, it was revealed he was cast as legendary wrestling star Gorgeous George in The Greatest, an Amazon Prime limited series surrounding the life of boxing legend Muhammed Ali. On November 4, Jericho took to social media to show off a photo of himself dressed as the flamboyant star.

Jericho also stars in an episode of Dexter: Resurrection, as a character known as The Midnight Mangler. He also appeared in an episode of Margo's Got Money Troubles. Within the ring, Jericho was most recently defeated twice by Nick Wayne, including at AEW WrestleDream. The star's Rock 'N' Wrestling Rager At Sea, which will feature an episode of AEW Collision, will set sail this weekend.

WrestleCon Owner Urges Tony Khan to Run Forbidden Door During WrestleMania Weekend

WWE WrestleMania 43 is scheduled to take place in Riyadh, Saudi Arabia, in April 2027, marking the first time the flagship event will be held outside of North America. While WWE has not yet officially announced the exact dates or venue, recent reports suggest the official announcement could come during the Crown Jewel premium live event. The currently expected dates for the historic event are April 10 and 11, 2027.

This unprecedented move by WWE to host its biggest weekend overseas has prompted suggestions for rival promotions to capitalize on the domestic market left behind. During a recent Sign-it-Live session featuring wrestling legend Mick Foley, WrestleCon and Highspots owner Michael Bochicchio proposed that AEW President Tony Khan host an AEW Forbidden Door weekend in the United States while WWE holds WrestleMania 43 in Saudi Arabia.

The discussion unfolded as Foley talked about his schedule for the coming years. Foley mentioned that he is going to cut way back on outside appearances in 2027 and make AEW his priority, noting it might be one convention or two signings a weekend, just one weekend a month. When Bochicchio responded by hoping WrestleCon would fit into that schedule, Foley asked where the convention would be held that year.

Bochicchio stated that he needed to get ahold of Tony Khan to pitch his idea. He explained that since AEW runs the Forbidden Door pay-per-view where they bring in talent from other groups, they should make a global weekend out of that pay-per-view. Foley expressed surprise and asked where it would be held, to which Bochicchio replied that he would let Tony Khan decide the location, emphasizing that fans want something like that.

Foley agreed, noting that it is a long-standing tradition to have a week-long celebration of wrestling during that time of year. Bochicchio firmly stated that they are not going to do the convention in Saudi Arabia. Foley acknowledged the sentiment, noting that while the money in Saudi Arabia might be good, the absence of domestic events leaves a very wide space for someone to step in and fill that void.

Black Tiger Talks Being the 9th to Dawn that Legendary Mask, MLW Saturday Night Superfight, CMLL’s International Appeal, more

Major League Wrestling continues to showcase top talent from around the world through its global partnerships, bringing in international stars like CMLL competitor Black Tiger. As the ninth version of the legendary persona and formerly known as Magnus, Black Tiger is scheduled for action at Saturday Night Superfight live on VEEPS pay-per-view.

During a recent interview, the host welcomed the Lucha Libre star, noting the opportunity to conduct the conversation smoothly. Black Tiger expressed his happiness to be there and his readiness to explain the world of who Black Tiger is.

Discussing the prestigious lineage of the mask in both Mexico and Japan, Black Tiger reflected on what it means to be the ninth in history. He noted that the role carries a massive legacy, with past iterations including his brother Rocko, as well as major stars like Eddie Guerrero, Silver King, and Tomohiro Ishii. He explained that this history forces him to train hard every single day, maintain humility, and keep the level of performance high to honor the trust placed in him.

When asked if fans will ever see the return of his former persona Magnus, who helped him travel the world and open many doors, Black Tiger admitted he currently feels great focusing entirely on Black Tiger. He hopes to carry the mask and character for years to come, noting a deep personal connection to Japanese wrestling. Growing up, the third Black Tiger mask was actually the very first one in his personal collection at age nine, as he always loved Japanese wrestling and grew up watching matches featuring Tiger Mask and other legends.

The host complimented his English language skills, to which Black Tiger humbly replied that he tries his best, mentioning that his daughter is from El Paso, Texas, which helps explain his familiarity with the language.
